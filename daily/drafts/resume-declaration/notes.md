# Notes: declaration-in-resume

## Duplicate check
- resume/article.md (how-to-make-a-resume) mentions the declaration only once, as a common mistake ("biodata habit, not needed on a private-sector resume").
- student-resume/article.md lists "Declaration and signature" in a before-example only.
- Neither covers the topic itself, so this is not a near-duplicate. This article is consistent with both: optional or skip for private-sector resumes.

## Top results checked (search: "declaration in resume India sample")
zety.com (search intent only), unstop.com, naukri.com campus, cake.me, hirist.tech, cvwizard, goodspace.
- What they all say: definition, generic sample lines for freshers and experienced, "place it at the end", ATS tips.
- What they miss (checked on Unstop): no guidance on government forms, no legal meaning beyond "may carry weight", no place/date format, no official source.

## Information gain
1. A table of where it is needed (private, ATS/overseas, campus, offline government biodata, online UPSC/SSC/RRB forms).
2. The actual official wording and consequences from UPSC advertisements and the central government Attestation Form, the Khedkar case (PIB) and the Avtar Singh SC guidelines. Also an exact Indian place/date/signature layout and the line for serving government employees.

## Sources
- UPSC Advt No. 21/2023: https://upsc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Advt-No-21-2023-Eng-10112023.pdf. Contains: "should not furnish any particulars that are false or suppress any material information", "candidature will be liable to be rejected", and penalties for misconduct (disqualification, debarment by Commission permanently or for a period, debarment by Central Government from employment, criminal prosecution, disciplinary action if in government service). Serving employees must inform their Head of Office/Department in writing.
- Attestation Form (RRB Chennai): https://www.rrbchennai.gov.in/downloads/Blank-Attestation-Form.pdf. Contains: "furnishing of false information or suppression of any factual information … would be disqualification, and is likely to render the candidate unfit for employment under the government". The certificate covers termination and "criminal / civil / legal action", with Date/Place/Signature lines.
- PIB, UPSC on Puja Khedkar (31 July 2024): https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2039580. Provisional candidature for CSE-2022 cancelled and permanently debarred, for availing extra attempts by faking identity.
- Avtar Singh vs Union of India (2016, 3-judge bench: Gogoi, Arun Mishra, Pant JJ.): https://www.livelaw.in/suppression-information-employeecandidates-sc-issues-guidelines. Guidelines on disclosure, trivial cases, deliberate concealment, the specific-question rule and departmental inquiry for confirmed employees.
- Competitor gap check: https://unstop.com/blog/declaration-in-resume

## Unverified / judgement calls
- "No law requires a declaration on a resume" and "a resume declaration is not an affidavit" are general statements. They are logical and uncontroversial, but no single official source says so.
- "Most recruiters don't look for it" and "outside South Asia it looks unusual" are practice observations from competitor articles and general convention, not data.
- The UPSC advert used is from 2023 (No. 21/2023). The same warning text is standard in UPSC adverts, but I did not fetch the 2026 CSE notification PDF directly.
- I didn't fetch an SSC notice. The article doesn't quote SSC; it only lists SSC as a portal where the form has its own declaration.
- Avtar Singh: the judgment date (July 2016) comes from the LiveLaw publication date, and the article says only "2016".
