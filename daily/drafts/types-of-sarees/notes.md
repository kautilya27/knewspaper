## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Bewakoof, iTokri, Onmanorama; Aza blog page could not be fetched: permission timed out) list 8-25 sarees with loose descriptions and NO GI status, no registered names, no years. Ours: state-wise table of ~30 sarees with exact registered GI name and registration year from the IP India registry, identifying features from the official GI compendium/application, a list of popular names that are NOT saree GIs (kasavu, bandhani, chikankari, nauvari, Bengal jamdani), lookalike comparisons, and official handloom figures (census, Handloom Mark).

## Unverified / caveats
- "Around 50 sarees and saree silks" is my count of saree/silk-named entries in the registry list (about 55-58 incl. Muga, Salem, Kotpad fabrics); not an official figure.
- State counts in FAQ (TN ~10, WB ~9) come from a name-match count of the registry list via WebFetch summariser; approximate, hence "roughly".
- Years are the registry's financial-year sections, not exact certificate dates.
