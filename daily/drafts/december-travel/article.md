**Quick answer:** For December in India, pick the places where winter is the good season: Goa, Kochi and the Andaman Islands for warm beaches; Jaisalmer, Udaipur and the Rann of Kutch for sunny desert days; Varanasi for the ghats in cool air; and Kohima, Shillong or Gangtok if you want crisp hill weather. Kohima is special in the first ten days, when the Hornbill Festival runs from 1 to 10 December.

What decides a good trip is less the place than three practical things: how cold the nights get, how crowded it is in the Christmas–New Year week, and whether you need a permit or face a closed road. The table below puts those side by side, using India Meteorological Department (IMD) normals for 1991–2020 wherever they are available.

## December at a glance: temperature, crowds, transport and permits

Temperatures are the average daily maximum and minimum for December at the nearest IMD observatory (1991–2020 normals, as published in IMD's *Climatological Tables of Observatories in India*), except for Panaji, Jaisalmer and Port Blair (see the note below the table). Treat them as a typical month, not a forecast. Crowd level is our judgement based on the festival calendar and school holidays, not a measured number.

## Beaches and warm south: Goa, Kochi and the Andamans

December is dry and sunny on the west coast and in the Andamans. Afternoons are still warm, above 30°C in all three, so carry sunscreen rather than a jacket.

### Goa

December is Goa's busiest month. The Feast of St Francis Xavier at Old Goa falls on Thursday, 3 December 2026, and the Serendipity Arts Festival is scheduled for 13 to 20 December 2026 at venues across Panjim. After about 20 December, rooms, cabs and flights get noticeably more expensive. If you have a choice, go in the first half of the month. Our guide to the [best places to visit in Goa](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/best-places-to-travel-in-goa) helps you choose between the north and the south.

### Kochi and the backwaters

Kochi is humid but rain-free for most of December, with nights around 22°C. It is a good base for the Alappuzha backwaters. Munnar and the other Kerala hill stations are much cooler, but they have no IMD normals we could verify, so they are not in the table.

### Andaman Islands

Sea conditions are usually calm enough for snorkelling, and nights stay warm (about 24°C). Book Havelock (Swaraj Dweep) and Neil (Shaheed Dweep) ferries early for the last week of the year.

## Desert and heritage: Jaisalmer, Udaipur, Kutch and Varanasi

This is the best season for western India's deserts, but the gap between day and night is wide. Jaisalmer averages about 26°C in the afternoon and 11°C at night. Udaipur's nights are colder still, at under 9°C.

### Jaisalmer

Days suit the fort and havelis, but the Sam dune camps get cold after dark, so check for proper bedding and heaters. Our comparison of [desert camps in Jaisalmer](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/best-desert-camp-jaisalmer) explains what to look for. New Year's Eve gala nights push up prices.

### Udaipur

Clear skies suit the lakes and palaces. The wedding season overlaps with the holidays, so book hotels early.

### Rann of Kutch

The Rann Utsav tent city at Dhordo runs from 1 November 2026 to 7 March 2027, according to Outlook Traveller. To enter the White Rann you need a permit, which the Kutch district administration issues online. A counter at the Bhirandiyara checkpost has also issued permits in past seasons; check locally. Bhuj is the gateway by air and rail.

### Varanasi

Mornings on the ghats are cool (about 10°C) and often misty. Dense fog across the Indo-Gangetic plains regularly delays trains and early flights from mid-December, so leave a buffer day before onward connections.

## Hills of the Northeast: Kohima, Shillong and Gangtok

These are the coldest options here, with nights of 5–7°C, but skies are usually clear.

### Kohima and the Hornbill Festival

The 27th Hornbill Festival will be held from 1 to 10 December at the Naga Heritage Village in Kisama, about 12 km from Kohima, according to DD News. Read our [Hornbill Festival guide](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/hornbill-festival) for the programme and where to stay.

The permit rules are different for Indians and for foreigners:

- **Indian citizens** from outside Nagaland need an Inner Line Permit. Since 1 January 2025 applications are online only, on the state's [ILP portal](https://ilp.nagaland.gov.in).
- **Foreign tourists:** the Union Home Ministry, in a letter dated 4 November, relaxed the Protected Area Regime in Nagaland from 1 to 10 December every year, The Morung Express reported. Chief Minister Neiphiu Rio has asked for the window to be extended to 15 November–15 December. At the time of writing that was only a request. Outside 1–10 December foreigners still need a Protected Area Permit, and nationals of Afghanistan, China and Pakistan, and people of Pakistani origin, still need prior Home Ministry approval. Check the 2026 conditions with Nagaland Tourism before booking.

Nights average 5.2°C, so pack warm layers.

### Shillong

Shillong needs no ILP and is lively at Christmas. Most travellers reach Guwahati by air or rail and drive up.

### Gangtok

Gangtok itself needs no permit for Indians. Day trips to Tsomgo Lake and Nathu La, and trips to North Sikkim (Lachung, Lachen), need permits. A registered tour operator usually arranges them, and Sikkim has been moving the process online with QR-coded passes. Foreign nationals need a Restricted Area Permit for Sikkim. Snow can close the road beyond Tsomgo at short notice. India Today NE reported exactly that in April 2026, and it happens more often in winter. Plan these excursions as "if open".

## What to skip in December

- **High-altitude Ladakh and Spiti by road:** high passes are snowbound. Around Manali, the local administration usually stops tourist vehicles beyond Gulaba towards Rohtang once black ice forms. The Tribune reported this happening in late November 2023.
- **Char Dham:** Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri and Yamunotri close for winter in October–November every year.


## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the warmest place to visit in India in December?

Of the places in our table, Goa (normal maximum about 33°C) and Kochi (32.9°C) have the warmest days. The Andamans have the warmest nights, at about 24°C.

### Do Indians need a permit for the Hornbill Festival?

Yes. Indian citizens from outside Nagaland need an Inner Line Permit, which you apply for on the state's online ILP portal (applications have been online-only since January 2025). Foreigners benefit from the 1–10 December relaxation of the Protected Area Regime, but need a Protected Area Permit if they arrive before 1 December or stay past 10 December.

### When is the cheapest time in December to travel?

The first two weeks are usually cheaper and less crowded than 20 December to 1 January. That last stretch combines Christmas, New Year and school holidays. Hornbill week (1–10 December) is the exception in Kohima.

### Is it too cold to visit Rajasthan in December?

No. Daytime highs are about 26°C in Jaisalmer and Udaipur, but nights drop to 9–11°C, so carry a warm jacket for evenings and desert camps.
