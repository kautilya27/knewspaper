# Notes: borderline-personality-disorder (plan row #61)

Researched 21 Nov 2026.

## Sources per key fact
- Four symptom groups (emotional instability, disturbed thinking, impulsive behaviour, unstable relationships; "black-white" thinking): NHS symptoms page https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/conditions/borderline-personality-disorder/symptoms/
- Causes = genetic + environmental; most people with BPD report childhood trauma or neglect; recover over time: NHS overview https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/borderline-personality-disorder/
- Treatment: DBT (weekly individual + group; NICE first treatment to try for women with a history of self-harm or suicidal behaviour), MBT (~18 months), therapy can last a year or more, NICE does not recommend medicine for BPD itself, no medicine licensed: NHS treatment page https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/borderline-personality-disorder/treatment/
- Risk factors (genetics, brain differences with unclear causality, trauma/invalidating relationships; risk factors not destiny), diagnosis usually late adolescence/early adulthood, under-18 only if symptoms severe and lasting at least a year, higher self-harm/suicide rate, co-occurring conditions, CBT and family therapy, BPD symptoms occur "in the absence of significantly elevated mood": NIMH https://nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/borderline-personality-disorder
- DSM-5: 5 of 9 criteria, onset by early adulthood, across contexts: headspace clinical toolkit (Australia) https://headspace.org.au/assets/Uploads/Resource-library/Health-professionals/Clinical-Toolkit/CT-Criteria-for-Diag-BPD.pdf
- ICD-11 borderline pattern 6D11.5 (manifestations list); severity codes 6D10.0/.1/.2 mild/moderate/severe: https://www.cismef.org/page/detail/en/ICD11_SC_2006821354 and https://www.findacode.com/icd-11/code-2006821354.html
- EUPD alternative name; some find the label stigmatising: Mind https://www.mind.org.uk/information-support/types-of-mental-health-problems/borderline-personality-disorder-bpd/about-bpd/
- BPD vs bipolar table (hours vs days/weeks; event-triggered vs less linked; impulsivity brief vs persistent; therapy vs medication emphasis; can co-occur): Cleveland Clinic https://health.clevelandclinic.org/bpd-vs-bipolar and NewYork-Presbyterian https://www.nyp.org/healthmatters/understanding-difference-bipolar-borderline-personality-disorder
- Tele-MANAS 14416, 24x7, 20 languages, MoHFW programme: PIB backgrounder (Oct 2024) https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2024/oct/doc20241013415701.pdf ; JIPMER https://jipmer.edu.in/Tele-MANAS ; 1800-891-4416 confirmed on IIT Kanpur counselling page https://iitk.ac.in/counsel/TeleManas.php
- Clinical psychologists in RCI's Central Rehabilitation Register: https://rehabcouncil.nic.in/categories-u-s-19/
- Mental Healthcare Act 2017 s.18 right to access + free for homeless/destitute/BPL: Nyaaya explainer https://nyaaya.org/legal-explainer/right-to-access-mental-healthcare/

## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Mayo Clinic, NHS, NI Direct, NIMH, Cleveland Clinic) are US/UK-focused: none covers getting assessed in India (psychiatrist vs RCI-registered clinical psychologist, Mental Healthcare Act s.18 free care, Tele-MANAS), none sets DSM-5 and ICD-11 side by side, and Mayo/NHS have no BPD-vs-bipolar comparison table.
