# PTSD draft notes (plan #26)

## Top results for "PTSD symptoms causes" (WebSearch, 2026-11-09)
Mayo Clinic, Talkspace, NHS overview, HSE Ireland, Medanta (India), NAMI, BetterHelp.
All say: definition, the symptom clusters (intrusion, avoidance, negative mood/thinking, arousal), risk factors, "see a doctor", therapy names.
They miss (for Indian readers): a clear timeline separating a normal acute stress reaction / acute stress disorder / PTSD / delayed onset (the question families actually ask); ICD-11 vs DSM-5-TR and complex PTSD (Mayo/Talkspace US-centric, DSM only); Indian trauma contexts (road accidents, floods, dowry/domestic violence); Indian help routes (Tele-MANAS 14416, medical college/district psychiatry under DMHP, RCI-registered psychologists, Mental Healthcare Act 2017 right to care). Medanta covers India but only generically, no helpline/timeline.

## Sources
- WHO fact sheet, PTSD: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/post-traumatic-stress-disorder (3.9% lifetime, ~70% exposed to potentially traumatic event; recommends trauma-focused CBT and EMDR)
- NHS PTSD overview: https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/conditions/post-traumatic-stress-disorder-ptsd/overview/ (physical symptoms; delayed onset; watchful waiting ~4 weeks; TF-CBT/EMDR)
- ICD-11 (WHO, in force 1 Jan 2022): PTSD 6B40, CPTSD 6B41; acute stress reaction QE84 (not a mental disorder)
- DSM-5-TR: ASD 3 days–1 month; PTSD >1 month; no CPTSD
- Tele-MANAS: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in/ ; numbers 14416 / 1-800-891-4416 (confirmed by multiple search results: PIB gist, ThePrint, IIT Kanpur counselling page)
- Mental Healthcare Act 2017, Section 18 (right to access mental health care)
