**Quick answer:** In India, shoes are sold in UK sizes, so "Indian size 8" almost always means UK 8. A men's UK 8 fits a foot of roughly 25.9 to 26.2 cm, a US men's 8.5 to 9 and an EU 42 to 42.5, depending on the brand. Women's charts disagree more: the same UK number can mean a different US size and foot length at Nike, Adidas or an Indian brand.

Bha, the India-specific sizing system proposed by CSIR-CLRI in 2024, is still not in use. As of 28 November 2026, we could find no Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) standard adopting it, and no brand selling Bha-sized shoes.

The tables below use three published brand charts, not averages of blog charts. All conversions are approximate. Fit also depends on the shoe's last, width and material, so check the chart of the brand you are buying.

## Where these numbers come from

Most size-chart pages online repeat one table with no source. We used three charts you can check yourself:

- **Adidas** (its UK size guide): foot length in centimetres, heel to longest toe.
- **Nivia** (an Indian sports brand): one combined "Ind/UK" column, with foot length in cm.
- **Nike** (its UK and Ireland size guides): foot length in inches. We converted these to cm (1 inch = 2.54 cm) and rounded to one decimal place.

They don't agree. From UK 7 up, Adidas expects a foot about 3 to 4 mm shorter than Nike or Nivia does (at UK 6 it is 3 mm shorter than Nivia, and Nike lists two UK 6 lengths; at UK 4 and 5 they are within 1 mm). Nivia's men's figures match Nike's almost exactly. That is why measuring your foot in cm and checking the brand's chart beats "I'm always an 8".

## How to measure your foot at home

You need a sheet of paper, a pencil and a ruler or measuring tape. Nike's and Walkaroo's guides give a similar method (Nike says to mark the big toe; Walkaroo says the longest toe, which for some people is not the big toe):

1. Tape the paper to a hard, flat floor (not a carpet) so it can't slide.
2. Stand on it with your weight balanced evenly, one foot on the paper.
3. Have someone mark the back of your heel and the tip of your longest toe, holding the pencil straight up. For some people the longest toe is not the big toe.
4. Measure the distance between the two marks in centimetres.
5. Repeat with the other foot and use the **longer** measurement.
6. Measure in the evening, when feet are slightly larger.

If you fall between two sizes, Nike recommends going up a size. Walkaroo also suggests measuring the widest part of your foot. That matters in India, because the CSIR-CLRI survey found that Indian feet tend to be wider than European or American feet. A shoe can be the right length and still pinch.

The cm number printed on some shoeboxes (the "CM" or "JP" size) is not always your foot length. Nike's guide says so on its chart, so compare your measurement with the chart, not the box.

## What is the Bha shoe sizing system?

**Bha** (from Bharat) is a footwear sizing system designed for Indian feet. CSIR's Central Leather Research Institute (CSIR-CLRI) in Chennai built it from a national survey. According to [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/what-is-bha-the-new-shoe-sizing-system-proposed-specifically-for-indians-124042501098_1.html), the survey 3D-scanned the feet of 101,880 people at 79 locations between December 2021 and March 2022.

The proposal has eight sizes, numbered I to VIII, with no half sizes:

| Bha size | Group |
|---|---|
| I | Infants, 0–1 year |
| II | Babies, 1–3 years |
| III | Small children, 4–6 years |
| IV | Children, 7–11 years |
| V | Girls, 12–13 years |
| VI | Boys, 12–14 years |
| VII | Women, 14 years and above |
| VIII | Men, 15 years and above |

According to the same report, CSIR-CLRI recommended starting with sizes III to VIII, which it said would cover about 85% of the population. The reports did not publish foot-length ranges for each Bha size. Be wary of any website that "converts" your UK size to a Bha number.

## Is Bha in use yet?

Not as of 28 November 2026. The recommendations went to DPIIT, which sent them to BIS for approval. Reports in April 2024 hoped for rollout in 2025. That hasn't happened. Here is what the BIS standards portal shows today:

- **IS 1638:1969** ("Sizes and fitting of footwear") is still listed. It was reaffirmed in November 2021, with no replacement listed.
- The agenda of BIS's footwear committee (CHD 19) for its 3 September 2024 meeting says CLRI had submitted a report on revising India's footwear sizing system. It lists "the way forward" to turn the survey data into a revised standard as an item to discuss.
- In 2026, BIS published revised standards for **Mondopoint** sizing, which measures foot length and width in millimetres (IS 8751 Parts 1 and 2), and a revised standard for **marking shoe sizes** (IS 13800:2026). Their BIS listings don't mention Bha.

We found no official notice that BIS has adopted Bha, and no Indian brand selling shoes in Bha sizes. For now, UK sizes plus your foot length in cm are what you shop with.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Indian shoe size the same as UK size?

In practice, yes. Indian brands label shoes in UK sizes, and Nivia's chart, for example, uses a single "Ind/UK" column. Bha was proposed as a separate Indian system, but it has not been adopted.

### What is UK size 8 in cm?

A UK 8 fits a foot of about 25.9 cm on Adidas's chart and about 26.2 cm on Nivia's and Nike's charts. Measure your foot and check the brand's own table.

### How do I convert a US shoe size to Indian size?

For men, the Indian (UK) size is usually the US size minus 0.5 to 1. For women, it's the US size minus 1.5 (Adidas) to 2.5 (Nike). The gap depends on the brand, so compare foot lengths in cm instead.

### Has the Bha shoe size system been launched?

No. As of November 2026, BIS has not notified it as a standard, and shoes are not sold in Bha sizes. The proposal is with BIS, whose footwear committee has discussed revising India's sizing standard using CSIR-CLRI's survey data.
