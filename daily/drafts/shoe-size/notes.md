## Information gain vs the top results
The top results (Regal, Lusso, Nivia, Walkaroo, Impakto, Medium, PaisaWapas, calculator sites) each give one unsourced chart. Most still describe Bha as "set to replace UK/US sizes" in 2025. This article:
- puts three named, checkable brand charts side by side with foot length in cm;
- shows exactly where they disagree (Adidas runs 3–5 mm shorter per UK size; US women's is UK+1.5 at Adidas but UK+2.5 at Nike; Nike uses gendered foot lengths);
- adds a worked example and an Adidas kids' table with cm;
- gives the current Bha status from the BIS portal and the CHD 19 committee agenda, including the 2026 Mondopoint and marking revisions that don't mention Bha.
