# Notes: varicose-veins (plan row #70)

## Sources per key fact
- Symptoms, risk factors, self-care, treatment order on NHS, stockings not for arterial problems, pregnancy (veins often improve after birth; procedures not usually recommended), leg sore not healed in 2 weeks: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/varicose-veins/ and https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/varicose-veins/causes/
- Endothermal ablation under local anaesthetic: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/varicose-veins/treatment/
- Bleeding varicose vein first aid (lie down, raise leg, direct pressure; help if it doesn't stop) and "usually not serious": https://nhsinform.scot/varicose-veins
- DVT symptoms; breathlessness/chest pain = 999/A&E, don't drive; redness harder to see on brown skin: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/deep-vein-thrombosis-dvt/
- NICE CG168 referral criteria; ablation > UGFS > surgery; "Do not offer compression hosiery ... unless interventional treatment is unsuitable": https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/CG168/chapter/key-priorities-for-implementation
- Belagavi police study (150 personnel, 14.7% VV, 20% at risk, mean 6.69 h standing, risk linked to standing duration), Clinical Epidemiology and Global Health, Oct 2021: https://doaj.org/article/0db88a5df0e34a759c851e64a435a3b4
- Security guards study (Chaya, Sharma, Vinayaka; JEOH 24(4) 2024; n=100; ~15%): https://informaticsjournals.co.in/index.php/JEOH/article/view/44135
- Indian consensus (Dekiwadia et al., Phlebological Review 2016;24(1):7–19): symptoms list, at-risk occupations, Prajapati 2015 Ahmedabad traffic police 2.91%: https://www.termedia.pl/A-consensus-statement-part-I-Recommendations-for-the-management-of-chronic-venous-disease-CVD-in-India-and-the-key-role-of-primary-care-doctors,76,28082,1,1.html
- PM-JAY HBP 2.0 package "Management of Varicose Veins" (SG095A, General Surgery): https://nha.gov.in/img/resources/HBP-2.0-Package-master.pdf (rate shown there deliberately not quoted)
- Spider/thread veins description: https://www.spirehealthcare.com/health-hub/specialties/men-s-health/varicose-veins-and-spider-veins-causes-and-prevention/
- 112 = India's national emergency number (ERSS) – general knowledge, not fetched today.

## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Mayo Clinic, NHS, NHS inform, HSE, Cleveland-type pages) cover symptoms, valve failure, risk factors, complications and prevention. They miss: Indian occupational data (named police/security-guard studies), the Indian expert consensus's at-risk job list, a single action table separating routine / soon / urgent / emergency (with bleeding first aid and DVT/PE signs), NICE's explicit ordering of treatments and its position that stockings are not first-line, and whether PM-JAY covers treatment.
