**Quick answer:** CAT 2026 is on **Sunday, 29 November 2026**, conducted by IIM Indore as a computer-based test in three two-hour slots: **8:30–10:30 am, 12:30–2:30 pm and 4:30–6:30 pm**. Reporting starts 90 minutes before your slot (7:00 am, 11:00 am and 3:00 pm, according to coaching sites Career Launcher and Cracku), and coaching sites put gate closing 15 minutes before the start; go by the times printed on your admit card. Carry a **printed admit card** and **one original photo ID**. Leave your phone, watch, calculator and bag behind.

The paper has three sections in a fixed order: VARC, then DILR, then Quant. Each section gets 40 minutes, and you cannot move between them. A correct answer earns +3, a wrong MCQ costs −1, and a wrong type-in-the-answer (TITA) question costs nothing. Your admit card has the final word on your slot, reporting time and centre.

## CAT 2026 slot timings, reporting time and gate closing

The reporting and gate-closing times below are from coaching sites (Career Launcher and Cracku) and are not confirmed by IIM Indore, so go by the times printed on your admit card. Each slot runs like this:

| Slot | Reporting starts | Gate closes (unofficial) | Exam | Exam ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slot 1 (morning) | 7:00 am | 8:15 am | 8:30 am | 10:30 am |
| Slot 2 (afternoon) | 11:00 am | 12:15 pm | 12:30 pm | 2:30 pm |
| Slot 3 (evening) | 3:00 pm | 4:15 pm | 4:30 pm | 6:30 pm |

IIM Indore gives each candidate one slot, and it is printed on the admit card. You can't change it. If you arrive after the gate closes, you won't be let in.

The test is held in about 170 cities, according to The Hans India and Career Launcher. If your centre is in an unfamiliar part of town, plan for Sunday-morning traffic or a late metro and aim to be there when reporting opens, not just before the gate closes.

## Exam-day timeline for a slot 1 candidate

Add four hours for slot 2 and eight hours for slot 3.

1. **Night before:** Put the admit card printout, your original ID and a transparent water bottle in one envelope. Check the centre address on the card against a map.
2. **6:15–6:30 am:** Leave home. Wear simple clothes without metal buttons or heavy jewellery, since frisking is strict.
3. **7:00 am:** Reporting opens. Staff check your ID against the admit card and may take your photograph. IIM Indore is also trying out AI-based face recognition at exam centres, a first for CAT according to Careers360. It is an extra check on top of manual verification, and nobody will be refused entry if the system fails to recognise them. Mobile jammers are in use at select centres, reports Careers360.
4. **Around 8:15 am (check your admit card):** The gate closes. After this, nobody gets in.
5. **8:15–8:30 am:** You're seated, then log in and read the instructions.
6. **8:30–9:10 am:** VARC.
7. **9:10–9:50 am:** DILR.
8. **9:50–10:30 am:** Quant.
9. **10:30 am:** The test ends. Hand back the scribble pad as instructed and leave only when invigilators allow it.

**Photo ID:** Career Launcher and Physics Wallah list Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, voter ID and driving licence, and Physics Wallah adds college and employer ID cards. These are coaching sites, so treat the list on your admit card as final, and carry a government-issued ID if you can. Make sure the name on your ID matches the admit card.

**Only a printout will do.** CareerIndia and Careers360 both report that a PDF on your phone won't be accepted at the gate. Print on A4 paper in good quality so the photo and signature are easy to read.

Phones and bags may not be stored safely at the centre. Travel with as little as possible, or leave your things with someone outside.

## Inside the exam hall: calculator, scribble pad and timer

- **On-screen calculator:** The test window has a basic calculator, and it is the only one you may use. If you haven't used it yet, practise on the official mock at [iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in) (live since 6 November). A mock platform that copies the CAT interface helps too; this [comparison of mock test platforms in India](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/best-mock-test-platform-india) covers your options.
- **Scribble pad and pen:** The centre gives you these for rough work. Don't bring your own paper or pen. Return the pad at the end as instructed.
- **Sectional timer:** A clock on screen counts down each section. When 40 minutes are up, the section locks and the next one opens. Unused time doesn't carry over, and you can't go back.

## CAT 2026 pattern and marking scheme

The official CAT 2026 mock has 68 questions (24 VARC, 22 DILR, 22 QA) in 120 minutes, according to Cracku and Physics Wallah, which are coaching sites. IIM Indore hasn't published section-wise question counts, so the real paper may differ. PwD timings are as given by Physics Wallah; check the compensatory time on your admit card.

| Section (in this order) | Questions | Time (general) | Time (PwBD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (VARC) | 24 | 40 min | 53 min 20 sec |
| Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning (DILR) | 22 | 40 min | 53 min 20 sec |
| Quantitative Ability (QA) | 22 | 40 min | 53 min 20 sec |
| **Total** | **68** | **120 min** | **160 min** |

The number of questions and the mix of MCQ and TITA questions can change from year to year, so the real paper may differ slightly from the mock. The three sections and their timing have stayed the same in recent years.

| Question type | Correct | Wrong | Not attempted |
|---|---|---|---|
| MCQ | +3 | −1 | 0 |
| TITA (type in the answer) | +3 | 0 | 0 |

With 68 questions the maximum is 204 marks. Because wrong TITA answers cost nothing, it makes sense to enter an answer for every TITA question you've worked on. A wrong MCQ costs a mark, so guess on those only if you can rule out some options.

Scores from the three slots are normalised before percentiles are worked out, to even out any difference in difficulty. A tough slot is not a reason to panic.

## Admit card: was it postponed?

It was moved, but by only two days. IIM Indore had planned to release the admit card on 4 November and changed the date to **6 November**, according to Careers360 and CareerIndia. No reason was given. The card went live on iimcat.ac.in on 6 November. You can download it until exam day, 29 November.

## Photo, name or signature mismatch on the admit card

You can't edit the admit card yourself, and the application correction window closed in October. If something is wrong:

- Write to the CAT helpdesk using the contact details on iimcat.ac.in, not a number from a coaching website. Include your user ID and a scan of your photo ID.
- Don't correct the card by hand or paste a different photo on it, unless the official instructions tell you to.
- If IIM Indore sends a corrected admit card, download it again and print the new version.
- On exam day, reach the centre as soon as reporting opens, with your original ID, so any check doesn't eat into your time.

## After the exam: answer key and result

IIM Indore usually releases candidates' response sheets first. A provisional answer key follows and a short online window to raise objections. Then come the final key and the scorecard on iimcat.ac.in. IIM Indore hasn't announced dates for the answer key. The CAT 2026 information bulletin says results are likely to be declared by the first week of January 2027; coaching-site estimates of late December are guesses. Watch iimcat.ac.in for the official dates.

Your CAT score is used by 22 IIMs and many non-IIM B-schools, according to Physics Wallah. If you're still working out where it can take you, see [why the CAT exam matters](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/why-cat-exam-is-important).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the reporting time for CAT 2026?

Reporting starts at 7:00 am for slot 1, 11:00 am for slot 2 and 3:00 pm for slot 3. Gate closing is usually given as 8:15 am, 12:15 pm and 4:15 pm; your admit card has the exact times.

### Can I show the CAT admit card on my phone?

No. You need a printed admit card, preferably on A4 paper, along with an original photo ID. A phone PDF won't get you in, and phones aren't allowed inside anyway.

### Is there negative marking for TITA questions in CAT?

No. A wrong TITA answer scores zero, the same as leaving it blank. Only a wrong MCQ costs a mark, and a correct answer of either type earns +3.

### Can I carry my own calculator or watch to CAT?

No. You get a basic on-screen calculator and a countdown timer for each section. Physical calculators and smartwatches are banned, and Careers360 reports that wristwatches are not allowed either; check the prohibited list on your admit card.
