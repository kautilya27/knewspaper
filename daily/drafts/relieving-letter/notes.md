## Information gain vs top results
Top results (taggd, thepeoplesboard, enkash, instahyre, tpointtech; keka could not be fetched, permission timed out) give definitions, a difference table, templates and generic "remind HR, then labour commissioner" advice. Most cite no law or case. One (thepeoplesboard) mentions the MSO 10-day rule but none spell out who is excluded. None list EPFO service history as a substitute, none explain the "worker" exclusion (managerial / supervisors over ₹18,000), and none cover the May 2026 Bombay HC Bharat Aviation ruling that the letter is not an unconditional right.
