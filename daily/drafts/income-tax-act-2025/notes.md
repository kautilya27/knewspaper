## Information gain vs top results
Top results (ClearTax, Business Today, Upstox, TaxGuru, Tax2win, TaxBuddy) either dump a 150-row mapping with no explanation (ClearTax) or focus on one section (Business Today on 80C→123). None gives HRA's new location (Schedule III Sl. 11) or the standard deduction/gratuity/leave encashment serial numbers in section 19, the 24(b) sub-clause 22(1)(b), or salary TDS 392 / Form 130 / Form 124 together; none separates real changes (tax year, merges, form numbers, Rules 2026 allowance changes) from pure renumbering, or explains which Act applies to the FY 2025-26 return. Every row here is checked against the Act text on incometaxindia.gov.in or the CBDT FAQs, with the few exceptions flagged.
