# Notes: classical-dances-of-india (researched 28 Nov 2026)

## Sources for key facts
- SNA's eight forms: commonly cited list (GKToday, Drishti IAS, Vajiram); Wikipedia/Wikiwand "Indian classical dance" (SNA = 8, Ministry of Culture = 9 with Chhau). sangeetnatak.gov.in/sections/dance does NOT publish a "classical" list; it lists Chhau and Sattriya under National Projects of Support. https://sangeetnatak.gov.in/sections/dance
- Sattriya recognised 15 Nov 2000 (SNA secretary Jayant Kastuar, Rabindra Bhavan, Guwahati): Narthaki https://narthaki.com/info/snippets/snipjan1.html ; Guwahati conference, Bhupen Hazarika's role: Deccan Herald https://www.deccanherald.com/features/then-came-eighth-2185396 ; year 2000 also Drishti IAS https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/sattriya-dance/print_manually
- Ministry of Culture dance page lists nine incl. Chhau; describes Chhau as "a blend of martial traditions, temple rituals, and folk and popular performance"; features for each form (music, languages for Bharatanatyam/Odissi/Kathakali, Kathakali face colours, Manipuri inaudible footwork, no eye contact, Mohiniyattam edakka, Sattriya Gayan Bayan, Chhau instruments and no vocal music, Tanjore Quartet, 17th c. Kathakali, Odissi mid-20th c. remodelling, Sattriya "since the 16th century"): https://www.indiaculture.gov.in/dance
- SYA scheme guideline (CCRT, June 2026 PDF), heading "Indian Classical Dance / Dance Music" includes Thang-ta, Gaudiya Nritya, Chhau, Sattriya: https://ccrtindia.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Guideline-for-SYA-Scheme-English.pdf
- Ministry empanelled artists list as on 01.03.2024, "Classical & Traditional Dance form" incl. Yakshagana, Chhau: https://indiaculture.gov.in/sites/default/files/pdf/Art_Form_01032024.pdf
- Costumes/stances (pleated sari, aramandi, Kathak sama sthana, Kuchipudi brass plate and gochi kattu, Odissi pattasari/silver/tahiya/chauka/tribhanga, Manipuri potloi and men in white dhoti, Mohiniyattam kasavu, Sattriya pat silk, purusha/prakriti, mati-akhora 64 exercises): NCERT https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/hekr111.pdf
- Kathak bells, Odissi 50+ mudras, Bharatanatyam bent legs: Britannica https://www.britannica.com/list/6-classical-dances-of-india
- Chhau UNESCO 2010, three styles, masks in Seraikella and Purulia: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/chhau-dance-00337
- Chhau classical claim/debate: Asian Age 2018 (PressReader) https://www.pressreader.com/india/the-asian-age/20180307/283652530374563
- Gaudiya Nritya: recognised by Rabindra Bharati University; reservations by Banerjee/Venkataraman (Wikipedia) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaudiya_Nritya
- Mohiniyattam Malayalam/Sanskrit/Manipravalam, Sopana and Carnatic: INFLIBNET e-PG Pathshala https://epgp.inflibnet.ac.in/epgpdata/uploads/epgp_content/S000451PA/P001552/M018106/ET/1481009056P5M14TEXT.pdf

## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Britannica 6-list, GKToday, Drishti, CCRT, Vajiram, PW) give a list of 8 with origins; none explains where the 8 vs 9 comes from, that SNA's own site publishes no "classical list", that ministry scheme lists include Chhau/Gaudiya Nritya/Thang-ta/Yakshagana for funding, or the exact Sattriya recognition date. None has a see-and-hear identification table (costume, stance, hands, giveaway sign) or language column. We add both, plus a "debated origins" section.
