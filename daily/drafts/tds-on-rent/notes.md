## Arithmetic (checked)
- 10 × 55,000 = 5,50,000; 2% = 11,000; 55,000 − 11,000 = 44,000
- April: 2% of 55,000 = 1,100; 55,000 − 1,100 = 53,900
- 11,000 + 1,100 = 12,100 = 2% of 6,05,000 (11 × 55,000)
- No PAN: 20% of 5,50,000 = 1,10,000 > 55,000 cap → 55,000
- Interest 1.5% of 11,000 = 165 per month/part month
- Late fee: 30 days × 200 = 6,000; 60 × 200 = 12,000 > 11,000 cap → 11,000
- Dates: 30 days from 31 March 2027 = 30 April 2027; +15 days = 15 May 2027
