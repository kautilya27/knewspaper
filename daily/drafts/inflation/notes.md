## Sources for key facts

- **CPI 2024 series: basket, weights, markets, COICOP 2018, 358 items (308 goods + 50 services), HCES 2023-24, CAPI tablets, 12 online markets, rural rent, free social transfers excluded (IMF manual), food share 45.86% -> 40.10% on the old classification, base revisions every 3-5 years**: MoSPI FAQ on CPI 2024 (Annexure V) https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/documents/documents/1770891066052-Annexure_V.pdf, and MoSPI press release for January 2026 CPI, dated 12 Feb 2026 (first release of the new series, with division weights) https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1770891893893_6b458c0a-c327-4fef-a554-41131ea67273_Press_Relase_of_CPI_for_Jan26.pdf
  - Division weights (combined): food & bev 36.753, housing etc. 17.665, transport 8.796, clothing 6.383, health 6.100, personal care/misc 5.038, furnishings 4.469, info & comm 3.609, restaurants 3.348, education 3.333, paan/tobacco 2.989, recreation 1.516. Rural food 41.983, urban 30.251.
  - Urban markets: the FAQ says 1,395 urban + 12 online; the press release says 1,407 "including online". These agree (1,395 + 12). I used 1,407.
- **October 2026 CPI**: MoSPI press release dated 12 Nov 2026 https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestreleasesfiles/1794480048335-Press%20Release%20of%20CPI%20for%20October%202026%20.pdf. Combined 6.09% (Sept final 5.54%); rural 6.63, urban 5.41; CFPI 8.94 (rural 9.09, urban 8.68); index 110.05 (Oct 26), 103.74 (Oct 25), 109.49 (Sep 26); division rates; items onion 116.22, garlic 60.74, silver jewellery 52.39, potato -14.24, cauliflower -10.57; large states TN 7.29, Telangana 7.26, AP 7.25; November release 14 Dec 2026. Cross-checked against DD News https://ddnews.gov.in/en/retail-inflation-rises-to-6-09-percent-in-october-food-inflation-at-8-94-percent-goverment-data/ (Delhi 3.80%). Month-on-month 0.51% is my calculation from the indices; ThePrint reports the same.
  - Worked example: (110.05 - 103.74)/103.74 = 6.08%; the official 6.09% comes from unrounded indices (this explanation is mine and standard, not stated by MoSPI).
- **WPI 2022-23 series**: PIB press release, 15 June 2026 https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2272872&reg=3&lang=1. 957 items (from 697), weights primary 22.7573, fuel & power 14.11433, manufactured 63.12837; basic prices excluding net taxes and trade/transport margins; output PPI, trial input PPI, 7 service PPIs; WPI to run alongside PPIs for five years, then be discontinued.
- **October 2026 WPI**: DD News, 16 Nov 2026 https://ddnews.gov.in/en/wholesale-price-inflation-rises-to-12-29-in-october/ : 12.29% (Sept 11.39%), fuel & power 32.84%, primary 9.24%, manufactured 9.54%, food index 8.58%. Moneylife and Swarajya say it is the highest in the new series.
- **Inflation target 2026-31**: notified 25 March 2026 under Section 45ZA, 4% with a 2-6% band, 1 Apr 2026 to 31 Mar 2031. Business Today https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/govt-retains-inflation-target-at-4-with-2-6-band-for-next-five-years-522390-2026-03-25 and Business Standard (26 Mar 2026). Failure = three consecutive quarters outside the band, and six-member MPC: Drishti summary of the framework (consistent with the RBI Act).
- **Headline vs core**: RBI discussion paper (Aug 2025) favoured headline; food and fuel are more than 50% of the basket; spillovers. PRS summary https://prsindia.org/policy/report-summaries/review-of-monetary-policy-framework-by-rbi. The Economic Survey 2023-24 suggestion to examine a target excluding food: ThePrint, Business Standard, Business Today (22 Jul 2024). The statement that the RBI decides which items go into core comes from the MoSPI Secretary, as reported by Business Today on 12 Feb 2026 (not named in the article).
- **Food-weight debate**: the concern that the measured/felt gap may widen for food-heavy households is from Policy Circle's analysis https://www.policycircle.org/economy/new-cpi-series-base-year-2024/. MoSPI's rationale (weights follow HCES 2023-24; free items excluded by definition) is from the MoSPI FAQ and Outlook Business.

## Information gain vs top results

The top results for "how is inflation measured in india" are Wikipedia, GKToday, BYJU'S Q&A pages, old RBI/RBA papers (2014) and MoSPI yearbook chapters. All describe the **old** CPI (base 2012) and WPI (base 2011-12), with no current figures. None has the 2024 CPI basket and weights, the 2022-23 WPI and the new PPIs, the target period renewed for 2026-31, a worked reading of the latest (October 2026) release, or a "your own inflation" calculation from division-level rates.
