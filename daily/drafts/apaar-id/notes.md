# Notes: apaar-id

## Information gain vs top results
Top results for "apaar id" (apaar.education.gov.in, Bajaj Finserv, BankBazaar, ClearTax, Smartprix, Angel One, Drishti/PW IAS, Careers360 CBSE guide) all cover: 12-digit ID, One Nation One Student ID, NEP 2020, benefits, generic school/DigiLocker steps.
What they miss, and this article adds:
- The Supreme Court order of 20 July 2026 (Abhishek Baxi v. Union of India): opt-out must be on the consent form pan-India, DPDP Act applies, no third-party sharing. Most explainers still say only "voluntary" or "mandatory for CBSE".
- A status table (Centre / parents / CBSE 2026 boards / CBSE 2027 boards now optional / NTA / college) reconciling "voluntary" with CBSE's mandate, including the REFUSED/NOGEN mechanism.
- ABC ID = APAAR ID (renamed, per PIB) and what that means for multiple entry/exit credits.
- A mismatch-fixing table (which record to correct where) plus Lok Sabha data on Maharashtra validation failures.
- Current PIB figures (26.35 crore verified IDs as of 2 July 2026).
