# Notes: thank-you-email-after-interview (plan #59, Career & Jobs)

## Sources for key facts (checked 20 Nov 2026)
- Send within 24 hours, ideally the same day; from the same email you applied with; separate note per interviewer; keep short: SHRM India, "Boost Your Interview Chances with a Thank-You Note" (Roy Maurer, 14 Oct 2024) https://www.shrm.org/in/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/boost-your-interview-chances-with-a-thank-you-note
- 24-hour window, separate email per interviewer, email over text: Robert Half https://www.roberthalf.com/us/en/insights/landing-job/how-to-write-thank-you-emails-after-interviews
- Follow up after about a week if no reply: Naukri campus guide https://www.naukri.com/campus/career-guidance/post-interview-thank-you-email-formats
- NIT Sikkim placement rules, clause 3(a) (students "shall not directly contact any representative, recruiter, HR personnel, or employee of a recruiting organization" during an ongoing process; violations lead to disciplinary action) and clause 2(b) (reconsideration only through the T&P Cell): https://nitsikkim.ac.in/placement/rules.php
- IIT Madras Recruiters' Guide & Policies, Placements 2025-26: "All offers (made by the companies) shall be only through the Placement Office"; no direct contact by companies without consent: https://placement.iitm.ac.in/downloads/Recruiters_Guide_&_Policies_Document_Placements_2025-26.pdf (linked in article)
- "Revert" = reply, labelled "especially Indian English": Oxford Learner's Dictionaries https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/revert . Supporting: Business Standard, "Write what we say" (Rrishi Raote, 2012) on revert's Indian meaning.

## Information gain vs top results
Top results (The Muse, Robert Half, NerdWallet, Grammarly, Built In, Zety, IONOS, SHRM, Naukri) all say: send within 24 h, be brief, personalise, separate notes to panel members, generic templates (standard, panel, virtual, rejection, follow-up). None (including Naukri) covers who to thank in Indian hiring routes (HR-only contact, consultancy-led hiring, campus drives) or that campus placement rules can forbid contacting recruiters; none has an HR-round-with-CTC/notice-period template; none covers Indian-English email phrases (do the needful, revert back, PFA, "I am having", "Myself X") with replacements.
Our article adds: a who-to-thank table by hiring route, verified campus rules (NIT Sikkim, IIT Madras), five India-specific templates plus a follow-up, and a phrase-replacement table with the Oxford dictionary note on "revert".
