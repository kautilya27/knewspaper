# Notes: neet-eligibility (niche-topics.csv row 17)

## Sources for key facts
- NEET UG 2026 Information Bulletin (NTA), read via a mirrored copy of the PDF: https://karnatakahelp.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/NTA-NEET-UG-2026-Information-Bulletin.pdf
  - Age 17 on or before 31 Dec, citing GMER 2023 (Amendment 16.06.2023); cut-off 31.12.2009 for 2026 (p. 25)
  - No upper age limit, citing an NMC letter (p. 25). Medical Dialogues' summary of the bulletin cites an NMC letter dated 13.01.2026; the original decision was conveyed on 9 March 2022.
  - Qualifying codes 01 to 07; "Physics, Chemistry, Biology / Biotechnology along with English even as additional subjects" (pp. 26–27)
  - Qualifying percentiles 50th (UR/EWS), 40th (SC/ST/OBC-NCL), 45th (UR/EWS PwBD), 40th (reserved PwBD) (Chapter 8)
  - NRIs, OCI cardholders and foreign nationals eligible; embassy certificate for NRI/OCI; citizenship proof for foreigners; Indians and OCIs going abroad for MBBS/BDS must qualify NEET (Appendices VI, VII, X; SC order 03.02.2023 on OCI)

## Information gain vs top results (BYJU'S, Aakash, Vedantu, Adda247, PW, Careers360)
- The top results repeat "unlimited attempts" or the 50/40/45% PCB rule without quoting any regulation. Some contradict themselves (PW lists Biology as mandatory and also as additional; its PwD figures are inconsistent).
- None separates the three sources of the attempt confusion: the old 25/30 age cap, the four-attempt First Professional rule in GMER 2023, and the parliamentary panel's October 2026 recommendation.
- New here: one table per candidate type (NIOS/state open school, private, repeater, result awaited, biology added later, NRI/OCI/foreign, abroad) with the legal basis for each; the open-school history (2017 bar, Delhi HC 2018, Supreme Court 2024, NMC RTI 2025); the FMGL 2021 checklist; a clear statement that the 2027 bulletin is not out yet.
- Notes that the 50% PCB figure comes from older bulletins and isn't in the 2026 eligibility chapter, so readers should check counselling brochures.
