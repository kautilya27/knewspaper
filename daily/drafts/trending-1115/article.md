**Quick answer:** Central Government pensioners must submit their annual life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) by **30 November**. Pensioners aged 80 or above have been allowed to start from 1 October; everyone else submits in November. You can do it from home with the Jeevan Pramaan face authentication app, at your bank or post office, through doorstep banking, or at a Common Service Centre. If you miss the deadline, your pension stops from December and restarts, with arrears, once you submit.

In 2026, the Department of Pension and Pensioners' Welfare (DoPPW) is running its fifth nationwide Digital Life Certificate Campaign from **1 to 30 November 2026**. Camps are being held in about 2,000 cities and districts. That leaves about two weeks. Below are the deadlines, every way to submit, the fees and what happens if you miss it.

## Who has to submit a life certificate, and by when

A life certificate is yearly proof that the pensioner is alive; without it, the bank or post office cannot keep paying the pension. The rules differ by type of pensioner:

| Pensioner type | When to submit | How long it stays valid |
|---|---|---|
| Central Government pensioners below 80 | 1 to 30 November every year | Until 30 November of the next year |
| Super-senior pensioners (80 and above) | From 1 October to 30 November | Until 30 November of the next year |
| Family pensioners (Central Government) | November, like other pensioners; check with your bank whether the October window for those aged 80+ applies | Until 30 November of the next year |
| EPS-95 (EPFO) pensioners | Any time of the year | One year from the date of submission |

The October start for those aged 80 and above comes from a DoPPW office memorandum of 18 July 2019. The validity rule is spelt out in UIDAI's FAQ on Digital Life Certificates.

According to the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), EPS-95 pensioners can submit whenever it suits them, and the certificate stays valid for one year from that date. So check when you last submitted rather than counting down to 30 November.

State Government pensioners follow their own state's rules, so check with your treasury or pension-paying bank.

## Five ways to submit your life certificate

### 1. Face authentication from home (the quickest)

This uses your phone's camera and Aadhaar, so you need no fingerprint scanner or bank visit. DoPPW says it works on both Android and iPhone. UIDAI's FAQ recommends Android 9 or above, a camera of at least 13 MP, about 500 MB of free space and a working internet connection, though the official Jeevan Pramaan app guide lists a 5 MP camera as the minimum.

Step by step, based on the official Jeevan Pramaan Face App guide:

1. Install two apps: **AadhaarFaceRD** and **Jeevan Pramaan Face App**.
2. Open Jeevan Pramaan and complete the one-time **operator authentication**: enter the operator's Aadhaar number, mobile number and email, type in the OTP, and do a face scan. The operator can be the pensioner or a family member helping them.
3. Now do the **pensioner authentication**: enter the pensioner's Aadhaar and mobile number and the OTP received.
4. Fill in the pension details: PPO number, type of pension, sanctioning authority, disbursing agency (your bank or post office) and pension account number. If you used Jeevan Pramaan last year, these may appear pre-filled.
5. Check the preview, give consent and do the pensioner's face scan.
6. Once it succeeds, the screen shows a **Pramaan ID**, and an SMS with the ID and a download link reaches the mobile number you gave.

The certificate reaches your pension-paying agency electronically; no printout is needed. UIDAI notes the bank may take a few days to update it.

**Tips for a scan that works first time** (from UIDAI and the app guide):

- Sit in good, even light from the front, against a plain background.
- Keep only one face in the frame and look straight at the camera; blink when asked.
- If your Aadhaar photo shows you in glasses, wear them; if not, take them off. Remove tinted glasses.
- Make sure your Aadhaar biometrics are not locked.

### 2. Fingerprint or iris at a Jeevan Pramaan centre

If face scanning keeps failing, you can authenticate with a fingerprint or iris scan. This needs a UIDAI-registered biometric device attached to a Windows computer, so most people do it at a bank branch, post office or Common Service Centre (CSC). To find a CSC near you, UIDAI's FAQ says to send an SMS reading **JPL followed by a space and your PIN code** to **7738299899**.

### 3. In person at your bank or post office

Visit the branch that pays your pension and either sign the life certificate form before the official or have them generate a digital one. Carry your Aadhaar, PPO number and passbook.

### 4. Doorstep service

For pensioners who can't travel, two doorstep options are listed in UIDAI's FAQ:

- **India Post Payments Bank (IPPB):** a postman or Gramin Dak Sevak comes home with a biometric device. Book through the Postinfo app or at your post office. Nominal fee: ₹70.
- **Doorstep Banking by public sector banks:** book through the Doorstep Banking app, website or toll-free number. Nominal fee: ₹75.

During the November 2026 campaign, DoPPW says doorstep services will also be provided wherever required.

### 5. Certificate signed by an authorised official

If you can't appear in person at all, a life certificate signed by a designated official (such as a gazetted officer or magistrate, under the CPAO scheme rules) can be accepted instead. Banks have also been advised by the RBI to collect life certificates at home from pensioners over 70 and from those with a medically certified chronic illness or disability, so ask your pension-paying branch.

## Before you start: a short checklist

- **Aadhaar linked to your pension account.** UIDAI says you must link Aadhaar to the bank account where your pension comes before you can use Jeevan Pramaan.
- **PPO number handy.** It is on your Pension Payment Order and often in your passbook.
- **Re-employed or remarried?** UIDAI says re-employed and remarried pensioners cannot use face authentication. They have to submit the conventional way, at the bank or through an authorised official.

## What happens if you miss 30 November

According to UIDAI's FAQ, if the life certificate isn't submitted in November, **pension stops from December**. It is not cancelled. Once you submit the certificate and the bank updates its records, the pension restarts and the arrears are paid.

One limit matters: if the gap is **three years or more**, the bank cannot simply restart the pension. It has to be sanctioned again by the competent authority through the Central Pension Accounting Office (CPAO). For shorter gaps, the bank can pay the arrears after orders from the bank manager or officer in charge.

## How to check that your certificate was accepted

Keep the Pramaan ID from the SMS. You can download your certificate from the [Jeevan Pramaan portal](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in/) using it. If no Pramaan ID was generated, UIDAI says a certificate is not generated if wrong details are entered: try again carefully, and if it still fails, submit at your bank branch. For Aadhaar-related problems, UIDAI's helpline is **1947**, and its [Digital Life Certificate FAQ](https://www.uidai.gov.in/images/FAQs_Digital_Life_Certificate.pdf) answers most common errors.

Pension is also taxable income, so if you're sorting out your paperwork this month, our guide on [how to file your ITR online](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/how-to-file-itr-online) is a useful next step.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the last date to submit the life certificate in 2026?

For Central Government pensioners, the last date is 30 November 2026. Pensioners aged 80 or above could start from 1 October, while others submit between 1 and 30 November.

### Can I submit my life certificate online from home?

Yes. Install the AadhaarFaceRD and Jeevan Pramaan Face apps, complete the face scan with your Aadhaar and PPO details, and you'll get a Pramaan ID by SMS. Your Aadhaar must already be linked to your pension account.

### Will I lose my pension if I submit the life certificate late?

No. Pension stops from December, but it restarts with arrears once you submit. If the gap is three years or more, the pension needs fresh sanction through the CPAO before it can restart.

### Do EPS-95 pensioners also have to submit by 30 November?

No. EPFO says EPS-95 pensioners can submit their Digital Life Certificate at any time of the year, and it stays valid for one year from the date it is submitted.
