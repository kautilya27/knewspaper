## Information gain vs top results
Top results are news briefs (dates only) and travel blogs (stayvista, yatradham, tourmyindia) that mix the official Amrit Snan dates with extra unofficial "snan" dates (e.g. 19 Aug, 2 Sep, 17 Sep), still say "Shahi Snan", and push helicopter tours/stays. None combine: the officially announced dates with weekdays and tithis in one table (incl. the Nashik/Trimbakeshwar 11 vs 12 Sep split), the historical reason for the two venues, the Railways' station/holding-area plan, the NTKMA/MSRTC parking-shuttle-pedestrian-zone rules for Amrit Snan days, and the crowd-control measures turned into practical advice.
