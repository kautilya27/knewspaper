# Notes: makar-sankranti (niche #13), researched 18 Nov 2026

## Sources for key facts
- 2027 date 15 Jan; sankranti moment 9:14 pm IST 14 Jan (New Delhi): Drik Panchang sankranti list https://www.drikpanchang.com/sankranti/sankranti.html?year=2027 ; also Thai Pongal page https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/pongal/thai-pongal-date-time.html?year=2027 ; default (Lancaster, US) page shows 07:44 PST 14 Jan = 21:14 IST, consistent. https://www.drikpanchang.com/sankranti/makar-sankranti-date-time.html?year=2027
- Sunset rule (punya kaal moves to next sunrise): same Drik page.
- New Delhi punya kaal 7:15 am–5:46 pm and city windows (Chennai 6:35, Kolkata 6:19, Mumbai 7:15, Ahmedabad 7:23): Drik festival page https://www.drikpanchang.com/hindu-festivals/makar-sankranti/makar-sankranti.html?lang=en
- Maha punya kaal 7:15–9:00 am (Delhi): hindutone.com and astrosight.ai, both citing Drik. Drik's own New Delhi detail page could not be loaded for 2027 (with geoname-id it served year 2164). Not confirmed directly on Drik.
- Second sources: Wikipedia table gives 21:09 IST 14 Jan 2027 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makar_Sankranti); Astroyogi 9:14 pm, 15 Jan, punya kaal 7:10 am–6:02 pm, no city named (https://www.astroyogi.com/festival/makar-sankranti); ajmerastro ~9:10 pm, 15 Jan.
- Lohri 14 Jan, Bhogi 14, Magh Bihu/Uttarayana/Pongal 15, Mattu Pongal/Kanuma 16, Kaanum/Mukkanuma 17: Drik sankranti calendar https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/sankranti/sankranti-calendar.html . Bhakti Bharat gives Lohri 13 Jan (https://www.bhaktibharat.com/amp/en/festival/lohri). Disagreement stated in article.
- Drift: about 6h10m per year between Makar Sankranti moments; first 16 Jan in 2102; solstice coincided 291 CE (Mercier): Wikipedia, citing Daruwalla, Times of India 2022, and Mercier 2007.
- Regional names, dishes, rituals: Wikipedia Makar Sankranti regional sections (with their cited sources). Shishur ritual: Kashmiri Overseas Association booklet https://koausa.org/site/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/festivals2.pdf
- DoPT 2027: OM F. No. 12/2/2023-JCA dated 16.07.2026, via Department of Posts copy https://utilities.cept.gov.in/dop/pdfbind.ashx?id=15435 ; RH list confirmed at https://www.staffnews.in/2026/07/list-of-restricted-holidays-for-the-year-2027.html . Makar Sankranti/Magha Bihu RH on Thu 14 Jan; Pongal + Guru Gobind Singh's Birthday RH on Fri 15 Jan; none gazetted; Makar Sankranti and Pongal are in the 12 optional occasions for state capitals.

## Information gain vs top results
Top results (wionews photo gallery, panchang/astro sites, holiday sites) give either just the date and timings or a list of names. None combines: the 2027 New Delhi timings with the source disagreements; a clear why-it-shifts explanation (sidereal Makara, ~6h later each year, sunset rule, drift to 16 Jan in 2102); a state-by-state table with name, dish and ritual; and the DoPT 2027 restricted holiday position, including the 14 vs 15 Jan mismatch.
