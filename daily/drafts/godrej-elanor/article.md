A residential property is not evaluated only by its construction quality or apartment size. The development of commercial and employment centres can influence housing demand in surrounding areas. When businesses, technology companies, financial institutions, and industrial establishments expand, they create employment opportunities that attract professionals from different locations.

Residential demand is also influenced by the availability of social infrastructure. Schools, colleges, hospitals, pharmacies, grocery stores, banks, and recreational facilities support everyday activities.

Families with children may prioritise access to educational institutions, while senior citizens may consider healthcare facilities and convenient transportation essential. Working professionals may focus on nearby offices, shopping facilities, and everyday services.

## 5. Planned Urban Development and Organised Neighbourhoods

Unplanned growth can create several problems, including traffic congestion, inadequate drainage, limited open spaces, and pressure on public utilities. Planned urban development attempts to address these challenges through coordinated land use, road networks, public spaces, and supporting infrastructure.

Township development is one approach that combines residential areas with shared infrastructure and selected commercial or recreational facilities. Such planning can help organise different activities within a larger development framework.

## 6. Infrastructure and Long-Term Property Evaluation

Infrastructure improvements can influence how homebuyers assess the long-term suitability of a location. Better transportation, improved roads, and expanding social facilities may make an area more accessible and practical for residential use.

However, infrastructure development does not automatically guarantee an increase in property prices. Market conditions, construction quality, demand and supply, regulatory approvals, maintenance costs, and overall economic activity also influence property values.

Buyers should distinguish between completed infrastructure and proposed projects. A road or metro connection that is already operational offers a different level of convenience compared with an infrastructure project that is still under development or awaiting approval.

Before purchasing a home, it is advisable to verify project details, applicable approvals, connectivity information, and the expected completion status of surrounding infrastructure.
