## Is Season 5 really the final season?

Yes, and this part is official. MGM+ announced on 15 April 2026 that *From* had been renewed for a fifth and final season, a few days before Season 4 began. The Hollywood Reporter broke the news, and Variety, Deadline and TVLine reported it the same day.

According to TVLine, executive producers John Griffin (the show's creator), Jeff Pinkner and Jack Bender said in a joint statement that "we will get the chance to see our story to its conclusion." So the people making it have planned an ending. The show has not been cancelled on a cliffhanger.
