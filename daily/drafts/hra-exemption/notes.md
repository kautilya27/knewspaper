# Notes: hra-exemption-calculation (niche row #24)

## Sources for key facts
- Rule 279, Income-tax Rules 2026 (official text): least-of-three formula; 50% cities Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru; "relevant period"; salary = basic + DA if employment terms provide, other allowances excluded. It lists "Delhi" only. https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/rule-279-1
- Schedule III No. 11, Income-tax Act 2025 [See section 11]: the allowance must be for rent actually paid; home not owned by the assessee; rent actually paid. https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/schedule-iii-7
- Section 202 (new regime, the default; computed without Schedule III exemptions; salaried people opt out with the return under s.263(1)). https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-202-78
- Form 124 replaces 12BB (s.392(5)(b), Rule 205). https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/fn-124
- Final rules notified, effective 1 April 2026, with 8 cities (major outlets): Upstox https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/tax/final-income-tax-rules-2026-notified-hra-to-new-vs-old-regime-what-salaried-employees-must-know/article-191015 ; Moneylife https://www.moneylife.in/article/new-income-tax-rules-from-1st-april-hra-exemption-extended-to-8-cities-fewer-forms-but-landlord-disclosure-now-mandatory/79998.html (both dated 20 Mar 2026)
- Landlord PAN above ₹1,00,000 a year and relationship disclosure: Upstox (above), Taxmann https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/hra-exemption-calculation-income-tax-act-2025 , ClearTax Form 124 page https://cleartax.in/s/form-124-income-tax
- FY 2025-26 still under s.10(13A)/Rule 2A with 4 metros: Taxmann (above).
- TDS on rent above ₹50,000/month at 2%: s.393(1) Table Sl. 2(i), Taxmann https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/tds-on-rent-income-tax-act-2025 ; Income Tax Dept 194-IB page https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/tds-on-rent-by-certain-individual-or-huf

## Information gain vs the top results
The top results (calculators from ClearTax, Digit, Aditya Birla, ICICI Pru, Zoho, Omni, AmbitionBox, NoBroker forum) give the formula and a calculator, usually with the old 4-metro list and 10(13A) references. They miss: the new Rule 279 and Schedule III references, the year-by-year table (FY 2025-26 vs TY 2026-27), a side-by-side example showing the ₹60,000 difference from Bengaluru's move, the rent-to-parents example with conditions, Form 124's relationship field, the NCR/city-name point, and how the default new regime interacts with employer TDS.
