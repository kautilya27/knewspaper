# Notes: occupancy-certificate-vs-completion-certificate

## Top results checked (query: "occupancy certificate vs completion certificate")
Brigade Group, Housing.com, Godrej Properties, L&T Finance, BankBazaar, NoBroker, Sobha, Square Yards.
What they all say: definitions of CC and OC, a comparison table, "OC needed for utilities/loans/resale", and generic advice to "ask the builder".
What they miss:
- The actual RERA sections: 2(q), 2(zf), 11(4)(b), 17(1), 18(1), 19(10), 31. Housing.com cites none.
- Partial OC. Housing.com doesn't cover it.
- How to verify a certificate beyond "the developer portal", including cross-checking with the planning authority given forged OCs (MahaRERA 2025).
- State variation: Delhi's combined OCC with deemed issue, Bengaluru's 1,200 sq ft exemption, MahaRERA's OC upload and verification.
- Supreme Court rulings: Samruddhi 2022, Dharmendra Sharma 2024, Barjatya December 2024.
- What to do, step by step, if possession is offered without an OC.

## Left out or unverified
- Housing.com's claim that the developer must apply for the OC "within 30 days of CC": not verified, so left out.
- Haryana timelines and deemed OC (8 working days or 18 days by risk category): seen only in an amendment PDF, and the current consolidated text wasn't verified. Only the term is used.
- Whether sub-registrars refuse to register a sale without an OC: varies and wasn't verified, so it isn't claimed.
- The exact date of the Karnataka government order (July 2025 per context) isn't stated in the article.
- Debashis Sinha's own holding wasn't read directly; it is cited only as relied on in Dharmendra Sharma.
- No bank-specific disbursal rules are claimed.
- Removed after review: the claim that MahaRERA was reviewing partial OCs passed off as full OCs. It came only from a weak source, and the 2025 reports were about validating OCs of lapsed projects.
- Section 14(2) source: RERA Act (India Code). Minor changes are allowed; a change to the allottee's own unit needs that allottee's consent; other changes need consent of two-thirds of allottees.
