## Information gain vs top results
Top results (hindu-blog.com, timeanddate, quillbot, templepurohit, sanskrit.org) list the 12 names, sometimes with rough English months; timeanddate describes the full-moon/new-moon split without naming it or giving Gregorian mapping. None combines: a masa table with the purnimanta start point and a festival per month; a table of festivals that carry two month names (Diwali, Shivaratri, Janmashtami); a "which month is it now" table (Nov 2026) across purnimanta, amanta, Saka and Tamil; the Saka month start dates; verified Adhik Maas 2026 and next (2029); and Diwali 2025-28 dates illustrating the drift.
