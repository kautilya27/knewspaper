## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Business Today, Free Press Journal, NewsX, Netmeds, Onmanorama, adda247) define analogue paneer and list home tests (iodine, crumble, hot water) as if they detect it; most mention "states banning" without order details. They miss: the actual FSSAI paneer standard numbers (moisture/fat table), the exact label declarations in 2.1.1(5)(b), a dated timeline of the official orders (FSSAI April 2025 paper, April 2026 West Region notice, Maharashtra/Chhattisgarh/Gujarat orders with numbers and scope, MP announcement, Sept 2026 national draft), the point that iodine only detects starch, and the actual lab methods FSSAI uses.
