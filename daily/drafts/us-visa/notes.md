## Information gain vs the top results
The top results (immihelp, last updated 2022; BankBazaar; Royal Sundaram; Nova Credit; WorldTrips) still show the old waiver rules (under-14s and over-80s exempt), name ustraveldocs as the only portal, and leave out the 1 Oct 2025 dropbox limits, the country-of-residence rule, the one-free-reschedule rule, the social media rule for F/H-1B applicants, and whether the $250 integrity fee is collected. Many other sites add the $250 fee as if it were already charged. Our article gives a dated, official-source summary of each, a fee table for B1/B2, F-1 and H-1B, the India reciprocity figures (10-year B1/B2, no issuance fee), the marriage-certificate rule for spouses, and the fact that India is not in the $750 expedite pilot.
