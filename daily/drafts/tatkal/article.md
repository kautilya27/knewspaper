**Quick answer:** Tatkal booking opens one day before the journey date. It opens at 10:00 am for AC classes (2A, 3A, CC, EC) and at 11:00 am for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). The journey date is the date the train leaves its originating station. To book online you need an Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC account; on many trains you also enter an Aadhaar OTP during booking. Authorised agents cannot book Tatkal from 10:00 to 10:30 am (AC) or from 11:00 to 11:30 am (non-AC).

Tatkal adds 10% of the basic fare in second sitting and 30% in all other classes, within fixed minimum and maximum amounts. If you cancel a confirmed Tatkal ticket, you get no refund. A waitlisted Tatkal ticket is refunded after a small clerkage charge. Premium Tatkal opens at the same times, but its fares are dynamic, agents can't book it, and it has no waitlist.

## What changed since 2025

- **1 July 2025:** According to a [Ministry of Railways release on PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2135694&reg=48&lang=2), only Aadhaar-authenticated users can book Tatkal on the IRCTC website and app.
- **15 July 2025:** The same release made Aadhaar-based OTP mandatory for online Tatkal, required a mobile OTP for counter and agent bookings, and barred agents from the first 30 minutes of the window. The OTP part has been rolled out train by train.
- **17 November 2025 onwards:** Counter OTP began as a pilot on a few trains and covered 211 trains as of 4 December 2025, the Railway Minister told the Lok Sabha, with the rest to follow. No official notice confirming every train is covered could be found, so carry the phone whose number is on your form.
- **December 2025 to February 2026:** In a December 2025 Lok Sabha reply, the Railway Minister said Aadhaar OTP for online Tatkal was running on 322 trains; PIB said in February 2026 that it has been introduced. No notice confirming every train is covered could be found, but only Aadhaar-verified users can book Tatkal at all.
- **1 April 2026:** New cancellation slabs (72/24/8 hours) took effect for regular tickets, in step with chart timings that already applied, according to the [Railway Minister's 24 March 2026 announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2244597&reg=48&lang=2). Since journeys from 30 December 2025, the first chart is ready by 8 pm the previous day for trains leaving between 5 am and 2 pm, and at least 10 hours ahead for other trains, roughly 9 to 18 hours before departure (from July 2025 it had been 9 pm the previous day for those morning trains and 8 hours ahead for others). The Tatkal refund rule was not changed.

The opening times have not changed. IRCTC denied viral posts about new Tatkal timings in April 2025 (Business Today).

## Tatkal booking timings: the booking-day timeline

Say your train leaves its origin station on **Friday, 5 December**. Tatkal for it opens on **Thursday, 4 December**:

| Time on 4 December | What happens | Who can book |
|---|---|---|
| Before 10:00 am | Log in and check that your IRCTC profile shows Aadhaar as verified. Keep the Aadhaar-linked phone with you. | — |
| 10:00 am | AC Tatkal and AC Premium Tatkal open | Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC users online, and counters with OTP. No agents. |
| 10:30 am | Agent ban ends for AC classes | Authorised agents can now book AC Tatkal (not Premium Tatkal) |
| 11:00 am | Sleeper and second sitting Tatkal and Premium Tatkal open | Aadhaar-authenticated users online, and counters with OTP. No agents. |
| 11:30 am | Agent ban ends for non-AC classes | Authorised agents can book non-AC Tatkal |
| Night of 4 December / morning of 5 December | First chart prepared (9 to 18 hours before departure) | Waitlisted Tatkal passengers learn their final status |

### The two date traps

**Boarding midway.** The date that counts is when the train leaves its *originating* station, not when it reaches your station. Say a train leaves its origin at 10:00 pm on 4 December and you board at 3:00 am on 5 December. Tatkal opens on **3 December**, not 4 December.

**Trains just after midnight.** A train leaving at 00:15 on 6 December runs on 6 December, so its Tatkal opens on 5 December. Our explainer on the [12-hour and 24-hour clock](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/12-hour-and-24-hour-clock) shows why "12:15 am" on a ticket belongs to the next day.

## Aadhaar and OTP checklist before booking day

1. **Authenticate Aadhaar in your IRCTC profile.** It's a one-time step in the profile section of the website or app. Do it days before, not at 9:58 am.
2. **Make sure the OTP will reach you.** It goes to the mobile number linked with your Aadhaar. If you aren't sure which numbers are registered in your name, see our guide on [checking SIMs on your Aadhaar](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/sims-on-my-aadhaar).
3. **At a counter**, write a number you have with you on the form. The ticket is issued only after you give the OTP sent to it.
4. **Know the agent rule.** An agent promising a Tatkal seat "at 10:01" can't legally book in that window.
5. **Carry original photo ID.** One passenger on the ticket must show it during the journey. If nobody can, all passengers on that ticket are treated as travelling without a ticket, according to PIB. Aadhaar, voter ID, passport, driving licence and PAN card are accepted.
6. **No name changes.** Tatkal tickets can't be transferred, and a PNR holds at most four passengers.

Since January 2026, Aadhaar is also needed to book on the first day of the regular 60-day window, news reports said. A verified Tatkal account already meets this.

## Tatkal charges by class

The Tatkal charge is 10% of the basic fare for second sitting and 30% for other classes, within a minimum and maximum. These amounts date from the 25 December 2015 revision (Business Standard/PTI), and no later change was found. The 26 December 2025 fare hike raised base fares, so the 30% figure rises slightly too.

| Class | Tatkal charge (min) | Tatkal charge (max) | Minimum distance charged |
|---|---|---|---|
| Second sitting (2S) | ₹10 | ₹15 | 100 km |
| Sleeper (SL) | ₹100 | ₹200 | 500 km |
| AC Chair Car (CC) | ₹125 | ₹225 | 250 km |
| AC 3-tier (3A) | ₹300 | ₹400 | 500 km |
| AC 2-tier (2A) | ₹400 | ₹500 | 500 km |
| Executive Chair Car (EC) | ₹400 | ₹500 | 250 km |

Minimum distances are as listed by Zee Business. A short trip is charged as if it were that distance.

**Worked example:** Say a sleeper base fare is ₹450. 30% of that is ₹135, which is between ₹100 and ₹200, so you pay ₹135 extra. On a ₹800 sleeper base fare, 30% is ₹240, but the cap of ₹200 applies. Reservation fee, superfast charge and GST (for AC classes) come on top as usual.

## Tatkal vs Premium Tatkal

| | Tatkal | Premium Tatkal |
|---|---|---|
| Opens | 10:00 am AC / 11:00 am non-AC, a day before | Same |
| Fare | Base fare plus fixed-slab Tatkal charge | Dynamic: rises as seats fill |
| Agents | Allowed after the first 30 minutes | Not allowed at all |
| Waitlist / RAC | Yes | No: confirmed seats only |
| Booking mode | Online and counters | E-tickets only |
| Concessions | None | None, not even child fare |
| Refund on cancelling confirmed ticket | None | None |

These Premium Tatkal conditions are as summarised by Zee Business from IRCTC's rules; the quota isn't offered on every train. Its seat can cost well over the Tatkal fare for the same berth, so compare both quotas on the booking screen. If Premium Tatkal shows "available" while Tatkal shows a waitlist, remember you can't cancel it for money back.

## Cancellation and refunds

**Confirmed Tatkal: no refund.** Indian Railways has long said no refund is given when a confirmed Tatkal ticket is cancelled (PIB, Tatkal scheme features). The April 2026 slabs reported by Business Today apply to regular reserved tickets: a flat charge beyond 72 hours, 25% deduction at 24 to 72 hours, 50% at 8 to 24 hours, and nothing under 8 hours. No official statement extends them to confirmed Tatkal.

The usual exceptions still apply: a refund is due if the train is cancelled, or if it runs more than three hours late and you don't travel. Since April 2026, many e-ticket refunds no longer need a TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) and are processed automatically.

**Waitlisted or RAC Tatkal: refundable.** Business Today reported these still follow the older rules. You can cancel up to 30 minutes before departure, with a clerkage of about ₹60 per passenger. An online ticket still fully waitlisted after charting is cancelled automatically and refunded the same way. With charts now 9 to 18 hours ahead, you know a little earlier.

## Frequently asked questions

### What time does Tatkal booking open for sleeper class?

Sleeper (and second sitting) Tatkal opens at 11:00 am, one day before the date the train leaves its originating station. AC classes open earlier, at 10:00 am.

### Can I book a Tatkal ticket without linking Aadhaar?

Not online. Since 1 July 2025, only Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC accounts can book Tatkal on the website and app, and on selected trains an Aadhaar OTP is also required. At a reservation counter you need an OTP on the mobile number written on your form.

### Can a travel agent book Tatkal at 10 am?

No. Authorised agents are barred from 10:00 to 10:30 am for AC and from 11:00 to 11:30 am for non-AC Tatkal, according to the Ministry of Railways. They cannot book Premium Tatkal at all.

### Do I get money back if I cancel a confirmed Tatkal ticket?

No. Cancelling a confirmed Tatkal or Premium Tatkal ticket gives no refund, except in cases like a cancelled train or one running more than three hours late. Waitlisted Tatkal tickets are refunded after a clerkage of about ₹60 per passenger.
