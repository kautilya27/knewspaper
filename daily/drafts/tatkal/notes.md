## Information gain vs top results
Top results (ixigo inspiredBy, HDFC PayZapp blog, Zee News/Zee Biz, EaseMyTrip, erail, Onmanorama) all give 10/11 am timings, the charge table and "no refund on confirmed Tatkal". ixigo mentions Aadhaar OTP but treats the agent ban only in passing, gives no Premium Tatkal numbers/limits side by side, and nothing on the April 2026 refund slabs, earlier charting (9–18 h), counter-OTP rollout status, or the origin-station / midnight date traps. Our article adds: a dated change log (2025–2026) from PIB, a booking-day timeline showing who can book when, a checklist, worked charge example with cap, Tatkal vs Premium Tatkal table, and how the 2026 refund changes do/don't apply to Tatkal.

## Not verified / handled cautiously
- Whether counter OTP now covers every train nationwide: no official confirmation found; article says so.
- Lok Sabha reply of 23 Jul 2025 said OTP-based Tatkal "not introduced as yet", conflicting with PIB's 15 Jul date; resolved by citing PIB Feb 2026 confirming OTP is in place online; exact start date of online OTP not asserted beyond PIB's announcement.
- No official 2026 document explicitly restating "no refund on confirmed Tatkal"; based on longstanding rule (PIB 2016, erail, ixigo) and absence of any change in 2026 announcements. Railway Board CC 8 of 2026 PDF could not be fetched.
- Tatkal charge amounts: no revision after Dec 2015 found; not confirmed by a 2026 official tariff page. Minimum distances only from Zee Business.
- Clerkage "about ₹60" from Business Today; exact current class-wise clerkage not confirmed officially.
- Premium Tatkal source is an undated Zee Business article; "not on every train" is general knowledge.
- Chart timing: PIB says 9–18 h; Time Out says 9–12 h, ETV says at least 8 h; used PIB.
- Excluded: per-user monthly ticket limits and IP limits (2016 figures possibly outdated).
