## 2. CBSE's official sample papers: free, and not optional

Whatever else you buy, solve CBSE's own [sample question papers and marking schemes](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/) first. They show the real paper's sections, internal choices and mark split, and the marking scheme shows the points and steps examiners reward. They exist only for Classes 10 and 12; the Class 10 set includes English, Hindi A and B, Mathematics Standard and Basic, Science and Social Science, each 80 marks and 3 hours. The catch is quantity: one SQP per subject, which is why most students add a book.

## How to use sample papers well

1. **Do the official SQP first, under exam conditions.** Three hours, a quiet table, phone in another room, no notes.
2. **Mark yourself with the marking scheme, honestly.** Give a mark only where your answer has the step or point the scheme lists.
3. **Keep an error log.** In one notebook, write the question, why you lost marks (concept, calculation, time, misreading) and the correct approach. Read it before every new paper.
4. **Space the papers out.** One paper per subject per week until mid-January, then two a week. If you run out of time, note which section ate it and practise that section the next day.
5. **Redo the official SQP in the last week.** It shows how far you have come.

Presentation earns marks too: see our guide on [how to write answers in CBSE board exams](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/how-to-write-answers-in-cbse-board-exams).

### When to start

For CBSE Class 10 and 12, take the first full paper in early December, once the syllabus is mostly done. Practicals and internal assessment run from 1 January to 13 February 2027 (winter-bound schools: 9 November to 19 December 2026), so plan papers around them, and finish your last new paper about a week before the first theory exam on 16 February.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best sample paper for Class 10 CBSE 2027?

Start with CBSE's free official sample question paper and marking scheme for each subject. For extra practice, Future Topper's 2026-27 Class 10 bundle gives 60 solved papers in four subjects for ₹179 as an eBook. Oswaal, Educart and Arihant are the main printed options.

### Are Future Topper sample papers printed books?

No. They are eBooks: you download them after paying and can download them again from your account later. Print them at home if you prefer writing on paper.

### How many sample papers should I solve before the board exam?

Around 10 to 15 per main subject, including the official CBSE paper, is enough if you check each one against the marking scheme. A few carefully checked papers teach more than many unchecked ones.

### Does CBSE release sample papers for Class 9 and Class 11?

No. CBSE releases sample question papers only for Classes 10 and 12, the board exam years. CISCE's specimen papers do cover Classes 9 to 12.
