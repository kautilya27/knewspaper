# Notes: eisenhower-matrix (plan #63)

## Top results checked for "Eisenhower matrix urgent vs important"
TechTarget, Todoist, ClickUp, Asana, Dropbox blog (and MindTools, intent only).
What they all say: four quadrants (do / schedule / delegate / delete), definitions of urgent vs important, generic examples, app-specific how-tos.
What they miss:
- Accurate origin: most say "Eisenhower's matrix" or give only "a 1954 speech". Few mention he attributed the saying to an unnamed former college president, the exact venue (WCC Second Assembly, Northwestern campus, Evanston, 19 Aug 1954), or that he never drew a grid (Covey, 1989).
- Research: Asana mentions the mere urgency effect but without the study citation or its moderators (busyness, payoff reminders).
- No Indian examples; no filled-in matrices for a student / IT employee / shop owner.
- Almost no honest limits (subjective importance, no one to delegate to, no sequencing, burnout).
