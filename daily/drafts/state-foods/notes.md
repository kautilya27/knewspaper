# Notes: famous-food-of-indian-states

## Top results for "famous food of indian states" (checked 19 Nov 2026)
Swiggy blog (inspiredBy), Club Mahindra, Zee News lists, ritiriwaz, wildvoyager, traveltriangle.
- What they all do: one dish per state, a photo and a paragraph. Swiggy covers only the 28 states, with no UTs, staples, sweets or GI info.
- What they miss: the 8 UTs, the staple/sweet split, and any verified GI data. None say that food varies within a state.

## Information gain
- A complete 36-row table (28 states + 8 UTs) in six regional tables: signature dish / staple / sweet / GI foods.
- The GI column is checked against APEDA's official state-wise registered food and agri GI pages (data up to May 2024), plus later registrations verified in news (Jharkhand 2026, Andaman Dec 2024). Where nothing was found, the cell says "None found" rather than guessing.
- PIB figures for GI counts by state; the Odisha/West Bengal dual-rasgulla GI; what a GI does and doesn't protect.

## Not verified / judgement calls
- The dish, staple and sweet choices for states are widely known food knowledge and were not each sourced. A reviewer may want to spot-check the less familiar ones: Chhattisgarh dehrori, HP mittha, Meghalaya pukhlein, Haryana malpua, MP mawa bati.
- The Andaman GI year (2024) is inferred from the PTI dateline (Dec 4) plus the Jan 2025 follow-up. The PTI text doesn't state the year.
- "None found" for Chandigarh, Puducherry, Lakshadweep and DNHDD: based on the APEDA list and one search each. There could be very recent registrations.
- Skipped APEDA items with a blank certificate date (e.g. Arunachal Adi Apong, Bhaderwah Rajmash, Uttarakhand Lal Chawal) in case they aren't fully registered.
- APEDA lists Chak-Hao under Nagaland too; treated as Manipur only (likely a listing error).
- Kangra Tea, Coorg coffee, Malabar pepper and similar board-administered GIs weren't checked, so they're left out.
- "India has no official national dish" is stated from general knowledge. It's not sourced this session.
