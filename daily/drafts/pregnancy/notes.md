Top results reviewed (searches "early symptoms of pregnancy" and "pregnancy symptoms", 17 Nov 2026)
- Mayo Clinic (competitor): classic and other symptoms, short caveats on look-alikes, a line on home tests and prenatal vitamins.
- NHS signs and symptoms of pregnancy: symptom list, "usually start around 4 to 6 weeks", flags bleeding and tummy/pelvic pain.
- NICHD (NIH): signs with timings after conception, look-alike causes of a missed period.
- The Bump / Live Science / WebMD / Pampers: long symptom lists (12 to 19 items) with little sourcing.
What they share: a symptom list. What they miss for Indian readers: one timeline table that explains LMP-based week counting, a clear test-timing rule with hCG explained, a single urgent-warning section, and any Indian next steps (first-trimester registration, MCP card, PMSMA, JSSK, JSY).

Information gain in the article
- Timeline table (NHS + NICHD) with an explanation of how pregnancy weeks are counted.
- Look-alikes list from the NHS missed-periods page (stress, thyroid, PCOS, contraception etc.).
- Test timing: first day of missed period / 21 days after unprotected sex (NHS).
- Urgent warning signs: ectopic, heavy bleeding, hyperemesis, MCP-card danger signs.
- India next steps: first-trimester registration, MCP card, PMSMA (9th of month), JSSK, JSY.

Sources and what each confirms
- https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/trying-for-a-baby/signs-and-symptoms-of-pregnancy/ : symptom list; symptoms usually start around 4 to 6 weeks pregnant; nausea any time of day; spotting around when period is due.
- https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/trying-for-a-baby/doing-a-pregnancy-test/ : test from first day of missed period; 21 days after unprotected sex if unsure; hCG made from about six days after fertilisation; negative less reliable; retest and see a doctor.
- https://www.nichd.nih.gov/health/topics/pregnancy/conditioninfo/signs : fatigue from about 1 week after conception; breast changes 1 to 2 weeks; spotting 6 to 12 days after conception; nausea 2 to 8 weeks after conception; mood swings a few weeks; headaches.
- https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/getting-pregnant/in-depth/symptoms-of-pregnancy/art-20043853 : search intent only; also says some people have few or no symptoms (FAQ 4, consistent with NHS "not everyone has every symptom").
- https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/missed-or-late-periods/ : causes of late periods (stress, weight, exercise, contraception, breastfeeding, PCOS, thyroid, diabetes, perimenopause 45 to 55). Note: NHS page now calls PCOS "PMOS (previously called PCOS)"; article uses the familiar term PCOS.
- https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/ectopic-pregnancy/ : one-sided low tummy pain, bleeding/brown watery discharge, shoulder-tip pain, dizziness/fainting, paleness; symptoms usually between weeks 4 and 12; emergency.
- https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/miscarriage/symptoms/ : any bleeding needs urgent help; heavy bleeding or bleeding with severe pain/dizziness is an emergency.
- https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/complications/severe-vomiting/ : can't keep food/drink down, dehydration signs, weight loss: contact hospital/doctor promptly.
- https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/pregnancy-vitamins-and-supplements/ : folic acid when trying and for the first 12 weeks; reduces neural tube defects; some need a higher dose prescribed by a doctor (no numbers used in article).
- https://nhm.gov.in/New_Updates_2018/NHM_Components/Immunization/Guildelines_for_immunization/MCP_Card_English_version.pdf : "Register with the health centre in the 1st trimester"; danger signs (high fever, headache with blurred vision, fits, swelling all over body, bleeding); JSY. (Older MCP card version; the 2018 revised card could not be fetched, robots disallowed.)
- https://www.who.int/en/news-room/detail/07-11-2016-pregnant-women-must-be-able-to-access-the-right-care-at-the-right-time-says-who : first antenatal contact within the first 12 weeks.
- https://pmsma.mohfw.gov.in/about-scheme/ : free minimum ANC package incl. investigations and drugs, 9th of every month, designated government facilities, women in 2nd/3rd trimesters.
- https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1843841 (PIB, 22 Jul 2022) : JSSK free delivery incl. caesarean in public institutions, free transport, diagnostics, medicines, diet, blood; JSY is a conditional cash transfer for institutional delivery; PMSMA 9th of every month.
