## Information gain vs top results
Top explainers (Outlook Business Dec 2025, Deccan Herald, Gizbot, The Federal, Onmanorama) are from Dec 2025 and still state the six-hour logout and the 90-day timeline. Beebom's Oct 2026 guide is current on the deadline and risk-based logout but lacks the Rajya Sabha "no fixed period" wording, the reported 24-hour check, the 18 Nov 2026 I4C advisory, the PIB roaming exception, and a situation-by-situation table. Our piece adds a dated timeline table with sources, a situation table (NRI, dual SIM, iPad, laptop, expired prepaid, shared business number) stating clearly where DoT hasn't specified, and Indian follow-up steps (Sanchar Saathi/Aadhaar SIM check, CEIR).
