**Quick answer:** Rann Utsav 2026-27 runs from **1 November 2026 to 7 March 2027** at Dhordo in Kutch, Gujarat, according to [Gujarat Tourism](https://gujarattourism.com/fair-and-festival/rann-utsav.html). The full moons this season fall on **24 November, 24 December (early morning), 22 January and 21 February (early morning)**. Fly or take a train to Bhuj, then drive about 80 km north to Dhordo.

A tent booking does not get you onto the salt by itself. The White Rann sits in a border area, so every visitor needs a separate entry permit. It costs ₹100 per adult and you can get it online or at the Bhirandiyara checkpost. If tent city rates are too steep, stay in Bhuj or a village resort near Dhordo and drive in for sunset.

## Rann Utsav 2026-27 dates and who runs the tent city

Gujarat Tourism lists the season as 1 November 2026 to 7 March 2027. The tent city at Dhordo is run by **Evoke Experiences** in partnership with Gujarat Tourism, according to the official site [rannutsav.net](https://rannutsav.net/) and a [Republic World report](https://www.republicworld.com/travel/rann-utsav-202627-to-begin-november-1-evoke-experiences-announces-upgraded-stays-and-new-visitor-experiences-2026-11-03-140302) from 3 November 2026. Bookings go through booking.rannutsav.net. Gujarat Tourism's page links to both sites.

- **December is the crunch.** The 23-24 December full moon is right before Christmas, so tents and flights fill up fastest then.

- **Expect to pay more.** The official tariff page says a "peak season surcharge applies from one day prior to the full moon dates". It does not give the amount.

## White Rann permit: who issues it, fee and online process

### Why you need a permit

The White Rann and Kalo Dungar lie close to the India-Pakistan border. Entry is controlled through a permit. The online system was launched by the **Kutch district administration** (Collector's office), [DeshGujarat reported](https://deshgujarat.com/2018/11/16/online-permit-system-for-visit-to-white-rann-of-kutch-launched-by-collector-with-dedicated-website/) when it went live. It covers both the White Rann and Kalo Dungar.

### White Rann permit fee

| Who or what | Fee |
|---|---|
| Adult (above 12) | ₹100 |
| Child (6-12 years) | ₹50 |
| Child up to 5 years | Free |

These fees come from the district's 2018 announcement, as reported by DeshGujarat. [Esquire India's 2026-27 guide](https://www.esquireindia.co.in/lifestyle/travel/the-only-rann-utsav-guide-you-need-luxury-stays-full-moon-nights-and-kutchs-best-experiences) also gives ₹100 per adult. The child rate may have been revised since then, and the 2018 vehicle charges (₹25 for a two-wheeler, ₹50 for a car) are no longer reliably reported, so check the current amounts on the portal.

### How to get it online or at the checkpost

1. **Online:** Apply on the official Gujarat government permit portal (a gujarat.gov.in address; Esquire India describes it as Gujarat Tourism's portal). The main traveller uploads an ID proof and pays the fee. **Be careful with look-alike sites:** the old address rannpermit.com, which DeshGujarat reported in 2018, no longer belongs to the government and redirects elsewhere, so never upload ID or pay there.
2. **On the spot:** Buy it at the checkpost in **Bhirandiyara village**, on the way from Bhuj to Dhordo. Carry original photo ID for everyone in the group.
3. **If you're staying at the tent city:** Esquire India says many official packages include the permit. Ask Evoke before you queue for one separately.

Applying online is meant to cut the wait at the checkpost, as DeshGujarat noted.

## How to reach Dhordo for Rann Utsav

### By air

Bhuj airport is about 4 km from the city, according to Gujarat Tourism. After Air India added a morning Delhi flight, Bhuj has four daily flights to Delhi and Mumbai, DeshGujarat reported on 26 October 2026.

### By train

Bhuj station is on the Western Railway network. Overnight trains such as the Kutch Express and the Sayajinagari Express link Bhuj with Mumbai. Check current timings on IRCTC. The Namo Bharat Rapid Rail between Ahmedabad and Bhuj covers 359 km in about 5 hours 45 minutes, according to a 2024 railways statement reported by DT Next.

### By road from Bhuj

Dhordo is about 80 to 85 km from Bhuj, according to the Ministry of Tourism's rural tourism portal and rannutsav.net. Expect a drive of roughly 1.5 to 2 hours. The tent city's packages include shared transfers from Bhuj airport and railway station at fixed times: 8:15 am, 10 am, 1:30 pm, 3:30 pm and 5:30 pm. Transfers at any other time cost extra.

### Staying in Bhuj instead

Bhuj has far more hotel choice than Dhordo; Gujarat Tourism's Kutch list includes the heritage hotel The Bhuj House. It suits you if you want time in Bhuj and its craft villages, have awkward flight times, or need just one sunset on the salt.

The catch: a sunset visit is a round trip of about 160 to 170 km, a moonlit walk means a late drive back, and you miss the tent city's shows.

### Village resorts near the Rann

Hodka and Dhordo have village-run resorts with *bhungas*, the round mud houses of Banni. Outlook Traveller describes Shaam-e-Sarhad in Hodka, 63 km from Bhuj, as run by local villagers. You'll still need the White Rann permit if you stay at one of these.

## Places to add near Dhordo

- **Kalo Dungar:** At 462 m it's the highest point in Kutch, about 90 km north of Bhuj, according to Gujarat Tourism. It comes under army jurisdiction, and Gujarat Tourism says photography is not allowed. It's covered by the same permit.
- **Dholavira:** This Harappan city became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2021. It's about 250 km from Bhuj via Bhachau and Rapar, according to Gujarat Tourism, so give it a full day or stay overnight at the Dholavira camp.
- **Mandvi:** A beach town 58 km southwest of Bhuj. Vijay Vilas Palace is 7 km west of it.
- **Dhordo village:** UN Tourism named it a Best Tourism Village in 2023, as Outlook Traveller notes.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the best full moon for Rann Utsav 2026-27?

The full moon falls on 24 November, 24 December (6:58 am), 22 January and 21 February (4:52 am). Outlook Traveller flags Christmas and New Year as peak demand, so the November and January full moons are easier to book.

### Do tent city guests need the White Rann permit?

Yes. Esquire India says many official tent city packages already include it, so confirm with Evoke before you buy one.

### Can I visit the White Rann without staying in the tent city?

Yes. Get the ₹100 permit online or at Bhirandiyara and drive about 80 km from Bhuj for sunset. The tent city's own facilities and shows are meant for its guests, so plan your meals and evening around Dhordo village or Bhuj.

### How many days do I need for Rann Utsav?

Two nights is typical. Republic World says that's the average stay. Add a day each for Dholavira and Mandvi.
