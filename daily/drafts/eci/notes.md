# Notes: election-commission-of-india-powers (civics #9)

## Information gain vs top results
Top results for "election commission of india powers and functions" (GeeksforGeeks, GKToday, BYJU'S, Wikipedia, Adda247/Testbook, Bajaj Finserv, iPleaders) are static UPSC-style lists of functions. They miss or are out of date on: the 2023 appointments Act with both sides and the 23 Sept 2026 referral to a Constitution Bench (and 20 Nov 2026 recall plea); the Section 16 immunity challenge; the MCC's actual legal status; the April 2024 VVPAT directions; the 27 May 2026 SIR judgment and live Phase III/Delhi status; the 2002 limits on deregistration vs the 2025 delisting and Kerala HC stay. Our article adds a provisions table, current court status, and a "cannot do" list.
