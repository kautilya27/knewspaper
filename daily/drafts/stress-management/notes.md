# Notes: stress-management (plan #51)

## Sources for key facts
- WHO definition of stress, signs, self-help tips (routine, sleep, exercise, limit news, connect, substances), "persistent stress disrupting functioning may indicate a condition": https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/stress
- Burnout as ICD-11 occupational phenomenon, three dimensions, "should not be applied to other areas of life", not a medical condition: https://www.who.int/news-room/detail/28-05-2019-burn-out-an-occupational-phenomenon-international-classification-of-diseases
- Doing What Matters: five skills named on WHO page: https://www.who.int/thailand/activities/doing-what-matters-in-times-of-stress ; a few minutes a day, languages, audio list: https://production-cms.platform.who.int/fctc/resources/publications/i/item/9789240003927
- Step-by-step content of each skill (grounding notice/slow down/refocus; unhooking notice/name/refocus; values + one small action, leave/change-accept/give up; being kind; making room): summary reproducing the WHO guide at https://ssanpete.org/links-ss/mental-health/doing-what-matters-in-times-of-stress.html (WHO PDF itself failed to fetch: digitallibrary.un.org and depression.lab.iu.edu copies)
- Audio exercise lengths 2–9 min: https://internationalmedicalcorps.org/?p=13284
- ACT basis, part of Self-Help Plus, Turkey pilot RCT: https://www.mhpss.net/toolkit/curated-space-for-psychological-interventions/resource/doing-what-matters-in-times-of-stress-an-illustrated-guide
- SH+ Uganda trial (694 women, Lancet Global Health Jan 2020, K6 distress reduced at 3 months, longer-term unknown): https://news.liverpool.ac.uk/2020/01/23/study-highlights-effectiveness-of-behavioural-interventions-in-conflict-affected-regions/
- WHO 2020 physical activity guidelines (reduces symptoms of depression and anxiety; 150–300 min/week moderate): https://www.who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789240014886
- NHS breathing exercise (count 1–5 in/out, at least 5 min): https://nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress
- NHS stress symptoms, things to avoid, when to see GP: https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/feelings-and-symptoms/stress/ ; split tasks, plan ahead, physical symptoms should stop when stress does: https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-health-issues/stress/
- Tele-MANAS 14416 / 1-800-891-4416, launched Oct 2022, toll-free: https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2022057 ; 24/7, 20 languages, 18,27,951 calls by 7 Feb 2025: https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/feb/doc202527497201.pdf
- Manodarpan toll-free 8448440632 for students (Ministry of Education, PIB 7 Dec 2022): https://dsel.education.gov.in/sites/default/files/update/PIB1881446.pdf
- WHO/ILO long hours (55+ h: +35% stroke, +17% IHD death; 745,000 deaths 2016; heavy burden in SE Asia and Western Pacific): https://www.who.int/news/item/17-05-2021-long-working-hours-increasing-deaths-from-heart-disease-and-stroke-who-ilo
- Economic Survey 2024-25 on 55–60 h weeks (news report): https://www.indiatvnews.com/business/news/economic-survey-flags-mental-health-concerns-at-work-place-60-hour-week-will-have-adverse-affects-2025-01-31-973971
- NMHS 2015-16 (NIMHANS; 34,802 adults, 12 states, 10.6% current, treatment gap 28–83%): MoHFW summary report https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/National%20Mental%20Health%20Survey%2C%202015-16%20-%20Summary%20Report_0.pdf

## Information gain vs top results (HelpGuide, Simply Psychology, BetterUp, GoodRx, Positive Psychology)

This draft adds: WHO's actual five DWM skills step by step with the trial evidence behind them; a stress vs burnout (ICD-11 wording) vs anxiety disorder table; Indian context (Tele-MANAS and Manodarpan numbers verified from PIB/MoE, NMHS 2015-16 figures from MoHFW report, WHO-ILO long-hours data and Economic Survey 2024-25); clear when-to-see-a-doctor list.
