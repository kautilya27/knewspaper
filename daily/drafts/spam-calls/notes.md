## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Beebom 2021, Business Today 2023, Business Insider, 91mobiles, Asianet, Vi FAQ) give operator DND steps only, use old codes (START 0 / FULLY BLOCK), cite 3-day windows (even TRAI's own FAQ page still says 3 days) and don't separate DND, UCC complaints and Chakshu. None covers the Feb 2025 amendment figures together with the Sept 2026 Third Amendment (BLOCK PROMO, FULLY BLOCK migration, 3-complaint + AI-flag threshold, 15-day appeal, caller-ID app limits on 140/1600/1601). Our piece adds a three-way comparison table, current rules with sources, the 140/1600/header guide, and when to use 1930 instead.
