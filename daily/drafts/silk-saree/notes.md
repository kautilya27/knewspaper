# Notes: how-to-identify-pure-silk-saree (niche #14, fashion)

## Sources for key facts
- SMOI FAQ (silkmarkindia.com/index.php/faq/): label only on 100% pure silk by Authorised Users; hologram, unique serial number and QR code against duplication; Silk Mark does NOT assure zari purity/quality; label price ₹5.90; misuse -> notice, then cancellation + newspaper notice; SMOI facilitates replacement/refund and consumer court; online buyers keep original bill; testing at SMOI offices/testing centres; "very difficult to identify silk just by look and feel"; burn smell like burnt hair; four silks and states; silk sari lasts generations.
- SMOI page (silkmarkindia.com/index.php/smoi/): launched 17 June 2004, CSB / Ministry of Textiles initiative, registered society, HQ Bengaluru, ten chapters; paper hangtags and sew-in labels with hologram and unique number.
- SMOI AU page: premises inspection, legal agreement; helpline +91 82828 24345 Mon-Fri 10-5.
- SMOI home: Label Authentication page (series letter A-Z + serial number), Locate Authorised Outlets.
- SMOI How to Test Silk: flame test results (silk / cotton-rayon / nylon-polyester).
- SMOI Silk Testing Center page: all 10 chapters operational as testing centres (Srinagar-Pulwama, New Delhi, Guwahati, Varanasi, Kolkata, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Palakkad).
- SMOI 2024 post (silkmarkindia.com/index.php/2024/03/20/hellow/): confirmatory test at a chapter is free, test certificate issued.
- Fibre2Fashion, 17 Mar 2019: nine SMOI testing centres in Bengaluru, ₹20 per test, flame + microscopic, non-damaging.
- Wikipedia "Silk Mark" (citing The Hindu): hang-on tags reused/faked -> fabric label proposed. FashionUnited Mar 2013: fusion label fused into fabric.
- CSB csb.gov.in/node/408: India second-largest producer, largest consumer; mulberry states incl. West Bengal. csb.gov.in/node/582 (Vanya silk): tasar states, India exclusive producer of tropical tasar; eri open-ended cocoons, spun, ~64% of vanya; muga golden, Assam >95%, most expensive.
- Kancheepuram GI application, Form GI-1 by TN Dept of Handlooms & Textiles (origin-gi.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/10-kancheepuram-silk.pdf): pure mulberry silk; co-op zari ~57% silver, ~0.59% gold, ~24% silk, ~18.41% copper (scan partly garbled).
- FashionUnited, Jan 2012: TN approved zari norm change 57%->40% silver, 0.6%->0.5% gold.
- IP India GI public search, application 15: Kancheepuram Silk, applicant Dept of Handlooms & Textiles, filed 07-10-2004, certificate 02-06-2005, valid to 06-10-2034, 12 authorised users listed incl. Co-optex.
- varanasi.nic.in: Banaras Brocades and Sarees GI certificate 04/09/2009.
- handicrafts.gov.in Surat Zari Craft page: real zari uses pure silver; imitation zari = copper wire electroplated with silver.
- BIS TXD 28 18th meeting agenda, 12 Dec 2024 (services.bis.gov.in/tmp/17749_11122024171155_0.pdf): CSTRI proposed computerised zari testing protocol; gold grades A >0.55% ... E <0.20%; silver A >50% ... E <20%. Proposal only.
- kancheepuram.nic.in GI page: zari testing unit (TN govt, TIFAC, Tamilnadu Zari) checks gold/silver content, nominal fee.

## Information gain vs top results
