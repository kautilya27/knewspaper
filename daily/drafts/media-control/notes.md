# Notes: media-control-and-dictatorship (civics.csv row 5)

## Information gain: top results
Queries: "is media control a sign of dictatorship", "how dictators control the media" (WebSearch, 17 Nov 2026).

1. CPD Bangladesh op-ed (cpd.org.bd, "Autocrats and media control"): loyalist appointments and censorship; USSR, China, Myanmar, Eritrea. No India, no legal safeguards, no reader checklist.
2. International Media Support (mediasupport.org, "how autocrats use the media to keep control"): Myanmar, Afghanistan, Russia; lawfare, SLAPPs, infrastructure control. No India.
3. Lawfare (lawfaremedia.org, "How authoritarians control the news"): book review; Russia/China laws, Hungary's bought-up outlets, online manipulation. Mentions India only in opinionated terms, with no constitutional or legal angle.
4. Journal of Democracy ("How autocrats use proxies to control the media"): proxy-owned private media as the main propaganda channel. No India, no legal safeguards.
5. Foreign Policy ("Is 'the Media' Really Under Attack?", 2024): tactics against journalists, the press and owners (Hungary, Turkey, Italy). Does not separate regulation from control.
Also seen: Norris & Inglehart academic paper; CIMA "Tipping Point" report.

**What they all say:** autocrats use censorship, ownership capture, money and lawfare.
**What they miss (our gap):** (a) a clear table separating legitimate regulation from control; (b) a dated, sourced examples table; (c) India's Article 19(1)(a)/19(2) and the Supreme Court line of cases on press freedom; (d) India's own documented Emergency censorship; (e) India's latest RSF and Freedom House figures with the government's stated objections in Parliament; (f) a practical reader checklist. All are in the article.
