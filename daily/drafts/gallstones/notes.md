# Notes: gallstones-symptoms-causes (plan #75)

## Sources per key fact
- Symptoms (pain >30 min to hours, middle/right under ribs, nausea), urgent GP if >30 min, emergency list (sudden severe pain, pain to back with vomiting, high temperature/shivery, jaundice, don't drive), risk factors (over 40, women, obesity, high-fat low-fibre diet, rapid weight loss, diabetes, Crohn's), ultrasound + blood test, no treatment for silent stones, cholecystectomy, ERCP for bile duct stones: NHS https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/gallstones/
- Causes (too much cholesterol/bilirubin, too few bile salts, poor emptying), attacks after heavy meals, evening/night, more attacks after first, emergency signs incl. tea-coloured urine and light stools, most stones silent: NIDDK https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/gallstones/symptoms-causes
- Stone types, oestrogen/pregnancy/HRT/pill, diabetes, insulin resistance, triglycerides, cirrhosis, haemolytic anaemia, Crohn's, rapid weight loss, high-calorie refined-carb low-fibre diet, obesity esp. women: NIDDK https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/gallstones/definition-facts
- Pain spreading to right shoulder / between shoulder blades; women ~3x; ultrasound first test; MRCP: Cleveland Clinic https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/gallstones
- 20 in 100 have symptoms, 80 don't; ~10 in 100 looser stools after removal; liver still makes bile: NHS England decision aid https://www.england.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/PRN00250-dst-making-a-decision-about-gallstones.pdf
- Delhi community study 4.3% (Punjabis 7.4%, Bengalis 4.4%, south Indians 1.8%), 1966 railway survey 7x north vs south, cholesterol stones north vs pigment south: Tandon RK, World J Gastroenterol 2000;6(Suppl 3):4-5 https://wjgnet.com/1007-9327/full/v6/iSuppl3/4.htm
- Gangetic basin rural survey 4.15% overall, 5.59% women, 1.99% men; unsafe water and heavy metals as possible GBC factors: Unisa S et al., HPB 2011;13(2):117-125 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3044346
- GBC ASR 11.8/lakh women north India, 17.1 north-east, <1 south/west; high-risk states along Ganga/Yamuna/Sutlej/Brahmaputra; 80% of Indian GBC patients have gallstones; symptomatic gallstones 20x north vs south; prophylactic cholecystectomy criteria (stones >30 mm, non-functioning GB, polyps, family history, remote areas), no uniform Indian guidelines: Dutta U, Bush N, Kalsi D, Popli P, Kapoor VK, Chin Clin Oncol 2019;8(4):33 https://cco.amegroups.org/article/view/28518/25067
- Gallbladder cleanse: no reliable evidence; "stones" are oil/juice globs; nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea: Mayo Clinic https://www.mayoclinic.org/gallbladder-cleanse/faq-20058134
- Gallstones vs kidney stones (pain location, urinary symptoms, jaundice): Cleveland Clinic https://health.clevelandclinic.org/whats-the-difference-between-gallbladder-and-kidney-stones

## Information gain vs top results (NIDDK, Mayo, NHS, HSE, Cleveland, Hopkins)
All cover symptoms, causes, risk factors, complications in a Western context. None give: India's north-south difference in gallstone frequency and stone type (with Indian study figures in a table); the Gangetic-belt gallbladder cancer link with registry rates, put in proportion; why Indian surgeons may advise surgery for some silent stones; the NHS decision-aid "20 in 100" figure; a gallstones-vs-kidney-stones table.
