## Information gain vs top results
Top results (ClearTax home-bakers page, IndiaFilings home bakery, Kotak inspiredBy page, LegalRaasta etc.) still give the pre-April-2026 ₹12 lakh / ₹20 crore thresholds, 1–5 year validity and renewals; ClearTax tells home bakers to use "Restaurant". None maps home businesses to the official FoSCoS kinds of business, flags that "Caterer" has no registration tier, explains auto-migration of old State licences, or cites the Dec 2024 e-commerce advisory / own-website e-commerce licence rule. This article gives the current 2026 thresholds, fees and perpetual validity from FSSAI's own documents, a category-mapping table, and the aggregator rules.

## Could not verify / handled with care
- Whether pre-1 April 2026 registrations also became perpetual (FAQ explicitly covers those issued on/after 1.4.2026); article says "issued from 1 April 2026".
- Exact list item "net quantity" was not in the Labelling FAQ fetched (it is a standard requirement; included briefly).
- Whether taking orders via WhatsApp/Instagram counts as "own e-commerce platform": not addressed by FSSAI; article says so and suggests asking the Designated Officer.
- Mapping "home kitchen cooking dishes to order for delivery apps" to Restaurants is an interpretation of the official category definitions (both lead to ₹100 registration).
- Whether the 7-day registration timeline is changed by "instant registration": both stated, neither contradicted.
- Not used: Kotak's capacity limits (100 kg/day etc.); the April 2026 FoSCoS sheet lists no capacity limits.
