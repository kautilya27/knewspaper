## Sources for key facts
- Nutrient values per 100 g (486 kcal, 34.4 g fibre, 30.7 g fat, 17.8 g PUFA 18:3/ALA, 16.5 g protein, 42.1 g carb, 631 mg Ca, 335 mg Mg, 7.72 mg Fe): USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, FDC ID 170554 "Seeds, chia seeds, dried", read via https://getfoodfacts.com/food/seeds-chia-seeds-dried-170554 (mirror of USDA; fdc.nal.usda.gov not fetched directly). Per-28 g column calculated by me (x0.28).
- 1 oz = 2-3 tablespoons; pudding 2 tbsp in 1/2 cup milk, 15 min/overnight: Harvard Health https://www.health.harvard.edu/nutrition/chia-seed-benefits-what-you-need-to-know
- Average serving 2 tbsp, start with 1 tbsp, fibre/gut side effects, soak: Cleveland Clinic https://health.clevelandclinic.org/benefits-of-chia-seeds
- IFCT 2017 nuts & oil seeds group has 21 items, no chia (includes linseed, garden cress): https://nin.res.in/ebooks/IFCT2017.pdf (read characters 0-200,000; full H group list seen)
- ALA AI 1.1 g women / 1.6 g men; conversion "only in very small amounts": NIH ODS https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/omega3fattyacids-consumer/
- Teoh et al. 2018, Nutrition Reviews 76(4):219-242, 12 RCTs, no significant overall differences, GRADE low/very low: https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/clinical-evidence-on-dietary-supplementation-with-chia-seed-isalv/ (PubMed 29452425 blocked by captcha)
- 2024 glycaemic meta-analysis, Diabetes & Metabolic Syndrome, 8 RCTs, 362 people, no effect on FBG/HbA1c/insulin: https://keele-repository.worktribe.com/output/875298 (PMID 38917708)
- Karimi et al. 2024, Nutrition & Metabolism 21:74, 10 RCTs, 424 people, waist -2.82 cm, SBP -3.27 mmHg, CRP lower; no change BMI, lipids, glycaemic markers: https://nutritionandmetabolism.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12986-024-00847-3 (note: paper has internal inconsistencies, e.g. HDL direction, BMI p-value; I reported only the clear results)
- MSKCC About Herbs (chia): "very limited" evidence, no effect on weight loss in a trial, case reports (oesophageal impaction, anaphylaxis, dermatitis), antihypertensive interaction, omega-3 and bleeding: https://mskcc.org/cancer-care/integrative-medicine/herbs/chia
- Choking case: Rawl RE, Carolinas Medical Center, ACG 2014 abstract P643: https://gi.org/journals-publications/acg-blog/watch-it-grow-esophageal-impaction-with-chia-seeds/ ; 39-year-old, tablespoon dry seeds then water: https://www.healthday.com/health-news/alternative-medicine/use-chia-seeds-with-caution-researcher-warns-692782.html
- FSSAI: chia seed standards in FSS (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Third Amendment Regulations, 2020 per FSSAI news bulletin https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/media/FSSAI_News_Gazette_FNB_25_08_2020.pdf ; chia oil standards in Third Amendment Regulations, 2019 per https://foodsafetyhelpline.com/tag/salvia-hispanica/ (2018 draft: https://mail.fssai.gov.in/upload/media/FSSAI_News_Chia_Oil_FNB_18_07_2018.pdf)
- Chia vs sabja (species, origin, look, swelling times, uses): Metropolis Healthcare https://www.metropolisindia.com/blog/preventive-healthcare/sabja-vs-chia-seeds-guide

## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Healthline, Harvard, Cleveland Clinic, MNT, listicles) list 6-13 "benefits" with little quantification of trial evidence, no Indian regulation, no sabja comparison. Ours: named meta-analyses with sizes and limits (most benefits not supported), USDA per-serving table plus note that IFCT 2017 lacks chia, chia vs sabja table, FSSAI standard status, Indian ways to eat it, documented choking case and MSKCC interaction/allergy cautions.
