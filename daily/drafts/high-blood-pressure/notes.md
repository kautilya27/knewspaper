# Notes: high-blood-pressure (plan #65)

## Sources for key facts
- WHO definition (≥140/90 on two different days), symptoms at ≥180/120, complications, risk factors: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hypertension
- WHO India: ~220 million with hypertension, only 12% controlled; IHCI launched Nov 2017 by MoHFW, ICMR, WHO India, state govts (Resolve to Save Lives technical partner): https://www.who.int/india/health-topics/hypertension
- WHO 2023 global report, India: 188.3 million aged 30–79, 37% diagnosed, ~30% treated: https://theprint.in/health/31-indians-live-with-hypertension-only-37-diagnosed-in-time-fewer-treated-says-who/1769424
- ICMR-INDIAB 35.5%, 113,043 people, 31 states/UTs, Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology (June 2023): https://business-standard.com/health/over-11-4-indians-diabetic-35-5-suffer-from-hypertension-lancet-journal-123060900263_1.html
- NFHS-5: 24% men, 21% women (≥140/90); over 1 in 5 men aged 30–39; almost half of seniors: https://www.deccanherald.com/india/24-men-21-women-have-hypertension-survey-1108227.html
- US 2025 AHA/ACC categories (normal <120/<80; elevated 120–129/<80; stage 1 130–139 or 80–89; stage 2 ≥140 or ≥90; severe >180/120): https://www.ama-assn.org/delivering-care/hypertension/new-bp-guideline-5-things-physicians-should-know
- ESC 2024: elevated 120–139/70–89; hypertension ≥140/90: https://escardio.org/The-ESC/Press-Office/Press-releases/New-ESC-Hypertension-Guidelines-recommend-intensified-BP-targets-and-introduce-a-novel-elevated-blood-pressure-category-to-better-identify-people-at-risk-for-heart-attack-and-stroke (ESC "non-elevated" <120/70 from general knowledge of the guideline, consistent with the elevated range lower bound)
- Hypertensive crisis >180/120, recheck after 1 minute, emergency symptoms: https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/high-blood-pressure/understanding-blood-pressure-readings/hypertensive-crisis-when-you-should-call-911-for-high-blood-pressure
- NHS: home threshold 135/85, clinic 140/90; South Asian background, salt, stress etc. as risk factors; 150 min activity: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/high-blood-pressure/
- Home measurement steps: https://www.cdc.gov/high-blood-pressure/measure/index.html
- WHO salt <5 g/day (<2,000 mg sodium): https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/salt-reduction
- Indian salt intake 8.0 g/day (men 8.9, women 7.1), NNMS 2017–18, ICMR-NCDIR, Scientific Reports 2023: https://link.springer.com/10.1038/s41598-023-42694-x
- AAMs 1,80,906 functioning and 38.79 crore hypertension screenings to 31 Oct 2025 (MoHFW release, 9 Dec 2025): https://mohfw.gov.in/en/pressrelease/update-ayushman-arogya-mandir-aams
- NP-NCD screening 30+, free medicines and follow-up (Rajya Sabha reply, Aug 2025, via IANS): https://www.newkerala.com/news/o/111-cr-bp-64-lakh-diabetes-cases-diagnosed-treatment-787 ; also 30+ in https://www.lokmattimes.com/health/govt-treats-4201-mn-for-hypertension-2527-mn-for-diabetes-under-75-by-25-initiative
- 75 by 25 launch (17 May 2023): https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1924730

## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Mayo Clinic lifestyle list, Manipal, 1mg, PharmEasy, Redcliffe) give one BP chart (usually the US one) and generic lifestyle tips. None compares WHO/Indian vs US vs European cut-offs, none gives sourced Indian prevalence/control data (NFHS-5, ICMR-INDIAB, WHO), none quantifies Indian salt intake (ICMR-NCDIR 8 g vs WHO 5 g), few give exact home-measurement technique, none mentions free NP-NCD screening/medicines at Ayushman Arogya Mandirs.
