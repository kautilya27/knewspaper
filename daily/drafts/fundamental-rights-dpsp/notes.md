## Information gain vs top results
Top results (GeeksforGeeks, Testbook, BYJU'S, iPleaders, INFLIBNET) are exam-notes lists of differences. They miss: (1) a "DPSP to the law it led to" table with Act years, citing the Statements of Objects and Reasons that name Articles 40 and 47; (2) the 2026 replacement of MGNREGA by the VB-G RAM G Act; (3) the 2024 nine-judge ruling that the original Article 31C still stands; (4) accurate case details from the judgments (Champakam decided under Art 29(2) by seven judges, several sites say 14/15(1) or five judges; Drishti wrongly says Golaknath overturned Champakam).
