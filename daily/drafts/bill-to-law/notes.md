# Notes: how-a-bill-becomes-law-in-india (civics.csv row 13)

## Information gain vs top results
Top results for "how does a bill become a law in India" (Wikipedia, BYJU'S, GKToday, GeeksforGeeks, iPleaders, LiveLaw explainer, sansad.in RS page) all cover the readings, joint sitting and money bills from a textbook angle. What they miss or get out of date:
- The current Governor/assent position: most were written before the 20 Nov 2025 Presidential Reference opinion. Some still describe the April 2025 Tamil Nadu timelines/deemed assent as the law.
- Real data on how often bills are actually scrutinised (PRS: 16% referred to committees in the 17th Lok Sabha, 35% debated under an hour, 58% passed within two weeks).
- A recent worked example of a bill going through a Select Committee, being withdrawn and re-introduced (Income-tax Bill 2025 to Income-tax (No. 2) Bill 2025).
- A single step table plus a three-way comparison table (ordinary / money / amendment).
