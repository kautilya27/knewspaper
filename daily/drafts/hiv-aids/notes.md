# Notes: hiv-aids-symptoms-causes (plan row #60, Health & Fitness)

Research date: 21 Nov 2026.

## Sources per key fact
- 25.61 lakh PLHIV in 2024, from the "India HIV Estimation 2025" report released on World AIDS Day 2025. The figure is reported by Medical Dialogues (https://medicaldialogues.in/amp/mdtv/healthshorts/25-lakh-indians-living-with-hiv-second-highest-globally-report-160006), Medlarge and iasexam (citing The Indian Express). I could not open the NACO report PDF itself. NACO's "HIV Facts & Figures" page still shows the 2019 estimates (23.48 lakh).
- Prevalence 0.20% (2024) vs 0.33% (2010), global 0.7%; new infections 64,500 (2024) vs 1.25 lakh (2010); deaths 32,200 vs 1.73 lakh; 18 lakh+ on free ART by 2025; test and treat; dual HIV/syphilis kit for antenatal screening; Ombudsmen in 34 states/UTs. Source: PIB World AIDS Day 2025 backgrounders https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251130711801.pdf and https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251130711901.pdf
- Free prevention, detection and treatment; 725 ART centres (June 2023); free viral load testing. Source: Lok Sabha Unstarred Q 2555, 4 Aug 2023, https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2501065/1/AU2555.pdf
- ICTCs are in government hospitals (general OPD / O&G) and offer confidential counselling and testing. Source: NACO https://naco.mohfw.gov.in/division/basic-services/hiv-counselling-testing-services/
- Helpline 1097 is toll-free, 24x7, from any mobile or landline. Source: NACO https://naco.mohfw.gov.in/living-with-hiv-aids/hotlines-helplines/
- Symptoms; how HIV does and doesn't spread; advanced HIV defined as CD4 <200 or WHO stage 3/4; undetectable means "will not transmit HIV to sexual partners"; antibodies within 28 days, retest after 28 days. Source: WHO fact sheet https://who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hiv-aids
- Acute illness at 2–6 weeks, symptom list, PEP within 72 hours, about 6 months to become undetectable, long healthy life with treatment, most people don't develop AIDS. Source: NHS https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hiv-and-aids
- Chronic stage lasts about 10 years or more untreated; about 3 years survival with untreated AIDS. Source: NIH HIVinfo https://hivinfo.nih.gov/understanding-hiv/fact-sheets/stages-hiv-infection
- Act commenced 10 Sep 2018 (NACO https://naco.mohfw.gov.in/hiv-aids-p-c-act-2017/). Sections 3, 5, 8 and 37 are from the Act text https://lddashboard.legislative.gov.in/sites/default/files/A2017-16_2.pdf

External links in article: NACO helpline page and WHO fact sheet. Internal links: early-symptoms-of-pregnancy, uti-symptoms-causes, science-backed-ways-boost-immunity.

## Information gain vs top results
Top results (Mayo Clinic, NIH/NICHD, NHS) cover symptoms, stages and transmission for US/UK readers. None of them give India's latest estimates (25.61 lakh PLHIV, 0.20% prevalence, trend table 2010 vs 2024), free ICTC testing and free ART at government centres, the 1097 helpline, or the rights under the HIV and AIDS (Prevention and Control) Act, 2017 (consent, confidentiality, anti-discrimination, Ombudsman, penalty). This article also adds an HIV vs AIDS comparison table and practical window-period/PEP timing in one place.

## Not verified / left out
- The 25.61 lakh figure is confirmed only through secondary news reports of the government report, not the NACO PDF itself. Several outlets agree on it, and it is consistent with the earlier 24.67 lakh (2022) and 25.44 lakh (2023) estimates.
- Number of ICTCs today: the HCTS Guideline 2024 says "more than 4400" stand-alone ICTCs plus 30,000+ screening sites. Left out to avoid confusion.
- PEP availability free at government hospitals in India: no official source found, so the article says to "go to a government hospital or a doctor" without claiming it is free.
- Section 29 (right of residence) left out because I was unsure whether it covers only minors.
- Fourth-generation test window periods (for example 18–45 days) were not used. Only WHO's 28-day antibody statement is used.
- WHO global figure (41.0 million) was not used.
