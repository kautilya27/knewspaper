# Notes: student-resume (plan #49, Career & Jobs)

## Duplicate check
- Existing how-to-make-a-resume (local draft drafts/resume/article.md; the live page could not be fetched because WebFetch permission was not answered) covers: resume vs CV vs biodata, ATS/Naukri, general section order, personal details table, CGPA vs percentage, a general before/after bullet, length, common mistakes.
- This article doesn't repeat those. It links to that guide for format, personal details and CGPA, and covers only the student or fresher case: what replaces work experience, how to write NSS, NCC, fest, hackathon and NPTEL items, campus placement-cell rules (fixed template, proof verification, master resume, marks and backlogs checked separately), a full B.Com before/after, and a student-specific "leave out" list.

## Sources for key facts
- NPTEL score formula (25% assignments + 75% exam), certificate categories (40/60/75/90 cut-offs), minimums of 10/25 and 30/75, online verification: NPTEL course announcements page https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/noc19_ge26/announcements (a 2019 course); the categories and the 25/75 split are confirmed by Careers360 https://www.careers360.com/courses-certifications/articles/nptel-certificate-how-to-download-and-topper-parameters
- NCC B/C preference in Central and State Government jobs; 32 IMA seats per course for C-certificate holders: Jamia Millia Islamia NCC incentives https://jmi.ac.in/NCC/Incentives
- IIT (BHU) placement policy (fixed resume format, unsupported data deleted, wrong or misleading information leads to being debarred for the session): https://placement.iitbhu.ac.in/policies/students
- IIT Madras Students' Guide and Policies, Placements 2023-24 (no point without proof permitted, resume approval before applying, multiple resumes plus a master resume): https://placement.iitm.ac.in/downloads/Students_Guide_&_Policies_Document_Placements_2023-24.pdf
- Smart India Hackathon 2025 run by the MoE Innovation Cell with AICTE, starting 8 December 2025: All India Radio https://www.newsonair.gov.in/smart-india-hackathon-2025-to-be-held-across-india-on-8th-9th-december/ and https://www.newsonair.gov.in/8th-edition-of-smart-india-hackathon-2025-begins-mic

## Information gain vs top results (Internshala, Naukri, Zety, Careers360 and others)
- The top results give generic fresher advice. Internshala doesn't name NSS, NCC or NPTEL and has no campus-template or backlog guidance. Naukri mentions NPTEL only as an example and doesn't cover NSS, NCC or backlogs.
- Ours adds: the NPTEL certificate categories and how to write them on a resume, the value of NCC B and C certificates with a source, how IIT placement cells verify resumes (examples from the actual policies), how marks and backlogs are handled, and an annotated Indian B.Com before/after.
