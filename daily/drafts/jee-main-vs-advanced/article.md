**Quick answer:** JEE Main is the first stage. The National Testing Agency (NTA) holds it twice a year, and anyone who has passed or is taking Class 12 with physics, chemistry and maths can sit it. Its rank leads to NITs, IIITs and other centrally funded colleges. JEE Advanced is the second stage, set by one of the IITs. Only the top 2.5 lakh JEE Main candidates can take it, you get at most two attempts in consecutive years, and it is the only route into the IITs.

So JEE Main decides two things: your NIT-level seat, and whether you may sit JEE Advanced. Your Advanced rank then decides your IIT seat. Both sets of seats are filled through the same counselling, JoSAA, and both also have a Class 12 marks condition.

## Which year these figures are from

NTA released the JEE Main 2027 notice on 24 October 2026, so the JEE Main details below are for 2027. IIT Delhi will organise JEE Advanced 2027, and the exam is on 23 May 2027 (announced on 13 November 2026, along with the 2027 eligibility rules on jeeadv.ac.in). Registration dates and fees are not out yet. The cut-offs, rank-list minimums and seat counts below are from the 2026 cycle, when IIT Roorkee held the exam on 17 May 2026.

## How the two exams are set

### JEE Main 2027 pattern

According to NTA's notice, Paper 1 has 75 questions for 300 marks, split equally between physics, chemistry and maths. Each subject has 20 multiple-choice and 5 numerical-answer questions, with no optional questions. A correct answer earns four marks and a wrong one costs one, numerical questions included. A blank costs nothing.

Because each shift gets a different paper, NTA ranks you on a percentile (NTA score) within your shift. If you take both sessions, only the better score counts. For what a given score means as a percentile and rank, see our guide on [whether 120 is a good score in JEE Main](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/is-120-a-good-score-in-jee-mains).

### JEE Advanced pattern

JEE Advanced has two compulsory three-hour papers on the same day, on computer. It has no fixed pattern. The number of questions, the question types (single-correct, multiple-correct with partial marks, numerical, matching) and the marks per section change from year to year, and candidates see them only on exam day. Timed mocks of both papers back to back help; our comparison of [mock test platforms in India](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/best-mock-test-platform-india) can help you pick one.

To get a rank at all, you must also clear a minimum total and a minimum in each subject. In 2026, IIT Roorkee set these at 92 in total and 8 per subject for the common rank list, 82 and 7 for OBC-NCL and GEN-EWS, and 46 and 4 for SC, ST and PwD.

## Step 1: JEE Main score to JEE Advanced eligibility

The JEE Advanced 2026 brochure admits the top 2,50,000 JEE Main Paper 1 candidates, but not as one list. The places are split by category, with a 5% PwD share inside each:

| Category | Share | Candidates eligible (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Open (common rank list) | 40.5% | 1,01,250 |
| OBC-NCL | 27% | 67,500 |
| SC | 15% | 37,500 |
| GEN-EWS | 10% | 25,000 |
| ST | 7.5% | 18,750 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **2,50,000** |

Because each category fills its own share, the JEE Main percentile you need depends on your category. NTA's 2026 qualifying percentiles for JEE Advanced, released with the Session 2 result, were:

- General: 93.4123549
- GEN-EWS: 82.4164528
- OBC-NCL: 80.9232583
- SC: 63.9172792
- ST: 52.0174712

All five were higher than in 2025, when the General cut-off was 93.1023262. Clearing this line only lets you sit Advanced. It does not decide an IIT seat.

### The other JEE Advanced conditions (2027 rules)

- **Age:** born on or after 1 October 2002 (1 October 1997 for SC, ST and PwD).
- **Class 12 year:** you must have first appeared for Class 12, with physics, chemistry and maths, in 2026 or 2027 (with a narrow exception for some 2025 candidates whose boards declared results late).
- **Attempts:** two, in consecutive years. Skip the year after your first attempt and the second is gone.
- **Earlier IIT admission:** anyone already admitted to an IIT or IISc programme is barred, with narrow exceptions such as not reporting online or withdrawing before the last JoSAA round.

These are the rules IIT Delhi published for 2027 on jeeadv.ac.in; check that page rather than coaching sites, which sometimes still show the 2026 dates.

## Step 2: From rank to seat through JoSAA

The Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) fills both sets of seats on one portal. You fill one list of choices, but different choices are judged on different ranks:

- **IITs:** your JEE Advanced rank (common or category rank).
- **NITs, IIITs and GFTIs:** your JEE Main Paper 1 rank. You do not need to have taken Advanced.

A student who sat both exams can therefore be eligible for an NIT seat on their Main rank and an IIT seat on their Advanced rank in the same round. JoSAA allots the one higher on their choice list. Most NITs also reserve 50% of seats for students from their home state, so a middling Main rank can still win a seat at your state's NIT.

According to JoSAA's 2026 seat matrix, as reported by Careers360, there were 67,323 seats in total:

| Institute type | Admitted through | Seats (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| IITs (23) | JEE Advanced rank | 18,826 |
| IISc Bengaluru | JEE Advanced rank | 125 |
| NITs (31) | JEE Main rank | 25,162 |
| IIITs (26) | JEE Main rank | 11,518 |
| GFTIs | JEE Main rank | 11,692 |

That is 18,826 IIT seats (18,951 with IISc) for about 2.5 lakh eligible candidates, while JEE Main ranks lead to more than twice as many seats.

## Step 3: The Class 12 marks rule (75% or top 20 percentile)

A rank alone does not secure a seat at an IIT or an NIT-level college. You also need one of these:

- at least **75%** in Class 12 (**65%** for SC, ST and PwD candidates), or
- a place in the **top 20 percentile** of your board's results that year, in your category.

The rule applies at admission, not at the exam, so a student with 70% can still sit both exams and get a rank. In 2026, IIT Roorkee let candidates who did not yet meet it take part in JoSAA, but the seat was confirmed only if a revised marksheet (after re-evaluation, a supplementary or an improvement exam) reached it by 15 July 2026. ## Preparing for both

JEE Main rewards speed and accuracy; JEE Advanced tests how deeply you can apply a concept. Many students prepare for Main up to the January session, then shift to Advanced-style problems while using the April session to improve their Main score. Before applying, confirm dates and rules on [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in) for JEE Main and on [jeeadv.ac.in](https://jeeadv.ac.in) for JEE Advanced.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get into an NIT without JEE Advanced?

Yes. NIT, IIIT and GFTI seats in JoSAA are allotted on your JEE Main rank alone, as long as you also meet the 75% or top-20-percentile Class 12 condition. JEE Advanced is needed only for the IITs.

### How many attempts are allowed in JEE Main and JEE Advanced?

For JEE Main 2027, you can apply if you passed Class 12 in 2025 or 2026 or are appearing in 2027, and you can take both sessions each year. JEE Advanced allows only two attempts, in two consecutive years.

### What JEE Main percentile do I need to qualify for JEE Advanced?

It depends on your category, because the 2.5 lakh places are split by reservation. In 2026 the cut-off was 93.41 percentile for General, 82.42 for GEN-EWS, 80.92 for OBC-NCL, 63.92 for SC and 52.02 for ST.

### Which IIT is conducting JEE Advanced 2027?

IIT Delhi. It was announced in November 2026 that JEE Advanced 2027 will be held on 23 May 2027. IIT Roorkee conducted the 2026 exam. Watch jeeadv.ac.in for registration dates and the full brochure.
