**Quick answer:** You can use Claude for free by signing up at **claude.ai** or in the Claude app for Android, iPhone, Mac or Windows. The Free plan costs ₹0, needs no card, and works in India. You need to be at least 18 and verify a mobile number. The catch is the usage limit: Anthropic gives free users a limited amount of use that resets five hours after your first message, plus a weekly cap. The most advanced models and some features need a paid plan.

That's the only official free route for ordinary users. "Free Claude Pro" apps, shared logins and cracked tools are not from Anthropic and can put your account and data at risk. Below is what you actually get, the limits, and when paying makes sense, based on Anthropic's own pricing and Help Center pages as of November 2026.

## How to sign up for Claude Free (step by step)

1. Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) in your browser, or install the official Claude app from the Play Store or App Store (publisher: Anthropic).
2. Sign up with your email address or Google account.
3. **Verify your phone number.** Anthropic's Help Center says phone verification is required for all new users. VoIP numbers, Google Voice numbers, numbers made with apps, and landlines are not accepted, so use your normal Jio, Airtel, Vi or BSNL mobile number. One number can be used for at most three accounts, and it can't be changed after verification.
4. Confirm you are 18 or older. Anthropic's terms require users to be at least 18.
5. Start chatting. You're on the Free plan by default. Ignore the upgrade prompt if you don't want to pay.

India is on Anthropic's list of supported countries, and you must be physically in a supported location to use the service.

## What the Free plan includes

According to Anthropic's pricing page, the Free plan includes:

- **Chat on the web, desktop and mobile apps**, with your history synced across devices
- **Web search**, so answers can draw on current pages
- **Code execution and file creation**, for example turning a table into a spreadsheet or running a quick calculation
- **Memory**, which Anthropic opened to all users, including free users, in March 2026
- **Artifacts**, the side panel for documents, code and small apps, available on every plan including Free since September 2026
- **App connectors**, which link Claude to some other services you use
- **Projects:** up to five, for keeping files and instructions for one task together
- **Claude Design, Slides and Docs**, for making designs, decks and documents inside a chat
- **Models:** Claude Sonnet and Claude Haiku

What Free does **not** include, per the same page: Research, Claude Code, Claude in Chrome and Microsoft 365, scheduled tasks, more than five Projects, and the larger Opus and Fable models. Anthropic's Help Center states plainly that Fable models aren't available on the Free plan.

## The free usage limits, explained

This is the part most guides get wrong. Anthropic doesn't publish a fixed number of free messages. Its Help Center says:

- Free use has a **session-based limit that resets every five hours**. The five-hour window starts when you send your first message.
- How quickly you use it up depends on **the length and complexity of your conversations, the features you use and the model you choose**. A long chat with big files uses up your allowance much faster than short questions.
- The pricing page also mentions a **weekly** limit for Free.
- Claude notifies you when you've reached the limit, and you can see how much you've used and the time left in **Settings > Usage**. Anthropic says it may adjust usage limits at its discretion.

So "how many messages do I get free?" has no fixed answer. Ten short questions might cost less than one long conversation about a 50-page PDF.

### How to make the free limit last longer

- **Start a new chat for a new topic.** Long threads carry the whole conversation along, so every new message costs more.
- **Share only the part of a document you need**, not the whole file.
- **Ask clearly in one message** instead of many small follow-ups. Say what you want, for whom, and in what format.
- **Plan around the reset.** If you hit the limit at 2 pm, the next window opens five hours after the first message of that session, not at midnight.

### Is there a free student plan in India?

Not for individuals. Anthropic's Claude for Education is sold to universities, and students get access only if their university has a deal and they sign in with the university email. Check with your college IT or library team. There's no official individual student discount.

### Other official free offers

- **Claude for Open Source:** Anthropic's Claude for Open Source page offers six months of free Claude Ultra to open-source maintainers and contributors who meet its eligibility criteria, such as maintaining a widely used project. Most users won't qualify.
- **The Claude API** (for developers building apps) is billed separately by usage. We couldn't find any current official free sign-up credit, so don't rely on claims of one.

## Is the free plan enough for you?

The Free plan works well if you:

- ask a few questions a day, for studies, emails or quick research
- want help drafting, summarising or explaining something
- are trying Claude before deciding whether to pay

Paying starts to make sense if you:

- regularly hit the five-hour limit in the middle of work
- need more than five Projects, or the Research feature, for long assignments or clients
- write code and want Claude Code
- need the larger Opus or Fable models for harder reasoning tasks

A sensible approach: use Free for two weeks and note how often you hit the limit. If it's rare, stay free. If it happens most days, Pro for one month is a low-risk test.

## Stay safe: what to avoid

- **Fake "Claude Pro free" APKs and websites.** Use only claude.ai and the official apps from Anthropic.
- **Shared or bought accounts.** They break Anthropic's rules, can be shut down, and let strangers see your chats.
- **Pasting sensitive data.** Don't share Aadhaar, PAN, bank or OTP details in any AI chat.
- **Trusting answers blindly.** Like any AI assistant, Claude can make mistakes. Double-check facts, figures and anything legal, medical or financial.
