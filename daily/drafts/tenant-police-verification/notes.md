## Information gain vs top results
Top results (NoBroker blog and guides, Kotak, Square Yards, Digit, Housing.com) are mostly single-city guides or generic lists. They cite repealed IPC 188 or give BNS 223 penalties without the conditions (Kotak). Fees are missing or unsourced (Housing.com: Delhi ₹250, Gurgaon ₹500, both not confirmed officially). Our article adds a verified multi-state table (portal, fee only where official, mandatory basis), explains the Section 163 BNSS order mechanism and the two-tier BNS 223 penalty correctly, adds UP's ₹50 fee and ~8-day timeline, Mumbai's no-NOC/free rule, the Pune i-SARITA/CCTNS link, and a tenant checklist.
