**Quick answer:** The Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) has issued the 2027 holiday list in Office Memorandum No. 12/2/2023-JCA, dated 16 July 2026. Central government offices in Delhi and New Delhi get **17 gazetted holidays** in 2027, from Republic Day on Tuesday, 26 January to Christmas on Saturday, 25 December. On top of these, each employee can take **any 2 restricted holidays** from a list of 32.

There is a catch. Six of the 17 holidays fall on a Saturday or Sunday (Independence Day and Milad-un-Nabi both land on Sunday, 15 August), so a five-day-week office gets only **11 weekday holidays**. The good news: four holidays fall on a Friday or Monday, and a few well-chosen leave days turn several more into long breaks. The full list and a planner are below.

## How the 2027 list is put together: 14 + 3 + 2

The OM works on a simple formula that many holiday-list pages skip.

**14 compulsory holidays.** Every central government administrative office in India must observe these: Republic Day, Independence Day, Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday, Buddha Purnima, Christmas Day, Dussehra (Vijaya Dashami), Diwali, Good Friday, Guru Nanak's Birthday, Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Mahavir Jayanti, Muharram and Prophet Mohammad's Birthday (Id-e-Milad).

**3 more chosen from a list of 12.** The optional list is: an additional day for Dussehra, Holi, Janmashtami, Ram Navami, Maha Shivratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Makar Sankranti, Rath Yatra, Onam, Pongal, Sri Panchami/Basant Panchami, and one combined slot for Vishu, Vaisakhi, Vaisakhadi, Bahag Bihu, Ugadi, Chaitra Sukladi, Cheti Chand, Gudi Padwa, 1st Navratra, Navroz, Chhath Puja and Karwa Chauth. Delhi's list has [Holi](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/why-holi-is-celebrated), Ram Navami and Janmashtami as its three, which is how it reaches 17.

**2 restricted holidays per employee.** In addition, each employee may take any two holidays from the restricted list. The office stays open; you simply apply for the RH like a day of leave.

The OM also says no substitute holiday is given when a holiday falls on a weekly off or when two festivals fall on the same day. That is why 15 August 2027, a Sunday carrying both Independence Day and Milad-un-Nabi, gives no extra day off.

## Working outside Delhi? Your list may be different

The 17-holiday list and the 32-day RH list above are for offices in Delhi and New Delhi. For offices elsewhere, the 14 compulsory holidays stay the same, but the **Central Government Employees Welfare Coordination Committee in each state capital** picks the other three from the list of 12. That is why a central office in Chennai may close for Pongal while one in Kolkata closes for an extra Dussehra day.

These committees can also prepare their own RH list with occasions of local importance, and the 9 optional festivals not picked as holidays must be included in that RH list. A Department of Posts letter of September 2026 adds that the festivals and dates in the OM cannot be changed, and that the final state list applies to all central offices in that state. Some committees, such as the one in Dehradun, have already released their 2027 lists, so check the one your office has circulated.

Two other points from the OM:

- If a state government declares Naraka Chaturdasi (Thursday, 28 October) as its main Diwali holiday, central offices in that state may close on that day instead of 29 October. Read more on the festival in our piece on [why Diwali is celebrated](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/why-diwali-is-celebrated).
- Central government industrial, commercial and trading units can fix their own list of up to 16 holidays, but Republic Day, Independence Day and Gandhi Jayanti must be among them.

The single best deal is the Holi–Good Friday stretch: 9 days off for one RH and two leave days. The Ram Navami–Mahavir Jayanti gap gives 5 days for a single leave day. Remember you get only two RHs in the whole year, so pick the pair that suits you best. Leave still needs sanction from your office, so apply early for the popular weeks.

## Do banks follow this list?

No. Bank holidays come under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, and are notified state by state; the Reserve Bank of India publishes them for each state on its website. The OM itself says banks follow the instructions of the Department of Financial Services. So a bank branch near your office may be open on a day your office is shut, or closed on a day you are working.

You can read the full DoPT order, as circulated by the Department of Posts, in this [official copy of the 2027 holiday OM](https://utilities.cept.gov.in/dop/pdfbind.ashx?id=15435). DoPT also posts it under Orders and Circulars on [dopt.gov.in](https://dopt.gov.in).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many gazetted holidays are there for central government employees in 2027?

Offices in Delhi and New Delhi have 17 gazetted holidays in 2027, but six of them fall on a Saturday or Sunday. That leaves 11 weekday holidays for a five-day-week office.

### How many restricted holidays can a central government employee take in 2027?

Each employee can take any two restricted holidays in the year. For Delhi offices they are picked from the 32-day list in Annexure II of the DoPT OM; offices outside Delhi use the RH list of their state-capital committee.

### Is Diwali 2027 a holiday for central government offices on 28 or 29 October?

The OM lists Diwali on Friday, 29 October 2027. Where a state government has declared Naraka Chaturdasi (28 October) as its Diwali holiday, central offices in that state may observe it on that day instead.

### Does the central government holiday list apply to private companies?

No. The DoPT list covers central government administrative offices only. Private employers follow their state's Shops and Establishments Act (and, in some states, a separate National and Festival Holidays Act) and their own holiday policy, though many use the national holidays as a base.
