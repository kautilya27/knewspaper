New here: one table naming the exact clause/notice for each exam (JEE Main 3.2/5.8.1, JEE Advanced clauses 25-26, CUET 3.2, NMC 2025 notice, AICTE Sept 2025 circular, 1990 resolution, AIU 1991), honest "not named" for UPSC/SSC/foreign, the NIOS "NR" top 20 percentile issue with the CBSE fallback rule, the first-appearance rule for students who switch to NIOS, and streams 1-4 / ODES from the 2026-27 prospectus.
