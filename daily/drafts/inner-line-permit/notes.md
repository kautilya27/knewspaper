## Information gain
Top results (Wikipedia, GKToday/ForumIAS explainers, travel blogs) either explain the ILP concept for exam prep or cover one state. The comparable Bikat page could not be fetched (permission prompt timed out), so it was not reviewed directly. Most pages miss the December 2024 PAP reimposition in Manipur/Mizoram/Nagaland, e-FRRO-only permits (Sikkim January 2026), Andaman's day-visit exemption to 2027, Lakshadweep's 2026 easing, Meghalaya's proposed "virtual ILP", and current portal fees. The draft adds one table covering 10 states/UTs with the portal, fee, validity and foreigner rules.

- Arunachal eILP fee comes from a Feb 2024 MyGov post, not the live portal. It is labelled with its source.
- Manipur ILP fee could not be found on the portal pages, so the draft says the fee is shown when you apply.

- Ladakh fee amounts are 2021 figures (labelled). The wildlife fee amount is not stated.
