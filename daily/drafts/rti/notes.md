- RTI Act text (s.2(f), 5(2), 6(2), 6(3), 7(1) 30 days/48 hours, 7(2) deemed refusal, 7(6) free if late, 8(1)(a)-(j), 8(2), 8(3), 18, 19(1)/(3)/(6), 20 penalty ₹250/day max ₹25,000, 24 proviso corruption/human rights 45 days): PRS copy of the Act, https://PRSINDIA.org/files/bills_acts/acts_parliament/2005/the-right-to-information-act-2005.pdf

- Portal scope (central only; state/Delhi applications returned without refund), payment modes (net banking, Visa/Mastercard, RuPay, UPI), first appeal online, link to CIC second-appeal portal: https://rtionline.gov.in/guidelines.php?request and https://rtionline.gov.in/request/request.php
- Portal FAQ (no fee for first appeal, BPL certificate, payment reconciliation 24-48 working hours, online first appeal only for online requests, dsscic.nic.in): https://rtionline.gov.in/faq.php?pageid=
- 3,000-character box, PDF attachment up to 1 MB: portal user manual copy at https://www.cgtmse.in/files/User_Manual_for_RTI_Application.pdf (manual may be older; not shown on current portal pages)
- Pravasi Legal Cell v Union of India, SC order 20 Mar 2023 (online portals for states/UTs and high courts): https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2019/18483/18483_2019_1_21_42868_Order_20-Mar-2023.pdf
- 2019 amendment, government's reason (EC constitutional vs IC statutory, "anomaly"), critics incl. former CICs Sridhar Acharyulu and Yashovardhan Azad: https://livelaw.in/news-updates/rti-amendment-bill-passed-by-parliament-146671
- 2019 Rules: 3-year term, ₹2.5 lakh / ₹2.25 lakh, notified 25 Oct 2019: https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/govt-notifies-rti-rules-cic-tenure-cut-to-3-yrs-852443
