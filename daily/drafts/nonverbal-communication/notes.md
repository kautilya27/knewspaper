## Information gain vs top results
Top results (HelpGuide, Lumen/open textbooks, YourDictionary, QuillBot, BetterHelp) list the types (face, posture, gestures, eye contact, touch, space, voice) and give general tips. HelpGuide mentions culture only via the "OK" sign and does not mention India, Mehrabian, lie detection research or power posing. Our gain: what Mehrabian's studies actually measured (with his own caveat); a types table with Indian examples; sourced Indian cues (head wobble, namaste/handshake, charan sparsh, feet, pointing, indirect disagreement); cross-national personal-space data including India's participation; research verdicts on lie-spotting (54%) and power posing; video-interview advice tied to the Bailenson paper.
