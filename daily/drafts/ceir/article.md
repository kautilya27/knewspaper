**Quick answer:** File a police complaint (an FIR or an online lost report), get a duplicate SIM for your lost number, then fill the "Block Your Lost/Stolen Mobile" form on [Sanchar Saathi](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/Home/ceir-services.jsp) or the Sanchar Saathi app. You verify with an OTP on the re-issued SIM and receive an 18-digit request ID. The phone's IMEI is then blocked on every Indian network, and if anyone uses it with a SIM, the details go to the police for tracing. Keep the request ID: you need it to unblock the phone if you get it back.

CEIR (Central Equipment Identity Register) is the Department of Telecommunications' (DoT) national IMEI database. It will not show your phone on a map. It makes the handset useless on Indian networks and helps the police find it. The steps below follow DoT's CEIR user manual and the live request form.

## Before you start: what you need

The block form on Sanchar Saathi lists these prerequisites:

- **Police complaint copy:** an FIR or a lost report ("FIR/LR" in the manual). The complaint number and an upload of the complaint are both mandatory. Many state police forces, such as Delhi Police, let you file a lost report online. The process varies by state.
- **A re-issued (duplicate) SIM for the lost number.** The OTP is sent to this number, and the form warns that SMS must be working on the new SIM. Under DoT rules, SMS on a re-issued SIM starts only 24 hours after activation, so the OTP may not arrive before then. Ask your operator to block the old SIM and issue a replacement first; this also stops misuse of your number for OTPs. Comparing operators? See our guide to the [best SIM network in India](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/what-is-the-best-sim-network-in-india).
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar (the form currently asks for a masked copy that shows only the last four digits), PAN, Voter ID, driving licence or another ID, uploaded as a file.
- **IMEI number(s), if available.** Dual-SIM phones have two IMEIs, and the form has fields for IMEI 1 and IMEI 2. Enter both so that both slots are blocked. You will find them on the phone box, on the purchase invoice, or (for your next phone) by dialling *#06#.
- **Purchase invoice:** optional, but upload it if you have it.

The form also asks for the brand, model, approximate price, and the place, date, state, district and police station where you lost the phone.

## Step by step: from police complaint to CEIR block

| Step | What you do | Where | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Report the loss or theft to the police (FIR or lost report) | Police station or state police website/app | Complaint number and copy |
| 2 | Block the old SIM and get a duplicate SIM for the same number | Your telecom operator's store or app | Working SIM for the OTP |
| 3 | Open "Block Your Lost/Stolen Mobile" | sancharsaathi.gov.in or the Sanchar Saathi app | The request form |
| 4 | Fill in device, loss and owner details; upload the complaint and ID | Same form | — |
| 5 | Enter the captcha, get the OTP on the re-issued number, verify, accept the declaration and submit | Same form | **18-digit request ID** |
| 6 | Check progress with "Check Request Status" | Sanchar Saathi / CEIR | Status of your request |

A successful block shows "Blocking Request is successfully processed", meaning the IMEI has gone to all operators for blocking. DoT's manual gives no fixed time for it to take effect.

Two points on the form are easy to miss:

- **A new phone that was never used with a SIM** cannot be blocked through the regular form. The form tells you to contact the CEIR District Nodal police officer with a copy of the FIR or police complaint that mentions the IMEI number(s).
- **If the police filed the block themselves** through ZIPNET, CCTNS or a state police portal, the manual says you cannot check its status on the CEIR portal. Ask the police station instead.

Lost your request ID? Use "Forgot Request ID", enter the mobile number you used for blocking, and verify the OTP. The portal then lists every request made from that number.

## What CEIR can and cannot do

| CEIR can | CEIR cannot |
|---|---|
| Block the phone's IMEI(s) on all Indian telecom networks | Show you the phone's live location on a map |
| Record the details of anyone who puts a SIM in the blocked phone and share them with the police for tracing | Recover the phone for you. The police do the tracing and recovery |
| Tell a buyer that a phone is blocked when they check its IMEI | Protect data already on the phone. An IMEI block acts on mobile networks, not on the device itself |
| Let you unblock the phone with your request ID after recovery | Block a phone lost before CEIR launched in your state. The form currently accepts only losses on or after your State/UT's launch date, which it lists |

A blocked phone still holds your photos, chats and logins, so change key passwords and alert your bank. Our guide on [protecting your data from cyberattacks](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/how-to-protect-your-data-from-cyberattacks) covers the basics.

## How to unblock your phone after you get it back

If the police return your phone or you find it yourself, unblock it so that it works on mobile networks again:

1. Open "Un-Block Found Mobile" on Sanchar Saathi (or in the app).
2. Enter the **request ID**, the mobile number for the OTP, and the reason for unblocking. Then enter the captcha and tap "Get OTP".
3. Verify the OTP, submit, and choose "Click to unblock".
4. Check "Check Request Status". It should read "Un-Blocking Request is successfully processed".

The request then goes to the operators, and the phone works again once they remove the block on their networks.

## Buying a second-hand phone? Check the IMEI first

Before you pay for a used phone, check that it is not blocked:

- **SMS:** send **KYM** followed by the 15-digit IMEI, with no space (for example, KYM123456789012345), to **14422**.
- **App or website:** use "Know Genuineness of Your Mobile Handset" (Know Your Mobile) in the Sanchar Saathi app or on the portal.

A valid phone shows its brand and model; a blocked or invalid one says so. Dial *#06# on the phone and make sure the IMEI matches the box. A blocked handset will not work on Indian networks, and only the person who blocked it (using the request ID) or the police can get it unblocked.

## Other Sanchar Saathi tools worth using

- **Know mobile connections in your name (TAFCOP):** see every SIM registered against your ID, and report ones that aren't yours.
- **Chakshu:** report suspected fraud calls, SMS or WhatsApp messages, such as fake KYC-update scams.

According to a communications ministry release of 5 October 2026 (reported by DD News), Sanchar Saathi has blocked 60.96 lakh lost or stolen handsets, traced 37.85 lakh and helped recover over 14 lakh.

## Google Find Hub and Apple Find My: use them alongside CEIR

CEIR works on the network side. Your phone maker's tools work on the device itself, and they only help if they were switched on before the loss:

- **Android (Find Hub, earlier called Find My Device):** play a sound, see the estimated or last known location, mark the phone as lost (lock it and show a contact number), or erase it. To lock or erase it remotely, the phone needs to be on, signed in to a Google account and connected to mobile data or Wi-Fi.
- **iPhone (Find My):** see the location, Mark as Lost (which locks the phone with its passcode), or erase it remotely. Apple advises going to the police rather than recovering a stolen phone yourself. Don't remove the phone from Find My, because that also removes Activation Lock.

Use these first to mark the phone as lost and lock it. Erase it only as a last resort: an erased Android phone disappears from Find Hub, though an iPhone on iOS 15 or later can still be located in Find My after erasing. Then file the police complaint and the CEIR block.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I block my lost phone on CEIR without an FIR?

No. The form requires a police complaint number and an uploaded copy. Many states accept an online lost report for this, so check your state police website.

### Will CEIR tell me where my phone is?

No. CEIR blocks the IMEI and alerts the police when the phone is used with a SIM. Tracing and recovery are done by the police, and the location is not shown to you.

### How do I unblock my phone if I lost the request ID?

Use "Forgot Request ID" on Sanchar Saathi with the mobile number you used for blocking, and verify the OTP. Then use that ID in "Un-Block Found Mobile".

### Do I need to block both IMEI numbers on a dual-SIM phone?

Yes. Enter IMEI 1 and IMEI 2 on the form so that neither SIM slot can be used on Indian networks.
