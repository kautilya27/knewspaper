**Quick answer:** You can make a good resume in Canva for free. Pick a plain, single-column template, type your details into the existing text boxes (don't paste text as images), keep normal section headings, and download it as **PDF Standard**. Then open the PDF, select all the text and paste it into Notepad: if everything shows up in the right order, software and recruiters can read it.

Canva Pro, which Canva's India pricing page lists at ₹4,000 a year for one person, is not needed for a resume. Where Canva is the wrong tool: campus placement portals with their own rules, government recruitment forms, and any portal that asks for a Word file.

This guide covers only the Canva part. For what to write in each section, see our guide on [how to make a resume](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/how-to-make-a-resume).

## Making a resume in Canva, step by step

1. **Sign in** at canva.com or in the Canva app. A free account is enough.
2. **Search "resume"** on the home page and open the Resumes template category. Canva's help centre says it has hundreds of resume templates. Some are premium designs that need Canva Pro.
3. **Filter before you fall in love with a design.** Look for one column, black or dark-grey text on a white background, and no photo frame. (Below you'll find a checklist.)
4. **Check the page size.** Indian employers and campus cells normally expect A4. Some templates are US Letter size, which is slightly wider and shorter. If you can't tell, start a new design with a custom size of 210 × 297 mm and rebuild the template there. That works on the free plan.
5. **Replace the sample text** by clicking into each text box and typing.
6. **Delete what you don't need**: skill-level bars, star ratings, decorative icons and the photo placeholder. Software can't read rating bars.
7. **Download**: click **Share → Download** and choose **PDF Standard**. Use a clear file name like *Priya-Sharma-Resume.pdf*, not *Blue Minimalist Modern Resume.pdf*.
8. **Test the file** with the copy-paste check in the next section before you upload it anywhere.

### PDF Standard or PDF Print?

Canva's help centre describes **PDF Standard** as meant for on-screen use (96 dpi) and **PDF Print** as meant for printing, at 300 dpi, with options to add bleed and crop marks. A resume you upload or email should be PDF Standard. You don't need bleed or crop marks on an uploaded file, and PDF Print also makes the file bigger.

## How to pick a Canva template that software can read

Many companies use software, called an applicant tracking system (ATS), to store, search and sort applications before a person reads them. Fancy layouts can scramble that reading. You may have seen claims that "75% of resumes are rejected by ATS". There is no reliable study behind that number, so don't design your resume out of fear of it. The real risk is simpler: if your text comes out jumbled or missing, the recruiter's system shows them a jumbled or empty profile.

Use this checklist while browsing templates:

| Choose a template with | Avoid templates with |
|---|---|
| One column, top to bottom | Two or three columns with a coloured sidebar |
| Real text you can click and edit | Headings or names that are part of an image |
| Plain headings: Education, Experience, Skills, Projects | Creative headings like "My Journey" or "What I Bring" |
| Common, plain fonts for body text | Script or display fonts for body text |
| Contact details typed in the body | Phone and email placed inside icons or the page header graphic |
| Bullet points made with text | Skill bars, pie charts, star ratings |
| Little or no colour | Text on dark or patterned backgrounds |

Two-column templates are a common cause of mixed-up text: software may read across both columns line by line and merge your skills into your job history.

### The 30-second copy-paste test

Open the downloaded PDF in your browser or a PDF reader, press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + C, and paste into Notepad (or the Notes app on a phone). Check three things:

- **Is anything missing?** If your name or a heading doesn't paste, it's probably an image or a graphic. Retype it as a text box.
- **Is the order right?** Each section's text should appear together, top to bottom.
- **Are there odd characters?** Some decorative fonts paste as boxes or symbols. Switch to a standard font.

## Using Magic Write for resume text, carefully

Magic Write is Canva's built-in AI writing assistant. According to Canva's feature page, you select some text and it can rewrite, shorten, expand or polish it, or change the tone to make it more formal. It works on the free plan with usage limits. Pro raises those limits (Canva says about 10 times more AI use than Free).

Where it helps:

- **Shortening** a long bullet point to one line.
- **Making the tone consistent** across bullets written at different times.

Where to be careful:

- Canva notes that Magic Write's knowledge runs only up to mid-2021, so don't let it describe current tools, courses or exam names.
- AI text tends to add phrases like "results-driven professional" and claims you can't back up. Every number on your resume (marks, percentages, project results) must come from you. An interviewer will ask about it.
- Rewrite its output in your own words, using strong, specific verbs. Our list of [resume action verbs](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/resume-action-verbs) helps here.

## Uploading your Canva resume to Naukri and LinkedIn

| Portal | Accepted formats | Size limit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Naukri | PDF, RTF, DOC, DOCX | Under 2 MB | Naukri's profile FAQ |
| LinkedIn (Easy Apply) | Word or PDF | Under 2 MB | As reported by Business Insider India |

A text-only Canva PDF is usually far below 2 MB. If yours is bigger, the usual cause is a large photo or a high-resolution background image. Remove it rather than compressing the whole file with an online tool, which can turn your text into an image and fail the copy-paste test.

### When a portal or employer wants a Word file

Canva can't turn an ordinary resume design into a Word file. Its help centre says DOCX downloads are supported only for **Canva Docs**, not other design types, and that downloaded DOCX files may look different in Word. So if a company specifically asks for .doc or .docx, either build the resume in a Canva Doc from the start (plain text, simple headings), or write it in Word or Google Docs.

## When not to use Canva at all

- **Campus placement portals with their own rules.** Many college placement cells decide the format. For example, IIT Madras's internship resume guidelines for 2025–26 allow no pictures and no logo other than the institute's, leave out personal contact details, ask for set wording for some entries, and have the academic section check CGPA. A designed Canva layout adds nothing there. Use your college's format. If you're a fresher building your first one, our [student resume](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/student-resume) guide covers the content.
- **Government recruitment.** UPSC, SSC and state commissions don't ask for a designed resume. They use their own online application forms. UPSC, for instance, moved in May 2025 to a new portal at upsconline.nic.in, according to [All India Radio News](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/upsc-launches-new-online-application-portal). You fill in fields and upload documents, so no resume design is involved.
- **PSU, university or research posts** whose advertisement gives its own CV or biodata format. Follow that format exactly.

Canva is a fine choice for private-sector applications, emailing HR directly, freelance profiles, and creative roles where the look of your resume is part of the job. Even then, keep a plain version for portals and send the designed one by email or as a portfolio link.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Canva free for making a resume?

Yes. The free plan includes many resume templates, standard fonts, editing and PDF download. Premium templates need Canva Pro, which Canva lists at ₹4,000 a year in India.

### Are Canva resumes ATS-friendly?

They can be if you choose a single-column template with real text, standard headings and no skill bars, and download it as PDF Standard. Check it by copying all the text from the PDF into Notepad. If it pastes in the right order, the software can read it.

### Can I download a Canva resume as a Word file?

Only if you made it as a Canva Doc. According to Canva's help centre, DOCX download isn't supported for other design types, so a normal resume template downloads as PDF or an image only.

### Should I put a photo on a Canva resume for Indian jobs?

Most private-sector roles don't need one, and many campus cells, including IIT Madras for internships, don't allow pictures. Add a photo only if the employer asks for it.
