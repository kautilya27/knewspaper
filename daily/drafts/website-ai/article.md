**Quick answer:** To create a website with AI, decide what the site must do, pick either an AI website builder (Wix, Hostinger, Squarespace, Framer, Durable) or an AI coding assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable, Bolt, Replit), describe your business in a prompt, then edit the draft, connect a domain and check the content, SEO, accessibility and privacy page yourself before you publish.

Builders suit a shop, clinic, tutor or small business, because hosting, forms and payments come built in. Coding assistants give more freedom and can cost less for a simple site, but you handle hosting and fixes. Either way, AI gives you a draft in minutes; getting it right is still your job.

## Step 1: Decide what the site must do

Write three lines before you open any tool:

- **Who visits and why** (for example, "parents in Pune looking for maths tuition for Class 8–10").
- **The one action you want** (call, WhatsApp, book a slot, buy, fill a form).
- **The pages you need** (Home, About, Services or Products, Contact, Privacy Policy).

This brief becomes your prompt. Add your city, prices, timings and tone; a vague prompt gets a generic site.

## Step 3: Generate, then rewrite the draft

Paste your brief into the tool. Then go page by page:

1. **Replace every placeholder.** AI fills gaps with invented details: fake testimonials, a made-up founding year, "award-winning" claims, prices you never gave it. Delete anything you cannot prove.
2. **Fix local facts.** Check your address, PIN code, phone number, WhatsApp link, timings and Google Maps link.
3. **Keep the language natural.** If your customers search in Hindi, Tamil or Marathi, add a page in that language and have a native speaker check it.

## Step 4: Get a domain and sort out hosting

A **domain** is your address (yourname.in). **Hosting** is where the site's files live.

- **Builders** (Wix, Hostinger, Squarespace, Framer, Durable) include hosting. On the free plans (Hostinger and Squarespace offer trials instead) you get their subdomain; a custom domain needs a paid plan.
- **Coding assistants** give you files or an app. A plain HTML site can go on a free static host. GitHub's documentation says GitHub Pages hosts static sites for free from public repositories, and it supports custom domains.

**Buying a .in domain:** the .in registry is run by NIXI (National Internet Exchange of India). According to the [.IN Registry FAQs](https://registry.in/faqs), you register through an accredited registrar, the registrar sets the retail price, and you can register for up to 10 years. Indian-language domains under .भारत are also available in many scripts, including Hindi, Tamil, Bengali and Urdu. Compare the renewal price, not just the first-year offer, and keep the domain in your own name and email, not your developer's.

## Step 5: Check what AI still gets wrong

This is the step most "how to make a website with AI" guides skip. Run each check before you publish.

### Content accuracy

Google's [guidance on generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) says it is "critical to manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content", and that includes page titles, meta descriptions, structured data and image alt text. It also warns that mass-producing low-value pages with AI can break its spam policy on scaled content abuse.

### SEO basics

- Each page should have a clear title with your service and city ("Maths tuition in Kothrud, Pune").
- One main heading per page, short URLs, and a working sitemap (most builders make one).
- Connect Google Search Console once the site is live, so you can see whether Google is indexing it.

For promoting the site after launch, see our explainer on [what digital marketing is and how to start it](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/what-is-digital-marketing-and-how-to-start-it).

### Accessibility

AI-made templates often have low-contrast text, missing alt text, buttons with no label and forms that don't work with a keyboard. Run the free Lighthouse audit in Chrome's developer tools, which scores accessibility, SEO and performance, and fix what it flags. Then open the site on a budget Android phone over mobile data.

### Privacy policy and India's DPDP Act

If your site collects names, phone numbers or emails through a contact form, booking or checkout, you are handling personal data. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules (notified on 14 November 2025), businesses get an 18-month phase-in, so the main duties are due to apply from about May 2027. From then, a business must give a clear, standalone notice saying what data it collects and why, and take consent. Building this in now saves a rewrite later. The Press Information Bureau's summary lists penalties of up to ₹250 crore for failing to protect personal data.

AI tools will write a privacy policy, but it is usually generic, often US- or EU-style text that doesn't match what your site does. Use it only as a starting point:

- List exactly what your forms collect and which tools receive it (WhatsApp, Google Sheets, payment gateway, analytics).
- Say how people can ask for their data to be corrected or deleted, and give a contact.
- For a shop, add clear refund, shipping and terms pages too.
- For anything beyond a simple contact form, get a lawyer to review it.

## Launch checklist

- Every phone number, address, price and claim checked by you
- No fake reviews, awards or client logos
- Custom domain connected, SSL padlock showing
- Contact form tested (send yourself a message)
- Lighthouse run on mobile; contrast and alt text fixed
- Privacy policy that matches your real forms and tools
- Search Console connected and sitemap submitted
- Domain and plan renewal dates in your calendar

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I create a website with AI for free?

Yes, with limits. Wix, Framer, Durable, Lovable and Bolt have free plans, but your site sits on their subdomain with their branding, and a custom domain needs a paid plan. A static site written with a free AI assistant can be hosted free on GitHub Pages with your own domain, which you still have to buy.

### Which is better for beginners, an AI builder or ChatGPT/Claude?

For most small businesses an AI website builder is easier, because hosting, forms, payments and editing are built in. Chat assistants suit people happy to handle files and hosting, or anyone who wants to learn how websites work.

### How much does a .in domain cost?

NIXI, which runs the .in registry, leaves retail pricing to accredited registrars, so the price differs from one registrar to another. Compare the renewal price as well as the first-year offer.

### Does Google penalise websites made with AI?

Not for using AI as such. Google's guidance says AI content must meet the same quality standards as any other, should be fact-checked, and must not be mass-produced with little value, which can count as scaled content abuse.
