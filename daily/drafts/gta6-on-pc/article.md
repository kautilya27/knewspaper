## Option 1: Stream from your PS5 with PS Remote Play

Rockstar said on 10 November that GTA 6 will "fully support Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox", as reported by Dexerto. Remote Play doesn't run the game on your PC: the PS5 at home does the work and sends the picture to your laptop, which sends your button presses back.

According to [Sony's PS Remote Play support page](https://www.playstation.com/support/games/playstation-remote-play-on-pc-and-mac/), a Windows PC needs Windows 10 (64-bit) or Windows 11, a 7th-generation Intel Core processor or later, 2 GB of RAM, and a DualSense, DualSense Edge or DualShock 4 controller connected by USB cable. You sign in with the same PlayStation Network account as the console.

### Setting it up

1. On the PS5, go to Settings > System > Remote Play and turn on **Enable Remote Play**.
2. To start sessions while the console sleeps, go to Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode and turn on **Stay Connected to the Internet** and **Enable Turning on PS5 from Network**.
3. Install the PS Remote Play app on your PC, sign in and connect your controller.
4. Keep the PS5 on the latest system software.

Sony says Remote Play also works away from home over a high-speed connection. The only titles Sony excludes are games needing a VR headset or other peripherals and games streamed through PS Plus Premium. GTA 6 is a normal installed game, so neither applies.

## Option 2: Stream from your Xbox Series X|S

Xbox remote play works the same way. On its Indian remote play page, Xbox lists Windows 10 or 11 (version 22H2 or later) as supported, with play started from xbox.com/remoteplay in a browser. You need a supported controller and a Microsoft account with an Xbox profile.

On the console, turn on remote features in settings and leave it in a power mode that can be woken over the network rather than fully shut down; Xbox Support's "How to set up remote play" guide has the current menu names.

Don't confuse this with Xbox Cloud Gaming, where a Microsoft server runs the game: that route is closed to PCs for GTA 6.

## What internet speed you need in India

These are the companies' own figures. Your home upload speed matters as much as the laptop's download speed, because the console is sending video out.

| Service | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| PS Remote Play (Sony) | 5 Mbps upload and download | 15 Mbps or more |
| Xbox remote play (Xbox India page) | 10 Mbps down, on 5 GHz Wi-Fi or mobile data | Not stated |

A few practical points:

- **Connect the console to the router with an Ethernet cable.** Most Indian fibre routers have spare LAN ports, and it removes one wireless hop.
- **Put the PC on 5 GHz Wi-Fi, or wire it too.** The 2.4 GHz band is crowded in flats and apartment blocks.
- **Distance adds lag.** Sony notes that more distance between console and screen can increase latency, so the next room on home Wi-Fi beats a hostel or office over the internet.
- **Hotspots work but eat data.** Sony warns that mobile data counts against your allowance and quality varies by network. If you're picking an operator, see our guide to the [best SIM network in India](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/what-is-the-best-sim-network-in-india).

## Option 3: Just use the PC monitor

If you mainly want your PC's better screen and the console sits nearby, skip streaming. Most monitors have an HDMI input: plug the console in, switch inputs, and you get full quality with no network lag. A capture card is the alternative if the monitor has no spare HDMI port or you want to record or stream gameplay, but it adds cost.

## What about cloud gaming on PC?

Many guides written before launch are now wrong here.

- **Xbox Cloud Gaming:** Rockstar confirmed on 10 November that GTA 6 streams through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, using Series S graphics settings, according to Dexerto and Windows Central. It's not available on PC, phones or TVs. In October, Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball said the game "will not be streaming exclusively or to PC", GamesRadar reported. Xbox Cloud Gaming has been available in India since November 2025, but that doesn't lift the PC restriction.
- **GeForce NOW and PS Plus cloud streaming:** No announcement for either. GeForce NOW streams PC versions of games you own, and there's no PC version.

## Scams to avoid

Help Net Security reported on 13 November that researchers at Cynet found a Google Colab link promising a "GTA 6 EXE file download" that led to a fake software page with "Virus Free" and "Verified" badges. The file contained no game, just an information stealer that went after saved passwords, browser cookies, Steam and Roblox logins and crypto wallets.

Treat all of these as fake:

- **"GTA 6 PC download", repack or crack files.** There's no PC build to crack. As Help Net Security put it, any site offering a PC installer "is offering something other than the game."
- **"GTA 6 PC beta" or early-access sign-ups** asking for your Rockstar, Steam or email login.
- **PS5 or Xbox Series emulators that "run GTA 6".** No such working emulator exists, and pages pairing GTA 6 with an Android emulator are pushing mobile apps.
- **Cheap "GTA 6 PC keys"** sold on marketplaces or Telegram channels.

A real PC version will be announced first on the Rockstar Newswire and Rockstar's official social accounts, and then sold through official stores, the way GTA V is sold through the Rockstar Games Launcher, Steam and the Epic Games Store.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I play GTA 6 on PC right now?

Not natively. GTA 6 launched on 19 November 2026 only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version. You can show it on a PC screen by streaming from a console you own with PS Remote Play or Xbox remote play.

### Can I play GTA 6 on PC through Xbox Game Pass or cloud gaming?

No. Rockstar says GTA 6 streams through Xbox Cloud Gaming only on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, so not on PC, phones or TVs. There's no announcement for GeForce NOW or PS Plus cloud streaming.

### What internet speed do I need for GTA 6 Remote Play?

Sony asks for at least 5 Mbps upload and download and recommends 15 Mbps or more. Xbox lists 10 Mbps download on 5 GHz Wi-Fi. A wired Ethernet connection for the console gives the most stable result.

### Is the "GTA 6 PC download" on some websites real?

No. There's no PC version, so every PC installer, crack or "beta" file is fake. Security researchers have found these files stealing passwords, game logins and crypto wallet data.
