## Information gain vs the top results (Digit, Airtel, India TV, Zee, Ujjivan)
The top results give the portal steps and "9 SIMs (6 in J&K/NE)". None of them:
- cite the 17 Aug 2026 DoT circular (photo-based screening, the 30 Nov 2026 real-time deadline, immediate suspension of over-limit SIMs);
- separate the civil penalty for excess SIMs (Third Schedule 1(b): ₹50,000 / ₹2 lakh) from the criminal offences in s.42 (3 years / ₹50 lakh). Several pages wrongly link "3 years jail" to just having extra SIMs;
- explain what each reporting option triggers (re-verification, then disconnection), with official counts;
- give the re-verification timelines, the 90-day reallocation gap, the business-connection exception, or the fact that you log in with a mobile number, not an Aadhaar number.
