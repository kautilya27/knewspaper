# Notes: area-of-a-rectangle

## Top results checked (query "area of a rectangle formula examples")
- BYJU'S (byjus.com/area-of-a-rectangle-formula): formula + 2 examples (15×4 cm; 351 cm² ÷ 27). No unit conversion, tiles, paths, diagonal or mistakes.
- Physics Wallah (pw.live/maths-formulas/area-of-a-rectangle): formula, perimeter, diagonal formula, inch/ft examples. No sq ft ↔ sq m, tiles, paths, common mistakes.
- Also in results: SplashLearn, Testbook, Twinkl, Careers360, Third Space Learning (US) — all generic formula + basic examples.

What they all say: A = l × b, square units, rearranging for a missing side.
What they miss (our gain): sq ft ↔ sq m conversion with exact derivation and Indian room sizes; tile counting (including why dividing areas undercounts when sides are not multiples of the tile); area from diagonal + side and why diagonal alone is insufficient (55-inch 16:9 TV example); NCERT-type path-outside/path-inside problem with ₹ cost; area-and-perimeter → both sides; area vs perimeter table; common mistakes list; where the topic sits in current NCERT Ganita Prakash books.
