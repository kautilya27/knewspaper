# Notes: stamp-duty-and-registration-charges (niche #16)

Research checked 18 Nov 2026 with WebSearch/WebFetch only. The BankBazaar comparable page could not be fetched (WebFetch permission not answered in time), so it was judged from search results only.

## Information gain vs top results
Top results checked: Square Yards state-wise guide (updated July 2026), Housing.com Mumbai/UP pages, ClearTax TN, plus the bank and builder blogs that make up most of the SERP. They all give a 12–15 state table with no source for each row. Their mistakes:
- Square Yards still lists Karnataka registration as 1% (max ₹15,000), but it has been 2% since 31 Aug 2025. It shows Telangana as 4% + 0.5%, and its Karnataka total contradicts its own example.
- Housing.com UP (modified Oct 2025) still gives the women's rebate as up to ₹10 lakh, but the limit was raised to ₹1 crore in July 2025. Its Mumbai page conflicts on the registration fee.
- ClearTax TN gives 4% registration and contradictory dates.

Our gain: one table with a source for each row, built from official pages (Delhi Revenue Department, IGR Maharashtra fee table) and news reports of the latest change in each state. It includes the 2025–26 changes (Karnataka 2% fee, UP ₹1 crore women's limit, TN women's registration cut, Maharashtra RR freeze for FY27 and the pending ₹50,000 fee proposal, Telangana June 2026 value revision). It shows the cess and surcharge components and gives worked examples of the higher-of rule (Delhi) plus a three-city ₹1 crore comparison.

## Not verified / left out
- States left out: West Bengal (the official doc found was a history table; the current band structure above ₹40 lakh / ₹1 crore was not confirmed), Gujarat (sources conflict on women: registration fee waiver vs "3.9%" stamp duty), Rajasthan, Punjab, MP, AP and Bihar (no recent sourced figure).
- Delhi joint-owner rate (commonly quoted as 5%) is not on the official page, so it was not used.
- Haryana joint rate and urban/rural split: not verified, so not used.
- Telangana split of the 7.5% (stamp duty / transfer duty / registration) after 2021: not confirmed, so only the combined figure is given.
- TN registration fee: ClearTax and one 2026 blog say 4%. KS&K (2023 budget) and verified.realestate say 2%. We used 2%. Worth a check on tnreginet if possible.
- Whether Karnataka's planned April 2026 guidance value revision took effect: not confirmed (the article only says a rise was planned).
- The Maharashtra metro cess / LBT breakdown comes from a developer's opinion piece and a Tata Capital page, not an IGR notification.
- Income-tax deduction for stamp duty was dropped from the FAQ: the Income-tax Act 2025 took effect on 1 Apr 2026 and the section mapping was not verified.
