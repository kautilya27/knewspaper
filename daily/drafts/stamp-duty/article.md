**Quick answer:** In most big states, buying a home costs between about 5% and 10% of the property's value in stamp duty and registration charges together. Delhi charges 6% stamp duty for men and 4% for women, plus a 1% registration fee. Mumbai charges 6% stamp duty, including a 1% metro cess, and a registration fee capped at ₹30,000. Karnataka now charges a 2% registration fee on top of 5.6% stamp duty, cess and surcharge.

Two rules hold almost everywhere. First, duty is charged on the **higher** of the price in your agreement and the government's minimum value for the property (called the circle rate, ready reckoner rate, guidance value or market value, depending on the state). Second, the rates change often, through budgets, cabinet decisions and yearly revisions of those minimum values. The table below shows rates as reported up to 18 November 2026 for eight selected states. **Check your state's stamp and registration portal before you pay.**

## State-wise stamp duty and registration charges (2026)

This table covers only the states whose rates we could match to an official page or a news report of the latest change. Other states are left out on purpose.

| State | Stamp duty (general rate) | Women's concession | Registration fee | Cess or surcharge inside the duty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maharashtra | 6% in Mumbai; 7% in Pune, Thane, Nagpur and other municipal corporations | 1 percentage point lower for residential property bought by a woman or only by women | 1%, capped at ₹30,000 | 1% metro cess; outside Mumbai also a 1% local body surcharge |

### Where each row comes from

- **Maharashtra:** The registration fee table on the [IGR Maharashtra website](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in/pdf/eodb/3.1Registration%20fee%20table.pdf) sets the fee at ₹100 plus ₹10 for every ₹1,000 above ₹10,000, "subject to the maximum of Rs. 30,000". In July 2026, the Free Press Journal reported that the government was weighing a rise to ₹50,000, with no decision yet. The 1% metro cess has applied since 1 April 2022 in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (including Thane), Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad and Nagpur. According to Lokmat Times, the women's 1% concession dates from 2021, and in 2023 the state dropped the condition that stopped these buyers from selling to a man for 15 years. The state kept ready reckoner rates unchanged for 2026-27, according to the Free Press Journal.
- **Delhi:** These rates are from the [Delhi Revenue Department's registration page](https://revenue.delhi.gov.in/revenue/important-information-regarding-registration-property), which says duty and fee are charged on "the consideration amount or the circle rate, whichever is higher". The page gives no separate rate for joint buyers.
- **Karnataka:** Deccan Herald reported that the registration fee doubled to 2% from 31 August 2025, taking the total from 6.6% to 7.6%. A 2026 Deccan Herald report gave the same 7.6% total and said a rise in guidance values was planned.
- **Tamil Nadu:** The 2023-24 state budget cut the registration fee from 4% to 2%, according to the state Finance Department's policy note. The first sale of a new flat or apartment is charged differently: under G.O. 131 of 1 December 2023, the total including the 2% registration fee is 6% for flats below ₹50 lakh and 7% from ₹50 lakh to ₹3 crore, on the composite value. Resales pay the full rates in the table. DT Next reported that the women's registration fee cut (houses, plots and farmland up to ₹10 lakh) took effect on 1 April 2025.
- **Uttar Pradesh:** All India Radio and ThePrint reported that the UP cabinet raised the limit for women's 1% stamp duty concession from ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore in July 2025. Under the old limit, the most a woman could save was ₹10,000. At 1% of ₹1 crore, the most she can save now is ₹1 lakh.
- **Haryana:** In August 2025, the Chief Minister told the Assembly that stamp duty is 7% for men and 5% for women, including a development fee, and has not changed since 2008, according to The Tribune. The registration fee is a fixed amount for each value band, reaching ₹50,000 for property worth ₹90 lakh or more.
- **Telangana:** In June 2026, the state revised land and flat market values but kept the combined 7.5% rate, Deccan Chronicle reported.
- **Kerala:** Kerala Kaumudi reports 8% stamp duty and a 2% registration fee on sale deeds. The June 2026 state budget proposed a review of land fair values to correct disparities across the state.

## Why the circle rate matters more than the percentage

The percentage is only half of the sum. The other half is the value the percentage is applied to. Every state publishes a minimum value for each area: Delhi calls it the circle rate, Maharashtra the ready reckoner rate, Karnataka and Telangana the guidance or market value, and Kerala the fair value. You pay duty on whichever is higher, this value or the price in your agreement.

So you never get a lower duty by writing a lower price in the agreement. And if the government's value for your area goes up, your duty goes up too, even when the percentage stays the same. In Gurugram, ThePrint worked out that a collector-rate revision in August 2025 raised the duty on one 500-yard plot from ₹7.59 lakh to ₹13.5 lakh. ThePrint's figures use an average 6% rate (7% for men, 5% for women), and those rates had not changed.

### Worked example: a flat in Delhi

Say a couple agrees to buy a flat in Delhi for ₹80 lakh, but the circle rate for that size and locality works out to ₹90 lakh.

1. The duty is worked out on ₹90 lakh, because that is higher than ₹80 lakh.
2. If the flat is registered in the husband's name, stamp duty at 6% is ₹5,40,000.
3. If it is registered in the wife's name, stamp duty at 4% is ₹3,60,000, which is ₹1,80,000 less.
4. The registration fee at 1% of ₹90 lakh is ₹90,000, plus the ₹100 pasting fee.

The total is ₹6,30,100 in the husband's name or ₹4,50,100 in the wife's name. Now say the agreed price was ₹1 crore and the circle rate was still ₹90 lakh. Everything would then be worked out on ₹1 crore, because that is now the higher figure.

### The same ₹1 crore flat in three cities

For a home valued at ₹1 crore (the higher of agreement value and circle rate), using the rates in the table:

| City | Bought by a man | Bought by a woman |
|---|---|---|
| Pune (7% + registration capped at ₹30,000) | ₹7,30,000 | ₹6,30,000 |
| Bengaluru (5.6% + 2% registration) | ₹7,60,000 | ₹7,60,000 |
| Lucknow (7% + 1% registration) | ₹8,00,000 | ₹7,00,000 |

The Pune figures show how the ₹30,000 cap works. On a ₹1 crore flat, 1% would be ₹1 lakh, but the fee stops at ₹30,000. In Bengaluru, there is no women's rate and no cap on the 2% fee, so the fee alone is ₹2 lakh.

## Things that change the final bill

- **The size you are charged on.** Circle rates are often set per square foot or square metre. Our guide to [carpet area vs built-up area](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/carpet-area-vs-built-up-area) explains which measure appears where.
- **Who is on the deed.** A women's concession usually applies only when the property is in a woman's name. Maharashtra gives it to joint buyers only if every buyer is a woman, according to Lokmat Times. Delhi's page sets no joint rate, so check before deciding how to hold the title.
- **Type and location.** Maharashtra's concession covers residential property only. In several states, rates differ between corporation, municipality and village areas.
- **Other costs.** Stamp duty is separate from GST on under-construction flats, loan processing fees and the property tax you pay once you own the home. Your bank will also want the registered sale deed and proof of stamp duty paid, as our [home loan documents checklist](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/essential-documents-for-your-home-loan-application) explains.

Most states let you check the circle rate and work out the duty on the same portal where you pay it, such as GRAS in Maharashtra, Kaveri in Karnataka and the e-valuation tool on Delhi's revenue site. The figure on the portal on the day you pay is the one that counts. A table like this one only helps you plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is stamp duty charged on the agreement value or the circle rate?

It is charged on whichever is higher. Delhi's Revenue Department states this rule outright, and the other states in this table follow the same principle under their own names for the government value.

### Which states give women a lower stamp duty?

Of the states in this table, Delhi (4% against 6%), Haryana (5% against 7%), Maharashtra (1 point lower) and Uttar Pradesh (1 point lower on property up to ₹1 crore) charge women less stamp duty. Tamil Nadu cuts the registration fee instead, for property up to ₹10 lakh.

### Is the registration fee capped?

Maharashtra caps it at ₹30,000 and Haryana's highest slab is ₹50,000. In Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Kerala, the sources checked here give a percentage with no cap.

### Why can stamp duty go up when the rate has not changed?

The duty is a percentage of the government's value for your property, so a higher circle rate or guidance value means a bigger bill. Telangana in June 2026 and Haryana in August 2025 both raised these values and left the duty rate as it was.
