## Information gain vs top results
Top results (ClearTax, Bajaj Finserv, BYJU'S, Upstox, Business Standard, BOOM FAQ) give the definition and formula, mostly with old or no figures. Missing from them: a worked example covering all three deficits; the 2026-27 BE and 2025-26 RE table from Budget at a Glance; the financing breakdown (G-secs, small savings/NSSF, T-bills); the debt-to-GDP anchor (50±1% by March 2031); H1 2026-27 CGA data and overshoot forecasts; the 16th Finance Commission's state limits; and counter-views on both sides.

## Not verified / left out
- April–October 2026 CGA data: not released as of 28 Nov 2026 (due on the last working day of November). The article says it is due.
- Gross market borrowing for 2026-27: not verified, so only net market borrowing is used.
- Any extra borrowing allowance for states in 2026-27, such as a power-sector 0.5%: not verified. Only the SASCI exclusion is mentioned.
- The FY21 deficit is reported as 9.2% or 9.3% in different sources; "about 9.2%" (Business Standard) is used.
- CAG FRBM audit PDF: the fetch was blocked (permission not answered), so it was not used.
- No party and no officeholder is named. "The government" and "the Budget" are used throughout.
