Refund and cancellation policy

This policy covers payments for guest post plans on www.knewspaper.in. Please read it before you buy.

Last updated 5 November 2026.

No refunds

All purchases are final. We do not give refunds in any case, including:

  • unused article credits, or credits that expire after 12 months;
  • articles that don't pass the publishing checks;
  • articles removed because they broke our guidelines or terms (we return the credit instead, so you can publish one more article in its place);
  • a change of mind after payment.

Cancellation

Plans are one-time payments, not subscriptions, so nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. A plan cannot be cancelled for a refund after payment.

Failed payments

If money was taken from your account but no plan shows in your account on the Write for us page, the payment did not complete. Such amounts are normally returned to you by your bank or Razorpay. If you need help, email us with the payment details at knewspaper.in@gmail.com and we will look into it with Razorpay.

Contact

Email knewspaper.in@gmail.com or use our contact page.
