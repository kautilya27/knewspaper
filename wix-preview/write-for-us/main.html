We are looking for well-researched, engaging content between 500 and 1,500 words. Articles should be formatted for readability, with proper attribution for any sources used. Use at least two subheadings (at least one of them an H2), and include up to 5 links to other websites. Because these are paid placements, every outside link is marked rel="sponsored" and the post is labelled as a sponsored guest post, as Google and ASCI guidelines require.
