# Articles with out-of-date or wrong facts (found while adding FAQs, 5 Nov 2026)

The FAQs added to these articles avoid repeating the problems below, but the articles themselves
still contain them. Candidates for a full rewrite, roughly in order of impact.

## Clearly wrong or badly dated
- **gta-6-pc-requirements**: says release "Fall 2025" and treats PC specs as official. Game is out 19 Nov 2026 on PS5/Xbox Series only; no PC version or official PC specs announced.
- **what-is-swp**: capital-gains tax rates are pre-July-2024 (15% STCG, 10% LTCG over ₹1 lakh, indexation).

- **top-10-government-exams-in-india-you-should-prepare-for**: SSC CGL has 2 tiers (not 4); Navy SSR/AA now via Agniveer; CLAT is not a govt job exam; excerpt says 2025, title 2027.

- **use-linkedin-for-business-growth**: recommends LinkedIn Stories (shut down 2021); old user count; unsourced stats.
- **why-is-the-rupee-falling-against-the-dollar**: written around 2022 (US rate hikes, Russia-Ukraine).
- **real-estate-investment-trusts**: written for US readers (US law, IRAs, $); nothing on SEBI-regulated Indian REITs.
- **mcdonalds-vs-burger-king** and **side-hustles-for-college-students**: written for the US (beef Big Mac, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Poshmark…).
- **top-10-indian-web-series**: old platform names (Disney+ Hotstar → JioHotstar, MX Player → Amazon MX Player); Panchayat is on Prime Video; genre errors.
- **are-7-wonders-of-the-world-official**: Herodotus dated wrong (5th c. BCE); Ephesus is in Turkey, not Greece.
