## Best Korean restaurants in south Delhi

### 1. Gung The Palace (Green Park)

**Area:** D-1/B, Green Park, New Delhi

A well-known Korean restaurant that Delhi food guides regularly feature in 2026. It's a proper sit-down restaurant, which makes it a good pick for a family dinner or a group that wants to share a wide spread of Korean dishes. Book ahead on weekends.

### 2. Seoul Restaurant (Ansal Plaza)

**Area:** Ansal Plaza, Khel Gaon Marg, near Siri Fort, New Delhi

A Korean restaurant inside Ansal Plaza mall, convenient if you live in south Delhi or want to pair a meal with shopping. Good for trying classic Korean dishes in a comfortable setting.

### 3. Dalgrak (Lajpat Nagar)

**Area:** Korean Cultural Centre India, Ring Road, Lajpat Nagar 4, New Delhi

A small eatery inside the Korean Cultural Centre, so it's ideal if you're visiting the centre for an exhibition, a language class or a K-culture event. Treat it as a place for a light Korean meal or snack rather than a big dinner.

### 4. The For'est (Vasant Vihar)

**Area:** Basant Lok Market, Vasant Vihar, New Delhi

A café that lists Korean food alongside continental dishes. It suits a relaxed afternoon with friends when not everyone wants an all-Korean menu.

## Humayunpur: Delhi's Korean food lane

Humayunpur, an urban village next to Safdarjung Enclave, is packed with small Korean, Japanese and North-East Indian eateries. It's busy with students and young professionals, especially in the evenings.

### 5. Kori's (Humayunpur)

**Area:** 70, Humayunpur, Safdarjung Enclave, New Delhi

A popular, casual Korean spot with recent reviews through 2026. Go with friends and share several dishes; tteokbokki (spicy rice cakes), Korean fried chicken, kimbap and ramyeon are good shared picks at casual Korean places like this. Kori's also has an outlet in Gurugram (see below).

### 6. Dongne The Town (Humayunpur)

**Area:** 77B, Humayunpur, Safdarjung Enclave, New Delhi

A Korean café-restaurant that works well for a laid-back meal. It's a good place for beginners to try bibimbap or a noodle dish before moving on to spicier stews.

### 7. Kim Korean (Humayunpur)

**Area:** Humayunpur, Safdarjung, New Delhi

A small, no-frills eatery serving Korean dishes along with some Indo-Chinese options, handy for a quick, budget-conscious meal while you're in the area.

## Majnu ka Tilla: home-style Korean in north Delhi

Majnu ka Tilla, the Tibetan settlement near the Yamuna in north Delhi, is better known for momos and laphing, but it also has some of the city's most loved casual Korean restaurants. It's an easy trip from North Campus.

### 8. Busan Korean Restaurant (Majnu ka Tilla)

**Area:** 9B, Block 10, New Aruna Nagar, Majnu ka Tilla, New Delhi

A simple, homely restaurant known for everyday Korean food rather than décor. Kimchi jjigae (kimchi stew), bulgogi and japchae are good places to start.

### 9. Gangnam Korean Restaurant (Majnu ka Tilla)

**Area:** 1st Floor, Tara House, New Aruna Colony, Majnu ka Tilla, New Delhi

Another popular Majnu ka Tilla option with steady recent reviews. Order a mix of a rice dish, a stew and a pancake (pajeon) to share.

## What to order at a Korean restaurant

New to Korean food? These are safe bets:

- **Bibimbap:** rice topped with vegetables, egg and gochujang (chilli paste), mixed at the table.
- **Kimbap:** Korean rice rolls with vegetables, egg or meat.
- **Japchae:** stir-fried glass noodles with vegetables.
- **Bulgogi:** sweet-savoury marinated meat, usually beef in Korea; many Delhi menus offer chicken or pork.
- **Tteokbokki:** chewy rice cakes in a spicy-sweet sauce.
- **Kimchi jjigae:** a warming, spicy kimchi stew.
- **Bingsu:** shaved-ice dessert, popular at Korean cafés.

## Tips before you go

- **Vegetarian?** Many places offer veg bibimbap, kimbap, japchae and tteokbokki. Kimchi and some broths can contain fish sauce or seafood, so ask if you eat strictly vegetarian.
- **Spice levels:** Korean "spicy" can be quite hot; ask for less gochujang if you're unsure.
- **Banchan:** many Korean restaurants serve small side dishes with your meal; ask the staff what's included.
- **Check before you travel:** small eateries in Humayunpur and Majnu ka Tilla change hours, menus and even locations. Check the restaurant's current listing on a food app or map before making the trip.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which areas in Delhi are known for Korean food?

Humayunpur near Safdarjung Enclave and Majnu ka Tilla in north Delhi are the best-known areas for casual Korean eateries. South Delhi also has sit-down options in Green Park, Lajpat Nagar, Ansal Plaza and Vasant Vihar, and Gurugram's Golf Course Road has several Korean restaurants.

### Is Korean food in Delhi suitable for vegetarians?

Yes, many Korean restaurants and cafes offer vegetarian versions of popular dishes such as bibimbap, kimbap, japchae, tteokbokki and ramyeon. Traditional kimchi, broths and some sauces can contain fish sauce or seafood, so ask the staff if you want a strictly vegetarian meal.

### What Korean dishes should a beginner try first?

Good dishes to start with are bibimbap, a mixed rice bowl with vegetables, kimbap rice rolls, japchae glass noodles and bulgogi, a marinated grilled meat, which are flavourful without being very spicy. If you enjoy heat, try tteokbokki or kimchi stew, and end with bingsu, a shaved-ice dessert.

### Is eating Korean food in Delhi expensive?

It varies a lot. Small eateries in Humayunpur and Majnu ka Tilla serve noodles, kimbap and tteokbokki at modest prices, while sit-down Korean restaurants and mall outlets cost considerably more. Menus and prices change often, so check the current menu and opening hours before you go.
