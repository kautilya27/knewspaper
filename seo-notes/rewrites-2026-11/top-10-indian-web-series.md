Indian web series have grown from a few YouTube experiments into some of the most-watched shows in the country. If you want to know which ones are worth your time and where to stream them, this list picks ten of the best across crime, comedy, drama and thrillers, with the correct platform and season status as of November 2026.

**Quick answer:** the top Indian web series to watch are **Sacred Games** (Netflix), **Mirzapur** (Prime Video), **The Family Man** (Prime Video), **Panchayat** (Prime Video), **Scam 1992** (SonyLIV), **Criminal Justice** (JioHotstar), **Gullak** (SonyLIV), **Farzi** (Prime Video), **TVF Pitchers** (ZEE5) and **ImMATURE** (Prime Video). For crime fans, **Delhi Crime** (Netflix) and **Asur** (JioHotstar) are essential extras.

## 1. Sacred Games (Netflix)

Netflix's first Indian original, based on Vikram Chandra's novel, follows Mumbai police officer Sartaj Singh (Saif Ali Khan) as gangster Ganesh Gaitonde (Nawazuddin Siddiqui) warns him of a coming catastrophe. Its dark, layered take on Mumbai's underworld set the bar for Indian streaming drama. The story ends after two seasons, so you can binge it from start to finish.

## 2. Mirzapur (Prime Video)

A violent, darkly funny gangster drama set in eastern Uttar Pradesh, built around Kaleen Bhaiya (Pankaj Tripathi), Guddu (Ali Fazal) and Munna (Divyenndu). Three seasons are out, the latest in July 2024. The story continued on the big screen with **Mirzapur: The Movie**, released in cinemas on 4 September 2026. Watch the seasons in order before the film.

## 3. The Family Man (Prime Video)

Manoj Bajpayee plays Srikant Tiwari, a middle-class father secretly working for a fictional intelligence agency. Created by Raj & DK, it mixes espionage, action and sharp family comedy. Season 3 arrived on 21 November 2025, and Bajpayee has hinted at a fourth season, though no date has been announced.

## 4. Panchayat (Prime Video)

An engineering graduate takes the only job he can get, as secretary of a village panchayat office in Phulera, and slowly becomes part of village life. Produced by TVF, Panchayat streams on **Prime Video**, not on TVF's own platform. Four seasons are out (the fourth in June 2025), and Prime Video has announced a fifth for 2026.

## 5. Scam 1992: The Harshad Mehta Story (SonyLIV)

Hansal Mehta's drama about stockbroker Harshad Mehta and the 1992 securities scam, starring Pratik Gandhi, is one of the highest-rated Indian series ever. It's a single, complete season. The same team later made **Scam 2003** on SonyLIV about the Telgi stamp-paper scam.

## 6. Criminal Justice (JioHotstar)

Pankaj Tripathi plays Madhav Mishra, a small-time lawyer who keeps landing big criminal cases. Each season tells a new case, so you can start anywhere. There are four seasons; the latest, *A Family Matter*, ran from May to July 2025. It streams on **JioHotstar** (formerly Disney+ Hotstar).

## 7. Gullak (SonyLIV)

A gentle, funny slice-of-life series about the Mishra family in a small north Indian town, narrated by the family's piggy bank (gullak). It's the show to watch with parents. Season 5 premiered on SonyLIV on 5 June 2026.

## 8. Farzi (Prime Video)

Raj & DK's thriller follows an artist (Shahid Kapoor) who gets drawn into making counterfeit currency, chased by a task-force officer (Vijay Sethupathi). Slick and fast-moving, it was one of Prime Video's biggest Indian hits. Season 2 was officially confirmed in early 2026; check Prime Video for its release.

## 9. TVF Pitchers (ZEE5)

One of TVF's best-loved shows: four friends quit their jobs to build a start-up. Season 1 came out on TVF's own platform in 2015; season 2 arrived on **ZEE5** in December 2022. It's still the go-to series for anyone dreaming of a start-up.

## 10. ImMATURE (Prime Video)

A light coming-of-age comedy about school friends dealing with first crushes, exams and growing up. Season 1 premiered in 2019 on MX Player (now Amazon MX Player); seasons 2 and 3 are on **Prime Video**.

## More great Indian web series by genre

- **Crime drama:** **Delhi Crime** (Netflix) is a scripted crime drama, not a documentary. Season 1 was based on the investigation into the 2012 Delhi gang-rape case and won the International Emmy for Best Drama Series in 2020. Season 3 premiered in November 2025.
- **Crime thriller with mythology:** **Asur** (JioHotstar) is a crime thriller that mixes forensic science and Hindu mythology, as a forensic expert and a CBI team chase a serial killer. Season 1 was on Voot (2020), season 2 on JioCinema (2023), and season 3 began filming in 2026.
- **Crime drama:** **Aarya** (JioHotstar) stars Sushmita Sen as a woman pulled into her family's drug business; it ran for three seasons from 2020 to 2024.
- **Youth drama:** **Kota Factory** (Netflix) follows students preparing for IIT-JEE in Kota; season 3 arrived in June 2024.

## Why Indian web series took off

Cheap mobile data and affordable streaming plans brought shows to millions of phones. Streaming also gave writers freedom television didn't: longer story arcs, small-town settings, regional dialects and mature themes such as caste, politics, crime and corruption. Producers like TVF showed that simple, relatable stories (Panchayat, Gullak) can be as popular as big-budget thrillers (Mirzapur, The Family Man).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best Indian web series of all time?

There is no single answer, but Scam 1992, Sacred Games, The Family Man and Panchayat are the series most often named among the best. Scam 1992 is a complete, single-season story, while Panchayat and The Family Man are good picks if you want several seasons to binge.

### Where can I watch Panchayat?

Panchayat streams on Amazon Prime Video in India. It is produced by TVF, but it is not on TVF's own platform. Four seasons are available, and Prime Video has announced a fifth season for 2026.

### Which platforms are best for Indian web series?

Amazon Prime Video, Netflix, JioHotstar, SonyLIV and ZEE5 have the biggest libraries of Indian originals. JioHotstar replaced Disney+ Hotstar and JioCinema in 2025, and MX Player is now Amazon MX Player, so older guides may list outdated names.

### Is Delhi Crime a true story?

Delhi Crime is a scripted drama, not a documentary. Its first season was inspired by the Delhi Police investigation into the 2012 Delhi gang-rape case, while later seasons follow new cases with the same Delhi Police team. It streams on Netflix.
