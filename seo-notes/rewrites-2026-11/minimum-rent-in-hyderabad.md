Hyderabad is still one of the more affordable big cities in India to rent in, but rents in the IT corridor have climbed fast. What you pay depends mostly on how close you live to HITEC City, Gachibowli and the Financial District.

**Quick answer:** in mid-2026, the cheapest 1 BHK flats in Hyderabad were listed from about **₹7,000–12,000 a month in LB Nagar** and **₹8,000–13,000 in Miyapur**, according to locality ranges published by the property portal Square Yards (updated May 2026). In the IT hubs, a 1 BHK typically costs **₹15,000–24,000**, and in Banjara Hills or Jubilee Hills **₹18,000–30,000**. A standard 2 BHK in a newer project costs more: ANAROCK's Q1 2026 data puts it at **₹21,000–28,000 in Miyapur** and **₹32,000–43,000 in Gachibowli**.

## Why rent varies so much across Hyderabad

Rent isn't the same everywhere in the city. The main reasons:

- **Distance to the IT corridor.** Flats near HITEC City, Madhapur, Gachibowli and Kondapur cost the most because so many tech workers want short commutes.
- **Metro access.** Areas on the Hyderabad Metro, such as Miyapur, Kukatpally and LB Nagar on the Red Line, give you a cheaper home with a predictable commute.
- **Type of building.** A flat in a gated community with a pool, gym and security costs more than a similar flat in an independent building.
- **Size and age.** Bigger, newer flats with parking and power backup command higher rents.
- **Furnishing.** Fully furnished flats cost more than semi-furnished or unfurnished ones.

## So what is the minimum rent in Hyderabad?

If you want a self-contained flat, the lowest realistic budget in 2026 is around **₹7,000–8,000 a month for a basic 1 BHK** in outer, well-connected areas such as LB Nagar or Miyapur, going by the Square Yards ranges. Anything cheaper is usually a single room, a 1 RK, an older independent house further out, or a shared flat. Near the IT hubs, plan for at least **₹13,000–16,000** for a 1 BHK.

Remember that rent is not your only monthly cost. Add maintenance charges (common in gated communities), electricity, water, internet and cooking gas when you set your budget. ## Matching your budget to an area: three examples

**Student near Miyapur, budget ₹8,000–10,000 for a 1 BHK.** This sits at the lower end of Miyapur's listed 1 BHK range. Expect an older or smaller flat in an independent building rather than a gated community. Sharing a 2 BHK with a friend often gets you a better flat for the same money.

**Young professional working in Gachibowli, budget ₹12,000–15,000.** This is below the typical 1 BHK range in Gachibowli itself. Look at the lower end of Kondapur, or live in Miyapur or Kukatpally and commute. Sharing a 2 BHK in Gachibowli or Kondapur is another common option.

**Family looking for a 2 BHK, budget ₹15,000–20,000.** LB Nagar, Miyapur, Kompally and Kukatpally all have 2 BHK listings in this range. ## Security deposits and zero-deposit options

Landlords in Hyderabad usually ask for a refundable security deposit of a few months' rent, plus the first month's rent in advance. The exact amount is negotiable and varies by landlord, so agree on it, and on the conditions for getting it back, before you pay.

Some fintech services, such as [CirclePe](https://www.circlepe.club/), pay the landlord rent in advance so that the tenant can move in without a deposit, and charge the tenant a platform fee on top of the monthly rent. Compare the total extra cost over your lease with the deposit you would otherwise lock up, and read the terms on lock-in periods and missed payments before signing.

Whatever you choose, put everything in a written rental agreement. Our guide on [how to get a rental agreement online in India](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/how-to-get-a-rental-agreement-online-in-india) explains the steps and stamp duty.

## Tips for finding a cheaper flat in Hyderabad

- **Look one or two Metro stops out.** Areas on the Red Line can be much cheaper than the IT hubs while keeping your commute predictable.
- **Share a bigger flat.** Splitting a 2 or 3 BHK with flatmates usually costs less per person than renting a 1 BHK alone.
- **Negotiate.** Landlords may lower the rent or deposit if you offer a longer stay, move in quickly or take the flat unfurnished.
- **Go unfurnished.** Buying a few basics yourself can be cheaper than paying extra rent for furniture every month.
- **Compare several listing sites and visit in person.** Photos and asking rents can be out of date; check the flat, water supply and parking yourself.
- **Check all costs in writing.** Ask about maintenance charges, brokerage, lock-in period and notice period before you agree.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which areas in Hyderabad have the cheapest rent?

LB Nagar, Miyapur, Kompally and Kukatpally have some of the lowest rents among well-connected areas, according to Square Yards' May 2026 locality ranges. The IT hubs (Gachibowli, Madhapur, Kondapur) and upmarket areas like Jubilee Hills and Banjara Hills cost the most. Rents vary by building and furnishing, so compare current listings.

### What is the minimum rent for a 1 BHK in Hyderabad?

Based on Square Yards' locality ranges updated in May 2026, a basic 1 BHK starts at about ₹7,000–8,000 a month in outer areas such as LB Nagar and Miyapur. Near the IT corridor, 1 BHK flats are usually listed from about ₹13,000–16,000 a month.

### Is an unfurnished flat cheaper to rent in Hyderabad?

Yes. An unfurnished or semi-furnished flat usually costs less per month than a fully furnished one in the same building. If you plan to stay for a long time, buying a few basic pieces of furniture yourself can work out cheaper than paying extra rent for a furnished flat.

### How much security deposit do landlords in Hyderabad ask for?

It depends on the landlord and the area, but deposits are usually a few months' rent, refundable when you move out after deductions for damage or unpaid bills. Agree on the amount and the refund conditions before paying, and make sure both are written into your rental agreement.
