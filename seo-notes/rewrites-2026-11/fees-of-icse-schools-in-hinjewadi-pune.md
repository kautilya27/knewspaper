## What is an ICSE school?

ICSE schools are affiliated to the **Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE)**, which conducts the ICSE exam in Class 10 and the ISC exam in Class 12. The board is known for detailed syllabuses, a strong focus on English and languages, and a wide choice of subjects, including electives such as Environmental Science and Technical Drawing Applications. Many schools near Hinjewadi offer ICSE alongside CBSE, so check which board the specific campus and grade follow.

## How ICSE school fees in Hinjewadi are structured

Schools split their charges in different ways, but a fee sheet usually includes:

| Fee component | How it is charged | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Registration / application fee | Once, when you apply | Whether it is refundable (usually not) |
| Admission fee | Once, at admission | The amount, and what happens if you withdraw before the session starts |
| Tuition fee | Yearly, often in instalments | Whether it rises by class, and the instalment dates |
| Development or infrastructure fee | Yearly or once | Whether it is compulsory and how it is used |
| Transport | Yearly, by distance or route | Route, pick-up time and whether the bus has a female attendant |
| Books, uniform and kit | Yearly | Whether you must buy from the school or can buy outside |
| Activities, trips and clubs | Per term or per activity | Which are optional and which are compulsory |
| Meals, day care or after-school programmes | Monthly or yearly | Whether they are optional |
| Refundable caution deposit | Once | When and how it is refunded |

Fees also tend to rise as children move up, with secondary classes usually costing more than pre-primary and primary.

## Why do ICSE schools often cost more?

ICSE schools in Pune often charge more than state-board schools and many CBSE schools, though there is plenty of overlap. The usual reasons are:

- **Infrastructure:** labs, libraries, sports facilities, smart classrooms and transport fleets.
- **Smaller classes and more teachers**, especially in schools that promise individual attention.
- **Activities:** music, sports coaching, robotics, clubs and trips, some of which are charged separately.
- **Location:** land and building costs in and around an IT hub are high, and schools pass these on.

Board alone doesn't decide fees: a premium CBSE school can cost more than a modest ICSE school. Compare the full fee sheets of the actual schools you are considering.

## Costs parents often miss

- **Admission-year spike:** the first year includes admission and registration fees, a caution deposit, uniforms and books together, so it costs much more than later years.
- **Annual increases:** ask how much fees have risen in the last two or three years.
- **Transport:** bus fees can be a large share of the total if you live far from school.
- **Technology charges:** some schools charge for devices, learning apps or online platforms.
- **Optional activities** that become hard to say no to once your child's friends join.

In Maharashtra, how private unaided schools may raise fees is governed by the Maharashtra Educational Institutions (Regulation of Fee) Act, 2011, which gives parents' representatives a role in fee decisions. If a fee hike seems unusually steep, ask the school how it was approved.

## Financial planning tips for parents

1. **Budget for the full cost, not just tuition.** Add transport, books, uniforms, activities and a buffer for annual increases.
2. **Ask about instalments.** Many schools let you pay tuition quarterly or termly.
3. **Check sibling discounts** if you will have two children in the same school.
4. **Ask about scholarships or concessions** for merit or financial need.
5. **Look at RTE admissions if eligible.** Under the Right to Education Act, eligible families can apply for free seats in the entry class of private unaided non-minority schools through Maharashtra's online RTE admission process.
6. **Claim the tax deduction if it applies to you** (see below).

## Tax deduction on school tuition fees

If you file taxes under the **old tax regime**, the tuition fee you pay for up to two children's full-time education in India counts towards the overall ₹1.5 lakh yearly deduction limit, along with investments like PPF, ELSS and life insurance premiums.

- For fees paid up to 31 March 2026 (financial year 2025-26), this deduction is claimed under **Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961**.
- From 1 April 2026 (tax year 2026-27), the **Income-tax Act, 2025** has replaced the old Act, and the same deduction moves to **Section 123**, with the same ₹1.5 lakh overall limit.
- Only the **tuition fee** part qualifies. Admission, development, transport and activity charges do not.
- The **new tax regime does not allow this deduction**, so it only helps if the old regime works out better for you.

Ask the school for a receipt that shows the tuition fee separately.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I claim a tax deduction for my child's ICSE school fees?

Yes, but only the tuition fee part and only under the old tax regime. Tuition fees for up to two children count towards the overall limit of ₹1.5 lakh a year: under Section 80C for 2025-26, and under Section 123 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 from April 2026. Admission, transport, development and activity fees do not qualify, and the new tax regime gives no such deduction.

### What is the difference between ICSE and CBSE schools?

ICSE schools follow the syllabus of the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations, which is known for its detailed coverage and strong focus on English and languages. CBSE schools follow the Central Board of Secondary Education, whose NCERT-based syllabus is closely aligned with national entrance exams such as JEE and NEET. Neither is better for every child.

### Are ICSE schools more expensive than CBSE schools in Pune?

Often, yes. ICSE schools in Pune generally charge more than state board schools and many CBSE schools, mostly because of their infrastructure, smaller classes and extra activities. Fees still vary widely from school to school within each board, so compare the full fee structure of the schools you are considering rather than going by the board alone.

### What should parents ask a school about fees before admission?

Ask for the full fee structure in writing, including tuition, admission, development, transport, books, uniform and activity charges. Check how much fees usually rise each year, whether you can pay in instalments, whether sibling discounts apply, and what the refund rules are if your child leaves the school mid-year.
