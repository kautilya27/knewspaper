McDonald's vs Burger King in India is not the same contest as in the US. There is no beef Big Mac, no dollar menu and no ICEE here. Both chains have rebuilt their menus for Indian tastes, with chicken and vegetarian burgers, spicy flavours and value combos, so the comparison comes down to which Indian menu suits you better.

**Quick answer:** pick **McDonald's** for the bigger vegetarian line-up (McAloo Tikki, McVeggie, McSpicy Paneer), Happy Meals, McCafé coffee and a very wide store network. Pick **Burger King** if you want **flame-grilled, larger burgers** like the Whopper, more experimental limited-time ranges (Korean, peri-peri) and frequent app deals. Neither serves beef in India, and McDonald's India serves no pork either.

## Who runs McDonald's and Burger King in India?

Knowing who runs each chain explains why menus and offers can differ from city to city.

- **McDonald's India** is split into two regions. **Westlife Foodworld** runs McDonald's in West and South India; it had 478 restaurants in 78 cities at the end of March 2026, according to its quarterly results. A separate licensee, the Delhi-based **MM Agrawal Group**, has run McDonald's in North and East India since 2020. The two regions have their own websites, apps and some different menu items and offers.
- **Burger King India** is run by **Restaurant Brands Asia**, which opened the first Indian Burger King in Delhi in 2014. It opened its **600th Indian restaurant** in September 2026 and is present in more than 140 cities.

## Menu in India: what you can actually order

Both chains follow the same basic rule in India: **no beef**. McDonald's India also keeps **pork** off the menu and prepares vegetarian and non-vegetarian food separately. That's why burgers here are built on chicken, potato, paneer and other vegetarian patties.

| | McDonald's India | Burger King India |
|---|---|---|
| Signature burger | Maharaja Mac (chicken and veg versions), McSpicy | Whopper (veg and chicken versions) |
| Cooking style | Griddle-cooked and fried patties | Flame-grilled patties |
| Best-known veg items | McAloo Tikki, McVeggie, McSpicy Paneer, Veg Maharaja Mac | Veg Whopper, Crispy Veg and other veg burgers |
| Best-known chicken items | McChicken, McSpicy Chicken, Chicken Maharaja Mac, Chicken Kebab Burger | Chicken Whopper, Crispy Chicken and other chicken burgers |
| Fries | Classic fries, piri piri fries | Classic fries, peri-peri fries |
| Coffee | McCafé at many stores | BK Café at most stores |
| Desserts | McFlurry, soft serve, sundaes | Sundaes, shakes, soft serve |

Menus change often, and the West & South and North & East McDonald's menus are not identical, so check the app for your city before you go.

### Vegetarian options

**McDonald's has the edge for vegetarians.** The McAloo Tikki was created for India and is still one of its best-known items, and the veg range covers spicy, mild, paneer and double-decker options. McDonald's also keeps vegetarian and non-vegetarian preparation separate, which matters to many Indian families.

**Burger King's veg menu is smaller but bolder**: the Veg Whopper is a big burger built the Whopper way, and its limited-time ranges, such as the Korean range launched in 2025, keep adding new flavours.

### Chicken options

For chicken, it's closer. **McDonald's McSpicy Chicken** and **Chicken Maharaja Mac** are popular for spice and size, while **Burger King's Chicken Whopper** gives you the smoky, grilled taste that is BK's selling point. If you like a juicier, char-grilled flavour, Burger King usually wins. If you prefer crispy, spicy fried chicken, McDonald's is the safer pick.

## Value: which is cheaper in India?

Both chains now compete hard on price, and both push value combos rather than a fixed "dollar menu".

- **McDonald's** runs value ranges such as **McSaver** combos and everyday value meals, mainly built around the McAloo Tikki, McVeggie, McChicken and Chicken Kebab burgers. Offers differ between West & South and North & East India.
- **Burger King** relies on **combo meals, app-only deals and coupons**, and has said in its results that value offerings are a focus.

Prices change with city, store type (mall, highway, airport) and platform. Food-delivery apps often show higher prices than the counter. For the real cost, compare the same combo on each chain's own app on the day you order.

**Verdict on value:** both have low-priced entry burgers, and McDonald's McAloo Tikki is the best-known budget option in Indian fast food. Burger King's app deals can get you a bigger burger for a similar spend.

## Experience and availability

- **Reach:** McDonald's is in most big cities, and Burger King has grown to more than 600 restaurants. In smaller towns, availability depends on the city, so check each app's store finder.
- **Ordering:** both offer self-ordering kiosks in many stores, their own apps, delivery through Swiggy and Zomato and, at some stores, their own delivery service.
- **Families:** McDonald's has Happy Meals and a long-standing family image, which makes it the usual pick for children.
- **Hangouts:** both chains have added café counters (McCafé and BK Café), which makes them a common choice for students and quick meetings.

## Which is healthier?

Neither is a health-food choice. Most burgers, fries and shakes at both chains are high in calories, fat, salt or sugar. If you eat there:

- Choose a single burger instead of a double, and skip the cheese or extra sauce.
- Swap the fizzy drink for water or unsweetened coffee.
- Share fries or pick the smaller size.
- Use the calorie and nutrition information both chains show on their menus and apps to compare items.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do McDonald's and Burger King serve beef in India?

No. McDonald's in India serves no beef or pork, and Burger King has kept beef off its Indian menu since it opened in the country in 2014. Their burgers in India are mostly made with chicken or vegetarian patties such as potato and paneer.

### Is the Big Mac available in India?

No. Because the Big Mac is a beef burger, McDonald's sells the Maharaja Mac in India instead, with chicken and vegetarian versions built in a similar double-decker style. Menus also include many India-only items, so dishes you see in American reviews may not be available here.

### Why do Burger King burgers taste different from McDonald's burgers?

The main difference is how the patties are cooked. Burger King flame-grills its patties, which gives them a smoky, charred flavour, while McDonald's cooks its patties on a flat griddle or fries them for a softer or crispier, more uniform taste. Bun size, sauces and toppings also differ between the two chains.

### Which is healthier, McDonald's or Burger King?

Neither chain is a healthy everyday choice, as most burgers, fries and shakes at both are high in calories, fat, salt or sugar. If you eat there, smaller portions, fewer extras and water instead of fizzy drinks are lighter options, and both chains show calorie and nutrition information so you can compare specific items.
