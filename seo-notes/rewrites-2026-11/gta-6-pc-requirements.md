### How the release date moved

GTA 6 was first announced with its debut trailer in December 2023 for a **2025** release. In May 2025 Rockstar delayed it to 26 May 2026, and in November 2025 it moved again to **19 November 2026**. That is why older articles (including the earlier version of this one) still say "Fall 2025". Take-Two has said publicly since then that it is confident in the November 2026 date.

## When could GTA 6 come to PC?

Nobody outside Rockstar knows, and Rockstar has not said. What we do have is two kinds of evidence.

**What Take-Two has said.** Take-Two's CEO, Strauss Zelnick, has explained in interviews that the company does not always launch on every platform at the same time and that Rockstar has traditionally started on console. In 2026 he also said PC is becoming more and more important to the company. None of these remarks is an announcement or a date. Reports of specific PC launch windows have appeared in the gaming press, but Rockstar has not confirmed any of them.

**What Rockstar has done before.** Rockstar's big open-world games have usually reached PC months or years after console:

| Game | Console release | PC release | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTA IV | April 2008 | December 2008 | About 7 months |
| GTA V | September 2013 | April 2015 | About 19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | October 2018 | November 2019 | About 1 year |

Based only on that pattern, a PC version would most likely arrive **some time after the console launch, probably not before 2027**. Treat that as an educated guess, not a date. Wait for an announcement on Rockstar's Newswire before you plan a purchase around it.

## What "speculative specs" actually mean

Many sites publish tables with exact CPUs, GPUs and a storage size for GTA 6 on PC. They are built in one of three ways:

- **Copying another game's requirements** and bumping them up a generation.
- **Guessing from the console hardware.** The PS5 and Xbox Series X use 8-core AMD CPUs and RDNA 2 graphics, so writers estimate a "PC equivalent".
- **Pure invention** to rank for the search term.

None of these is a requirement. Real PC requirements depend on how Rockstar ports and optimises the game, which upscalers it supports and how big the install is, and those decisions are made late in development. Figures such as "150GB storage" or "GTX 1080 Ti minimum" that circulated earlier have no official source, so we have removed them from this guide.

## A useful reference point: Rockstar's current PC requirements

If you want something real to compare your PC against, look at Rockstar's most recent PC release of its own engine. In March 2025 Rockstar released **GTA V Enhanced** on PC, with ray tracing and modern upscaling. Its official requirements, from Rockstar Support, are:

| | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| OS | Windows 10 (build 1909 or later) | Windows 11 |
| CPU | Intel Core i7-4770 or AMD FX-9590 | Intel Core i5-9600K or AMD Ryzen 5 3600 |
| RAM | 8 GB | 16 GB (dual channel) |
| GPU | GTX 1630 or RX 6400 (4 GB VRAM) | RTX 3060 or RX 6600 XT (8 GB VRAM) |
| Storage | 105 GB, SSD required | 105 GB DirectStorage-compatible drive |

GTA 6 is a much newer and larger game built for current-generation consoles only, so its eventual PC requirements are very likely to be **higher** than these. Two trends from GTA V Enhanced are worth noting, though: Rockstar now **requires an SSD**, and it recommends **8 GB of video memory** even for a decade-old game.

## Should you upgrade your PC now for GTA 6?

For most people, no. Without official requirements you could spend money on the wrong part, and graphics card and SSD prices in India change a lot over a year. A better plan:

1. **Wait for the PC announcement.** Rockstar usually publishes requirements before a PC launch.
2. **If you are upgrading anyway**, build a balanced system rather than chasing one "GTA 6" part: a modern 6- to 8-core CPU, 16 GB of RAM (32 GB if you also stream or edit), and a graphics card with at least 8 GB, ideally 12 GB or more, of video memory.
3. **Get an NVMe SSD with plenty of free space.** Large open-world games load and stream textures from storage, and recent Rockstar PC releases require an SSD.
4. **Prefer a card that supports modern upscaling** (NVIDIA DLSS, AMD FSR or Intel XeSS). Rockstar added DLSS and FSR to GTA V Enhanced, so support in a future GTA 6 port is likely but not confirmed.
5. **Check your power supply and cooling** before buying a bigger graphics card.

## Want to play GTA 6 at launch?

The only way to play GTA 6 on 19 November 2026 is on a **PS5 or Xbox Series X|S**. Rockstar has not announced versions for PS4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch 2 or mobile. Cloud-gaming routes on PC depend on the game being offered on those services, which has not been announced either.

## The bottom line

GTA 6 has no PC version or official PC requirements yet. Rockstar's history points to a PC release after the console launch, most likely not before 2027, but that is a pattern, not a promise. Until Rockstar posts real specs, treat every requirements table as a guess, keep your current PC if it serves you well, and use the GTA V Enhanced requirements as a minimum baseline rather than a target.
