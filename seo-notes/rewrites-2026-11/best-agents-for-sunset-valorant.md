Sunset is Valorant's Los Angeles map: a two-site layout where almost every round is decided by who controls **Mid**. The best agents for Sunset are the ones that win Mid, watch flanks and take a site quickly: a strong smoker such as **Omen, Clove or Brimstone**, an information initiator such as **Sova, Fade or Gekko**, an entry duelist such as **Raze or Neon**, and a sentinel such as **Cypher or Killjoy**.

**Quick answer:** a reliable ranked comp for Sunset is **Omen, Sova, Raze, Cypher and Gekko**. If you prefer easy, forgiving picks, swap Omen for **Brimstone** and Sova for **Fade**. And yes, Sunset is in the competitive pool right now: it returned with patch 13.00 in June 2026 and is still in rotation after patch 13.09 (27 October 2026).

## Is Sunset in the Valorant map pool right now?

Yes. Sunset left Competitive in January 2026 when patch 12.00 brought back a reworked Breeze in its place, then returned with **patch 13.00 on 23 June 2026** alongside the new map Summit. After **patch 13.08 (13 October 2026)**, which swapped Ascent and Split out for Bind and Corrode, the competitive pool is **Abyss, Bind, Corrode, Haven, Lotus, Summit and Sunset**. Patch 13.09 added the new duelist Hush but made no map pool changes.

Riot rotates maps every few acts, so check the latest [official Valorant patch notes](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/tags/patch-notes) before a big ranked push.

## Sunset map overview and callouts

| Area | Main callouts | What matters |
|---|---|---|
| A site (smaller) | A Lobby, A Main, A Elbow, A Link, A Alley | Attackers fight through A Main and Elbow; defenders rotate in through A Link and A Alley |
| Mid | Mid Courtyard, Mid Top, Mid Tiles | Connects to both sites; the team that owns Mid controls the round |
| B site (larger) | B Lobby, B Main, B Market, B Boba | B Market links to Mid Courtyard and B Boba links B to defender spawn, so B has several angles to clear |

Two things make Sunset distinct. First, Mid leads into **both** sites, so a team that takes Mid can split either way or cut rotations. Second, A is a long way from Mid Courtyard for defenders, so a fast A take with a good post-plant is hard to retake.

## Best agents for Sunset by role

| Role | Best picks | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Controller | Omen, Clove, Brimstone | One-way smokes and teleports for Mid; Brimstone's three quick smokes for fast executes |
| Initiator | Sova, Fade, Gekko | Clear B Market, Mid Courtyard and A Elbow without peeking |
| Duelist | Raze, Neon, Jett | Fast entry through tight site entrances |
| Sentinel | Cypher, Killjoy, Chamber | Watch Mid and the long flank routes |

At Champions 2026, **Omen** was the most-picked agent in the tournament overall, and pro teams on Sunset usually play at least one controller who can smoke Mid and a site at the same time. In ranked, tracker data for the current act shows **Clove and Sova** among the most-picked agents on Sunset, with **Cypher** among the agents with the best win rates.

### Omen, Clove or Brimstone (Controller)

- **Omen:** Dark Cover smokes can be placed far across the map, so one Omen can smoke Mid Top and a site entrance in the same round. Shrouded Step and Paranoia help you take Mid Courtyard or punish defenders who over-rotate.
- **Clove:** smokes even after death with Ruse, which suits Sunset's busy Mid fights. Note that patch 13.09 cut her Pick-me-up speed boost from 15% to 10%.
- **Brimstone:** Sky Smoke drops three smokes almost instantly, ideal for a fast A or B execute. Stim Beacon gives the team a speed and fire-rate boost for the push, Incendiary denies the plant or defuse, and Orbital Strike clears a default plant spot or stops a defuse.

### Sova, Fade or Gekko (Initiator)

- **Sova:** Recon Bolt and Owl Drone clear B Market and Mid Courtyard safely. Hunter's Fury is strong in post-plant on both sites.
- **Fade:** Haunt and Prowlers clear corners on B site and A Elbow; Seize holds an enemy in place for a duelist to finish.
- **Gekko:** Wingman can plant or defuse the Spike for you, and Dizzy blinds enemies holding Mid Top or B Market. **Mosh Pit** is a thrown area-damage ability: it spreads a pool that explodes after a short delay, so use it to flush a corner, deny a plant or stop a defuse. It does not block movement or rotations. Thrash detains defenders hiding in tight corners.

### Raze or Neon (Duelist)

- **Raze:** Blast Pack gives fast entry onto A from Elbow or into B Main. Boom Bot scouts B Market or Mid before you push, Paint Shells clears tight corners or stops a defuse, and Showstopper wins crowded fights on either site.
- **Neon:** High Gear lets her sprint across Mid or into B before defenders react, and Fast Lane walls cut off two angles at once on entry.

### Cypher or Killjoy (Sentinel)

- **Cypher:** Trapwires catch lurkers through A Link, B Main or Mid. Spycam gives early information on Mid or a site, **Cyber Cage** creates a short-lived cage that blocks vision, useful to slow a push or cover a retake, and Neural Theft reveals the enemy team's positions after a kill.
- **Killjoy:** Turret and Alarmbot hold a site alone, while Nanoswarms deny the plant. Her Lockdown ultimate is strong for retaking B.

## Best team comps for Sunset

| Comp | Agents | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Balanced ranked comp | Omen, Sova, Raze, Cypher, Gekko | Mid control with good information and flank watch |
| Fast execute comp | Brimstone, Fade, Raze, Killjoy, Gekko | Quick A or B hits with a lot of utility at once |
| Double-controller comp | Omen, Viper, Fade, Neon, Cypher | Organised teams who want to split the map with walls and smokes |

**Deadlock** is a reasonable sentinel alternative: Barrier Mesh blocks a chokepoint like B Main, GravNet pulls enemies down in tight spaces, Sonic Sensor alerts you to pushes through Mid, and Annihilation can catch a player in a narrow corridor. Avoid running two sentinels in solo queue unless your team has a strong entry duelist, because it slows down attacks.

## Attack tips for Sunset

1. **Take Mid Courtyard first.** Smoke Mid Top, use Recon Bolt or Gekko's Dizzy, and push Mid with two players. From there you can hit A through A Link or B through B Market.
2. **A execute:** smoke A Link and A Alley to block defender rotations, clear A Elbow with a flash, and plant early. Because A is far from Mid Courtyard for defenders, a quick plant with a crossfire is hard to break.
3. **B execute:** smoke B Boba and the defender-side angle, clear B Market with utility, and send Raze or Neon in first. Save a Mosh Pit, Paint Shells or Hunter's Fury to stop the defuse.
4. **Watch your own flank.** Leave a Cypher trapwire or Killjoy Alarmbot on the route you left behind.

## Defence tips for Sunset

1. **Contest Mid early,** but not with one player. Use a Spycam, Sonic Sensor or Recon Bolt rather than peeking alone.
2. **Play for retakes on B** if you are outnumbered; it has more angles to clear for attackers too.
3. **Stack utility on A Main and Elbow.** Molotovs, Nanoswarms and Paint Shells slow the attackers' entry and buy time for rotations through A Link.
4. **Save ultimates** like Lockdown, Showstopper or Orbital Strike for retakes and eco rounds.

## Alternative agents for Sunset

- **Viper:** strong as a second controller for organised teams; Toxic Screen can split Mid.
- **Chamber:** good for Operator players holding Mid or A Main, with Rendezvous for safe escapes.
- **Jett:** Operator angles in Mid and fast entry with Tailwind.
- **Hush:** the newest duelist, added in patch 13.09. Her telekinesis kit is too new for proven Sunset setups, so learn her on maps you already know first.

## Final thoughts

Sunset is all about Mid. Pick a controller who can smoke it, an initiator who can clear B Market and Mid Courtyard, a duelist who can enter quickly and a sentinel who watches the flank. With Sunset back in the competitive pool, a simple comp like Omen, Sova, Raze, Cypher and Gekko will win you more rounds than a complicated plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is the Sunset map set in Valorant?

Sunset is set in Los Angeles and is a two-site map, with bomb sites A and B connected by a busy Mid area. Its tight site entrances and flank routes through Mid reward teams that control the centre of the map and watch their flanks.

### Why is Mid control so important on Sunset?

Mid connects both sites, so the team that holds it can rotate faster, cut off enemy rotations and threaten either A or B. On attack, taking Mid lets you split onto a site; on defence, losing it opens up your flanks, which is why smokes, traps and sensors are often used there.

### Which agents are easiest for beginners on Sunset?

Brimstone, Cypher and Killjoy are good choices for newer players, because their smokes and traps are simple to place and give steady value every round. Gekko is also forgiving, since Wingman can plant or defuse the Spike and some of his creatures can be picked up and reused.

### Is Sunset in the Valorant competitive map pool right now?

Yes. Sunset returned to Competitive with patch 13.00 in June 2026 and is in the current pool after patch 13.09, alongside Abyss, Bind, Corrode, Haven, Lotus and Summit. Riot rotates maps every few acts, so check the latest patch notes before practising Sunset-specific strategies.
