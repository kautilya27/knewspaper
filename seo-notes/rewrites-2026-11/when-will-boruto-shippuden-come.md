"Boruto Shippuden" is what many fans call the time-skip part of Boruto's story, the same way *Naruto Shippuden* followed *Naruto*. The good news is that this part already exists in the manga. The question is when the anime will catch up.

**Quick answer:** there is no anime called "Boruto Shippuden". The time-skip story is the manga **Boruto: Two Blue Vortex**, running monthly since August 2023. The anime ended "Part I" in March 2023, and Studio Pierrot's official site said then that **Part II had been green-lit**. As of 5 November 2026, though, **no release date, trailer or premiere window** has been announced for the *Two Blue Vortex* anime.

## What "Boruto Shippuden" really means

*Naruto* split its story into two parts: the original series and *Naruto Shippuden*, which picked up after a time skip with older characters and higher stakes. Fans expected *Boruto* to do the same, and that is exactly what happened in the manga.

- **Part I**, *Boruto: Naruto Next Generations*, ran as a manga from 2016 and as an anime from 2017 to 2023.
- **Part II**, *Boruto: Two Blue Vortex*, began in V Jump on **21 August 2023**, after a short break. It is written by Naruto creator **Masashi Kishimoto** and drawn by **Mikio Ikemoto**.

So if you want the "Shippuden" version of Boruto today, you can read it. There is just no anime of it yet.

## Is the Boruto anime coming back?

Officially, yes, but with no date.

When the anime's Part I ended, the official *Boruto* anime website announced that episode 293, on **26 March 2023**, would be the last episode of Part I and that production of **Part II had been green-lit**. Since then:

- No premiere date, season or trailer for a *Two Blue Vortex* anime has been released.
- *Boruto*'s **10th-anniversary** celebrations in 2026 brought recap and promotional videos, including "Boruto: Road to Two Blue Vortex", but no anime date.
- At **New York Comic Con on 10 October 2026**, the Naruto panel announced a new Kishimoto manga, *Naruto: Shadow Rising*, for 2027 and a four-part *Naruto* crossover limited series. There was **no new Boruto anime announcement**.

Some reports quote Studio Pierrot's president as saying future *Boruto* anime would be made in seasons rather than as a nonstop weekly show. Treat that as a direction, not a schedule: Pierrot has not dated anything.

## When could it realistically come out?

Nobody outside the studio knows, and we will not guess a date. A few things are worth knowing, though:

- **There is enough story.** With 38 chapters out by October 2026, and each chapter long, *Two Blue Vortex* already has more than enough material for a season or two.
- **Announcements usually come first.** Big anime returns are normally revealed with a key visual and trailer months before they air, often at events such as Jump Festa in December or the publisher's own livestreams. Until that happens, any "release date" you see online is a rumour.
- **A seasonal format takes longer to prepare** than a weekly show that mixes in filler.

If and when an official date arrives, it will appear on the official Naruto and Boruto channels first.

## What happens in Two Blue Vortex? (spoilers)

*Spoiler warning: this section covers the opening of Two Blue Vortex and the end of Part I.*

At the end of Part I, the god-like power of Eida's "Omnipotence" rewrites everyone's memories. Most of the world now believes that **Kawaki is Naruto's son** and that **Boruto attacked Naruto**. Naruto and Hinata are sealed away, and Boruto flees the Hidden Leaf with Sasuke.

*Two Blue Vortex* picks up about three years later. An older Boruto returns, now hunted by his own village, while a new threat, the **Shinju** (beings born from the God Tree), joins the remaining Otsutsuki-linked enemies such as Code. Sarada and a few others remember the truth, and the rivalry between Boruto and Kawaki drives much of the story.

That is the darker, older story fans have been asking to see animated.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a confirmed release date for Boruto Shippuden?

No. As of 5 November 2026, no release date has been announced for the *Boruto: Two Blue Vortex* anime. Part II was green-lit in March 2023, but no date, season or trailer has followed.

### What is Boruto Shippuden called officially?

The time-skip story is officially called *Boruto: Two Blue Vortex*. "Boruto Shippuden" is a fan name borrowed from *Naruto Shippuden*.

### Is the Boruto anime cancelled?

No. Part I ended as planned in March 2023, and the official site said Part II was green-lit. It has simply not been dated yet.

### Where can I read Boruto: Two Blue Vortex?

Officially on the MANGA Plus and Shonen Jump apps, which release new chapters in English around the same time as Japan. A new chapter comes out roughly once a month in V Jump.
