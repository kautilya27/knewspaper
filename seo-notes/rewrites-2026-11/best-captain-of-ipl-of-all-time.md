**Quick answer:** the two most successful captains in IPL history are **MS Dhoni and Rohit Sharma, with five titles each**. Dhoni won his with Chennai Super Kings (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023) and Rohit his with Mumbai Indians (2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020). Rohit won all five finals he led MI in; Dhoni took CSK to ten finals. The newest name on the list is **Rajat Patidar**, who led Royal Challengers Bengaluru to back-to-back titles in **2025 and 2026**.

So who is the best IPL captain of all time? It depends on what you value most: a perfect record in finals, or consistency across nearly two decades. Below are the full list of title-winning captains up to IPL 2026, the case for each of the big names, and today's captains.

## MS Dhoni: the most consistent captain

Dhoni led CSK from the first season in 2008 and made them the IPL's most reliable team. Every one of CSK's ten IPL finals came under his captaincy, and he won five of them, the last in 2023 in his forties.

What made him special:

- **Calm under pressure.** His decisions in tight finishes, from bowling changes to field placings, became a template for T20 captains.
- **Building around roles.** CSK often picked experienced players for specific jobs and backed them for seasons rather than matches.
- **Longevity.** He captained across three decades of the league's life, adapting as T20 batting became far more aggressive.

CSK were suspended for the 2016 and 2017 seasons because team officials were found guilty of betting during IPL 2013; the players were not punished, and Dhoni played for Rising Pune Supergiant in those two years. He handed the CSK captaincy to **Ruturaj Gaikwad** before IPL 2024, stepped back in briefly in 2025 when Gaikwad was injured, and Gaikwad led CSK again in 2026. ## Rohit Sharma: five finals, five titles

Rohit took over as Mumbai Indians captain during the 2013 season and won the title that same year. He went on to win in 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2020, and **never lost an IPL final as captain**, a record no other multiple-title captain can match.

Why many fans rate him the best:

- **Big-match temperament.** MI under Rohit were famous for slow starts to a season and strong finishes, peaking in the playoffs.
- **Bowling management.** He used Jasprit Bumrah and Lasith Malinga at the moments that decided matches.
- **Trust in his players.** He backed young players such as Hardik Pandya and Suryakumar Yadav, who became India regulars.

Rohit's run as captain ended after IPL 2023. **Hardik Pandya has captained Mumbai Indians since the 2024 season**, and Rohit continues to play for MI as a batter.

## The other title-winning captains

### Gautam Gambhir (KKR, 2012 and 2014)

Gambhir turned Kolkata from a struggling side into champions twice in three years, built around spin and a settled core. He later returned as KKR's mentor for their 2024 title and went on to become India's head coach.

### Shreyas Iyer (KKR, 2024)

Iyer led KKR to their third title in 2024. He also took Delhi Capitals to the 2020 final and Punjab Kings to the 2025 final, making him the first captain to reach IPL finals with three different franchises. He captains Punjab Kings.

### Rajat Patidar (RCB, 2025 and 2026)

Patidar became RCB captain before IPL 2025 and ended the franchise's long wait for a first title in his first season, then won again in 2026, beating Gujarat Titans in the final. That makes him only the third captain, after Dhoni (2010 and 2011) and Rohit (2019 and 2020), to win back-to-back IPL titles. Our [RCB playing XI](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/rcb-probable-playing-xi-ipl-2024) covers the squad behind both wins.

### David Warner, Hardik Pandya, Shane Warne and Adam Gilchrist

- **David Warner** led Sunrisers Hyderabad to the 2016 title while scoring more than 800 runs himself that season.
- **Hardik Pandya** won the 2022 title with Gujarat Titans in the team's first season and reached the final again in 2023.
- **Shane Warne** captained (and coached) a low-budget Rajasthan Royals side to the first IPL title in 2008.
- **Adam Gilchrist** led Deccan Chargers from last place in 2008 to the title in 2009.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which captain has won the most IPL titles?

Rohit Sharma and MS Dhoni share the record, with five IPL titles each as captain up to the 2026 season. Rohit won his with Mumbai Indians in 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2020, and Dhoni won his with Chennai Super Kings in 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021 and 2023.

### Which team has won the most IPL titles?

Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings are joint leaders with five titles each up to the 2026 season. Kolkata Knight Riders have three (2012, 2014 and 2024), and Royal Challengers Bengaluru won back-to-back titles in 2025 and 2026 under Rajat Patidar.

### Is Rohit Sharma still the captain of Mumbai Indians?

No. Mumbai Indians handed the captaincy to Hardik Pandya from the 2024 season, ending Rohit Sharma's run as captain that began in 2013. All five of Rohit's IPL titles as captain came with Mumbai Indians between 2013 and 2020, and he still plays for the team as a batter.

### Why was CSK banned from the IPL for two years?

Chennai Super Kings and Rajasthan Royals were suspended for the 2016 and 2017 seasons after a Supreme Court-appointed committee found officials of both franchises guilty of betting during IPL 2013. The players were not punished, and MS Dhoni played for Rising Pune Supergiant during those two seasons before returning to CSK in 2018.
