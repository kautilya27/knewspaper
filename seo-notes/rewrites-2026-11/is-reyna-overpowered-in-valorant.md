**Quick answer:** no, Reyna is not overpowered in Valorant in 2026. She is extremely strong in solo ranked games, especially at low and mid ranks, because every kill lets her heal or escape. But Riot has nerfed her repeatedly to curb exactly that, most recently in **patch 12.02 (February 2026)**, and in coordinated teams and pro play she brings far less than other duelists because her kit does almost nothing for teammates.

Reyna has been one of the most-picked agents since Valorant launched in 2020, and "Reyna is OP" is one of the game's oldest arguments. Below is her current kit (correctly explained, since many guides still get it wrong), what Riot has changed, where she is genuinely strong, and how to beat her.

## Reyna's abilities, explained correctly

Reyna's kit runs on **Soul Orbs**. Most of her abilities need an orb, and orbs only come from fights she wins.

| Ability | Key | Cost | What it actually does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soul Harvest (passive) | - | - | Enemies Reyna kills, or who die within 3 seconds of taking damage from her, leave a Soul Orb that lasts 3 seconds |
| Leer | C | 250 credits, 2 charges | Throws a destructible eye a short distance forward. Enemies who look at it are nearsighted |
| Devour | Q | 200 credits, 2 charges | Consumes a Soul Orb to heal quickly. Healing above 100 HP is now temporary overheal that lasts 10 seconds |
| Dismiss | E | 200 credits, 2 charges | Consumes a Soul Orb to become intangible for a short time and move faster, so she can reposition or escape |
| Empress | X | 7 ultimate points | Increases fire rate, equip and reload speed. Every kill auto-casts Devour without using an orb, and Dismiss also makes her invisible. Lasts until she dies or the round ends |

Three common mistakes in older guides are worth clearing up:

- **Dismiss is not a blind.** It makes Reyna intangible (untargetable and unable to shoot) for a moment so she can slip away or reposition.
- **Leer is not a smoke or a recon tool.** It is a small eye that nearsights anyone looking at it, and enemies can shoot it to destroy it. It does not reveal enemy positions.
- **Soul Orbs do not come only from ability kills.** Any kill drops an orb, and so does an enemy who dies within 3 seconds of Reyna damaging them, even if a teammate finishes them.

## What Riot has changed: Reyna's nerfs

Reyna has been toned down several times, almost always for the same reason: too much dominance in ranked.

| Patch | Main changes |
|---|---|
| 2.03 (2021) | Devour and Dismiss charges cut from 4 to 2 and cost raised; orbs also drop from enemies she recently damaged |
| 3.0 (2021) | Leer cost raised to 250 credits |
| 8.11 (2024) | Devour healing reduced and heal time shortened; Dismiss made faster but shorter (1.5 seconds); Empress no longer has a timer and lasts until death or round end |
| 12.02 (February 2026) | Soul Orbs last 3 seconds (down from 4); Devour overheal now lasts only 10 seconds, except during Empress; Empress costs 7 ultimate points instead of 6 |

In the 12.02 notes Riot said Reyna "has been ever present and dominating pickrates in ranked for a long time" and that it wanted more "proactive requirements" after she gets a kill. In practice, she can no longer take a fight, sit back with overheal and reset; she has to keep moving and taking fights. Balance changes regularly, so check the latest patch notes on the official Valorant site for current numbers.

## Why Reyna feels overpowered

- **She snowballs in solo queue.** Each kill gives her a heal or an escape. In ranked games with poor coordination, a confident Reyna can win several fights in a row.
- **Empress rewards momentum.** Once her ultimate is up and she keeps getting kills, she heals automatically and can turn invisible with Dismiss.
- **Escaping a failed peek.** Dismiss lets her take aggressive duels and survive them, which is frustrating for opponents who are not trading.
- **Low and mid ranks rarely punish her.** Players in these ranks often fight alone and don't trade kills, which is exactly the situation she is built for.

## Why Reyna isn't overpowered

- **No kills, no kit.** Apart from her ultimate, Leer is her only ability that works without an orb. If she loses her first duel, she is a duelist with one small nearsight and nothing else.
- **Very little team utility.** She can't smoke, flash for teammates, gather information or create space for the team the way Jett, Raze or Neon can. Organised teams usually prefer agents who help everyone.
- **Weaker in pro play.** Coordinated teams trade kills instantly and use utility before peeking, which denies her the isolated fights she needs.
- **The nerfs have bitten.** Shorter orbs, temporary overheal and a more expensive ultimate all make her reset and snowball less easily than before.

## The verdict: strong, not broken

Reyna is a **high-ceiling, low-utility** agent. Her power is real, but it depends almost entirely on the player's aim and decision-making. In ranked lobbies where nobody trades, she can look unbeatable; against a team that plays together, she is one of the easier duelists to shut down. Riot clearly sees her ranked pick rate as a problem and keeps trimming her, so "overpowered" is less true in 2026 than it was a few years ago.

## How to play Reyna well

- Take fights where a teammate can help you get the first damage, since an assist within 3 seconds still drops an orb.
- Use Leer before peeking a tight angle, not after you are already in the fight.
- Decide quickly after a kill: Devour if you can hold the position, Dismiss if you need to reposition. The orb only lasts 3 seconds.
- Save Empress for rounds you can keep taking fights in, such as retakes or full-buy attacks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Reyna a good agent for beginners in Valorant?

Reyna is easy to understand, which makes her popular with new players, but she is only as strong as your aim. Her abilities depend on getting kills and she offers little utility to help teammates, so beginners who are still learning to win gunfights may find her frustrating and could learn more from agents with team utility.

### Why do so many players pick Reyna in ranked games?

Reyna rewards individual skill. Each kill lets her heal or escape, and her ultimate lets a confident player chain fights together, so a good aimer can carry a round almost alone. That self-sufficiency appeals in ranked matches with strangers, where teamwork is not guaranteed.

### How do you counter Reyna in Valorant?

Avoid giving her isolated one-on-one fights. Peek together so any kill she gets can be traded straight away, shoot her Leer eye instead of looking at it, use flashes and other utility before engaging, and pressure her before her ultimate is ready. A Reyna who cannot get kills has very few tools left to influence the game.

### Has Reyna been nerfed in Valorant?

Yes, several times. The biggest changes came in patch 2.03, which halved her Devour and Dismiss charges, patch 8.11, which reduced her healing and reworked Dismiss and Empress, and patch 12.02 in February 2026, which made her overheal temporary, shortened Soul Orbs and raised her ultimate cost to 7 points.
