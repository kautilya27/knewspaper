**Quick answer:** There is no lifetime toll pass in India. A ₹30,000 pass for 15 years of highway travel was reported in early 2025 as an idea the government was considering, but it was never launched. What NHAI actually launched, on **15 August 2025**, is the **FASTag Annual Pass**. It costs **₹3,075 for 2026-27** (₹3,000 in its first year) and covers **one year or 200 toll plaza crossings, whichever comes first**, for private cars, jeeps and vans at national highway and national expressway toll plazas.

So if you were waiting to buy a "lifetime" pass, the real choice today is between the FASTag Annual Pass, a pass for one toll plaza, or simply paying tolls from your FASTag balance as usual. This guide explains each one, who can use it, where it works and how to work out whether it saves you money. Figures were checked on 5 November 2026.

## Lifetime toll pass vs annual pass: what is true

| Pass | Status (November 2026) | Price | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Lifetime" pass (15 years) | Only a proposal reported in early 2025. Never launched | No official price | Nothing; you cannot buy it |
| FASTag Annual Pass | Live since 15 August 2025 | ₹3,075 for 2026-27 | 1 year or 200 crossings at NH and NE toll plazas |
| Local Pass (for nearby residents) | Live from 1 November 2026 | ₹1,500 or ₹3,000 a year | One chosen toll plaza, for people living within 20 km |
| Monthly pass | Still available | ₹360 a month | One toll plaza |

The lifetime idea came up in media reports in early 2025, alongside the annual pass. When Road Transport and Highways Minister Nitin Gadkari announced the scheme in June 2025, only the annual pass was included. No lifetime or 15-year pass has been notified since then. If you see a website or message selling one, treat it as a scam and check with NHAI before paying anything.

## How the FASTag Annual Pass works

According to NHAI and the FAQs published by IHMCL (NHAI's tolling company), these are the rules:

- **Validity:** one year from activation or 200 trips, whichever comes first. After that, your FASTag goes back to working normally and toll is deducted from your balance. You can buy the pass again for a fresh year or 200 trips.
- **Price:** ₹3,000 when it launched; revised to **₹3,075 from 1 April 2026** for 2026-27. NHAI says the fee may be revised each financial year.
- **Eligible vehicles:** only private, non-commercial cars, jeeps and vans. If the pass is used on a commercial vehicle, it is deactivated without notice.
- **No new FASTag needed:** your existing FASTag works, as long as it is fixed on the windscreen, linked to your vehicle registration number (not just the chassis number) and not blacklisted.
- **Not transferable:** the pass belongs to the vehicle the FASTag is registered to. You cannot move it to another car.
- **Optional:** you do not have to buy it. Without it, your FASTag works exactly as before.

### What counts as one trip

- At a normal (point-based) toll plaza, **each crossing is one trip**. Going and coming back through the same plaza uses **two** trips.
- On closed toll roads, where you pay by distance between an entry and an exit, **one entry plus one exit is one trip**.

### Where the annual pass works and where it does not

The pass works at about **1,150 toll plazas on National Highways and National Expressways** run by NHAI and the road ministry. It does **not** cover toll plazas on state highways or on expressways and roads run by state governments or local bodies. There, your FASTag works as a normal FASTag and toll is deducted from your balance. It also does not pay for parking. So keep some balance in your FASTag even after buying the pass.

## How to buy and activate the annual pass

1. Open the **Rajmargyatra** app or the NHAI website. These are the only two official places to buy it; banks and FASTag issuers do not sell it separately.
2. Enter your vehicle number and FASTag details. The system checks that the vehicle and FASTag are eligible.
3. Pay the fee through the payment options in the app (UPI, cards or net banking). Your FASTag wallet balance cannot be used to pay for the pass.
4. The pass is activated on your existing FASTag, usually **within two hours** of payment. You get SMS alerts on your registered mobile number.

## Does the annual pass save you money?

The maths is simple. At ₹3,075 for up to 200 crossings, the pass works out to about **₹15 per crossing** if you use all 200 trips within the year. Compare that with what you normally pay at the plazas you use (the toll for each plaza is shown at the plaza and in the Rajmargyatra app).

- **Good value:** you cross national highway toll plazas often, for example a weekly drive to your home town or regular trips between two cities, and you will use most of the 200 trips within a year.
- **Poor value:** you drive on highways only a few times a year, or most of your tolls are on state highways or state-run expressways, which the pass does not cover.

Remember that unused trips do not carry over. If the year ends after only 60 crossings, the pass simply expires. A "lifetime" pass would not have solved this either: if you sell the car, change your route or move city, a long upfront payment is money locked into one vehicle.

## If you live near a toll plaza: Local Pass or monthly pass

If you cross the same toll plaza almost every day, a pass for that one plaza may suit you better than the annual pass.

- **Local Pass (from 1 November 2026):** for owners of non-commercial vehicles with a valid FASTag who live **within 20 km** of a national highway toll plaza. It costs **₹1,500 a year** for plazas outside municipal or town limits and **₹3,000 a year** for plazas inside them. It works only at the plaza it is issued for, and it is not available on closed (distance-based) toll roads or where a service road or alternative road is available.
- **Monthly pass:** still available for a single plaza at about **₹360 a month** (around ₹4,320 a year) for one plaza. You can choose either the monthly pass or the new Local Pass.

You can also hold an annual pass alongside a local or monthly pass. At the plaza covered by the local pass, no trips are deducted from your annual pass.

## Other toll rules worth knowing

- **No FASTag costs more.** Since 15 November 2025, a vehicle without a valid, working FASTag pays **double the toll in cash**, or **1.25 times the toll if paid by UPI**.
- **Toll rates change every year**, usually from 1 April, so the per-plaza toll you compare against will also change.
- **Help:** the National Highway helpline is **1033** for toll and highway problems. For wrong FASTag deductions, also raise a complaint with the bank or company that issued your FASTag.

## Should you buy the annual pass?

Buy it if most of your highway driving is on national highways and you will realistically cross 150 to 200 toll plazas in a year, for example if you drive home for festivals like [Diwali](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/why-diwali-is-celebrated) and take regular weekend trips. If you mostly cross one plaza near home, look at the Local Pass instead. And if you were waiting for the lifetime pass, there is nothing to wait for: it has not been launched, and the annual pass is the closest thing available.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a lifetime FASTag or toll pass in India?

No. A 15-year, ₹30,000 lifetime pass was only reported as a proposal in early 2025 and has not been launched as of November 2026. The scheme that exists is the FASTag Annual Pass for one year or 200 trips.

### What is the FASTag Annual Pass price in 2026?

It costs ₹3,075 for the 2026-27 financial year, up from ₹3,000 when it launched on 15 August 2025. You pay it once, through the Rajmargyatra app or the NHAI website.

### Does the annual pass work on state highways and all expressways?

No. It works only at toll plazas on National Highways and National Expressways. On state highways and state-run expressways, toll is deducted from your FASTag balance as usual.

### Can I use one annual pass for two cars?

No. The pass is tied to the FASTag and registration number of one private car, jeep or van and cannot be transferred. Each vehicle needs its own pass.
