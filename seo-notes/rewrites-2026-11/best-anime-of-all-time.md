The best anime of all time, by fan rankings, critics and lasting influence, include **Attack on Titan, Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood, Death Note, Steins;Gate, Demon Slayer, Naruto Shippuden, Cowboy Bebop, Code Geass, JoJo's Bizarre Adventure and Vinland Saga**. Each one either changed what anime could be or keeps topping "greatest ever" lists years after release.

**Quick answer:** if you watch only one, make it **Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood**: a complete story in 64 episodes with one of the most satisfying endings in anime. For a short thriller, pick **Death Note**. For modern blockbuster animation, pick **Demon Slayer**. This list has no major spoilers.

## 1. Attack on Titan

Humanity lives behind giant walls to hide from man-eating Titans, until the walls are broken. What starts as a survival horror story becomes a sweeping war drama about freedom, revenge and the cycle of hatred, with some of the best-planned twists in anime.

- **Why it's legendary:** layered politics, morally grey characters and a story that changes genre as it goes.
- **Animation:** Wit Studio made the first three seasons and MAPPA made the Final Season; both delivered some of the most famous action scenes in the medium.
- **Status:** complete. The final episodes, *The Final Chapters*, aired in 2023 and follow the manga's ending.

## 2. Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

Brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric pay a terrible price for trying to bring their mother back with alchemy, and set out to find the Philosopher's Stone to restore their bodies. Along the way they uncover a conspiracy at the heart of their country.

- **Why it's timeless:** strong rules for its magic system, a huge cast where everyone matters, and themes of sacrifice and ambition.
- **The ending:** widely regarded as one of the most satisfying conclusions in anime. It has long been at or near the top of fan-rated lists such as MyAnimeList.
- **Note:** *Brotherhood* (2009) follows the manga closely. The earlier *Fullmetal Alchemist* (2003) tells a different story after its first half.

## 3. Death Note

Student Light Yagami finds a notebook that kills anyone whose name is written in it, and decides to "cleanse" the world of criminals. The genius detective L sets out to catch him. Their battle of wits is one of anime's great rivalries.

- **Why it's unforgettable:** constant tension, clever plotting and big questions about justice and power.
- **Length:** 37 episodes, which makes it one of the best gateway anime for newcomers.

## 4. Steins;Gate

A self-proclaimed "mad scientist" and his friends accidentally invent a way to send messages to the past, and every change has consequences. The slow first few episodes build into one of the tightest time-travel stories in fiction.

- **Why it's brilliant:** time paradoxes that are complex but easy to follow, and a cast you grow to love.
- **Tip:** push through the first five or six episodes; the payoff is worth it. The follow-up *Steins;Gate 0* explores an alternate timeline.

## 5. Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

Tanjiro Kamado joins the Demon Slayer Corps to turn his sister Nezuko, now a demon, back into a human. The story is simple and heartfelt; the animation by ufotable is what made it a global sensation.

- **Why it's a modern classic:** breathtaking fight scenes, especially the water and flame effects, and an emotional focus on family.
- **Box office:** *Mugen Train* (2020) became the highest-grossing anime film worldwide and remains the top-grossing film ever in Japan. In 2025, *Infinity Castle* Part 1 overtook it to become the highest-grossing anime film worldwide, finishing second to *Mugen Train* in Japan.
- **Status:** the TV series ran from 2019 to 2024, and the final Infinity Castle arc is being told as a trilogy of films. See our guide to the [Demon Slayer Infinity Castle movie release dates](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/will-there-be-a-season-or-a-movie-for-demon-slayer).

## 6. Naruto Shippuden

Naruto Uzumaki goes from village outcast to hero in one of the most influential shonen stories ever made. *Shippuden* continues from the original *Naruto* and runs for 500 episodes.

- **Why it's iconic:** the Fourth Great Ninja War, the Naruto and Sasuke rivalry, and character backstories such as Itachi's and Kakashi's.
- **Tip:** watch the original *Naruto* first, and consider skipping filler episodes, which are not based on the manga.
- **Legacy:** the sequel *Boruto* continues the story with Naruto's son.

## 7. Cowboy Bebop

A crew of broke bounty hunters drift through the solar system in 2071. Mostly episodic, with a melancholy story about the past running underneath, it is one of the most stylish shows ever made.

- **Why it's timeless:** a mix of noir, western, comedy and philosophy in 26 episodes.
- **Soundtrack:** Yoko Kanno's jazz-and-blues score, performed by her band the Seatbelts, is legendary.
- **Influence:** it helped introduce a generation of viewers outside Japan to anime aimed at adults.

## 8. Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion

Exiled prince Lelouch gains a power called Geass that lets him command anyone, and uses it to lead a rebellion against the empire that destroyed his family.

- **Why it's genius:** chess-like strategy, giant robot (Knightmare Frame) battles and plot twists in almost every episode.
- **The ending:** often named among the best finales in anime.

## 9. JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

Each part follows a different member of the Joestar family across generations, from Victorian England to Egypt, Japan, Italy and the United States. From Part 3 onwards, characters fight with **Stands**, personal spirit powers with strange abilities.

- **Why it's unique:** wildly creative fights that are more about clever tricks than raw power, plus a bold, instantly recognisable art style and endlessly meme-able poses.
- **Famous Stands:** Jotaro's **Star Platinum** (Part 3, *Stardust Crusaders*) and Giorno's **Gold Experience** (Part 5, *Golden Wind*, set in Italy).
- **Status:** David Production has adapted Parts 1 to 6, and *Steel Ball Run* (Part 7) began streaming on Netflix in 2026.

## 10. Vinland Saga

In 11th-century northern Europe, young Thorfinn joins the mercenary band of the man who killed his father, hoping to duel him. The first season is a brutal revenge story; the second becomes a quiet, powerful story about what it means to live without violence. - **Why it's a masterpiece:** mature themes of war, slavery and pacifism, and one of the best character arcs in anime.
- **Studios:** Wit Studio made season 1 (2019) and MAPPA made season 2 (2023).
- **Status:** Makoto Yukimura's manga ended in 2025. As of early 2026, a third anime season had not been officially announced, so check official channels for news.

## Honourable mentions

- **Hunter x Hunter (2011):** a smart, dark shonen with a celebrated Chimera Ant arc.
- **One Piece:** a huge, long-running pirate adventure that is still ongoing and still drawing new fans.
- **Monster:** a slow-burn psychological thriller about a doctor hunting a killer he once saved.
- **Frieren: Beyond Journey's End:** a gentle fantasy about memory and time that quickly became a fan favourite.
- **Your Lie in April** and **Clannad: After Story:** for viewers who want a good cry.

## How to choose your first one

- **Short on time?** Death Note, Cowboy Bebop or Steins;Gate.
- **Want an epic with a proper ending?** Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood or Attack on Titan.
- **Want action and spectacle?** Demon Slayer or JoJo's Bizarre Adventure.
- **Want something mature and thoughtful?** Vinland Saga or Monster.

## Final thoughts

There is no single "best anime ever", but these ten have stood the test of time because they combine great storytelling, memorable characters and real influence on everything that came after. Pick one that matches your mood, and you will understand why anime has fans everywhere.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which anime should a beginner watch first?

Short, self-contained series such as Death Note and Cowboy Bebop are good first picks, because they tell a complete story without hundreds of episodes. Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood is another popular starting point if you want a longer series with a satisfying, complete ending.

### Has the Attack on Titan anime finished?

Yes. The Attack on Titan anime has concluded, with its final episodes, released as The Final Chapters, finishing the story in 2023 in line with the manga's ending. You can now watch the whole series from start to finish without waiting for new episodes.

### Can I watch Naruto Shippuden without watching Naruto?

You can, but it is better to watch the original Naruto first, because Shippuden continues directly from it and assumes you already know the characters and their history. To save time, many fans skip the filler episodes in both series, which are not based on the manga.

### Is anime only for kids?

No. Anime covers every genre and age group, and many popular series, such as Attack on Titan, Death Note and Vinland Saga, deal with violence, war and other mature themes meant for teenagers and adults. Check the age rating before watching a series with children.
