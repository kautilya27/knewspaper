**Quick answer:** There is no single best app for everyone. **Zerodha** is the cheapest of the three for long-term investors, because it charges **₹0 brokerage on delivery trades**, and it has strong tools and education. **Groww** has the simplest app and **no demat AMC**, which suits beginners who invest small amounts. **Upstox** charges a flat **₹20 per order**, has a clean app and suits people who trade more often. All three offer **direct mutual funds with no commission**, and all three now offer **margin trading (MTF)**, including Zerodha.

One small correction to the question first: Upstox, Groww and Zerodha are not stock exchanges. India's stock exchanges are the **NSE** and **BSE**. These three companies are **SEBI-registered stockbrokers**: their apps send your orders to the exchanges, and your shares are held in a demat account with a depository (CDSL for all three). So when people search for the "best stock exchange app", they usually mean the best trading or investing app. Charges below were checked on each broker's official pricing page on 5 November 2026.

### What a simple investment costs on each app

Say you buy shares worth ₹10,000 for delivery and sell them later at the same price (statutory charges left out, since they are the same everywhere):

- **Zerodha:** ₹0 brokerage to buy and sell, plus ₹15.34 DP charge on the sale. **About ₹15.**
- **Groww:** about ₹10 brokerage to buy and ₹10 to sell (0.1%), plus about ₹23.60 DP charge with GST, and GST on the brokerage. **About ₹47.**
- **Upstox:** ₹20 to buy and ₹20 to sell, plus about ₹23.60 DP charge with GST, and GST on the brokerage. **About ₹71.**

For a long-term investor who buys and holds, these differences add up over years. For an active intraday or options trader, all three charge a similar ₹20 per order, and the app's speed, charts and reliability matter more.

## Margin trading facility (MTF): what has changed

Older comparisons (including an earlier version of this article) said Zerodha does not offer margin funding. That is no longer true. **All three brokers now offer MTF**, which lets you buy shares for delivery by paying only part of the price; the broker lends you the rest and charges daily interest until you repay.

- **Zerodha:** 0.04% a day on the funded amount (about ₹40 per lakh per day), plus brokerage of 0.3% or ₹20 per order, whichever is lower, and a pledge charge.
- **Groww:** 14.95% a year on the funded amount, plus 0.1% brokerage per order.
- **Upstox:** ₹20 a day for every ₹40,000 of MTF used, over and above normal brokerage. Its paid Upstox Plus plan has a lower rate on the first ₹1 lakh.

MTF is borrowing. If the share price falls, your losses are larger than your own money would suggest, and the broker can sell your shares if you don't add margin. Beginners should avoid it until they understand the risks.

## Features and apps

### Zerodha

- **Apps:** Kite for trading, Coin for direct mutual funds, and Console for reports and tax statements.
- **Strengths:** zero delivery brokerage, detailed charts, a large free learning site (Varsity), and APIs for people who build their own trading tools.
- **Weak points:** a demat AMC after the first year unless you qualify for a basic account, and several separate apps rather than one.

### Groww

- **App:** one app for stocks, F&O, IPOs, mutual funds and MTF.
- **Strengths:** the simplest interface of the three, no demat AMC, and zero-commission direct mutual funds. Groww has become India's largest broker by active clients, with about 1.34 crore active NSE clients in September 2026.
- **Weak points:** delivery trades are not free, and advanced traders may find its tools more basic than Kite's.

### Upstox

- **App:** the Upstox app for stocks, F&O, IPOs, mutual funds and MTF, with an optional paid Upstox Plus plan.
- **Strengths:** flat ₹20 pricing, a clean app and good charting.
- **Weak points:** brokerage on delivery trades and the highest standard MTF rate of the three. Our [Upstox review](https://www.knewspaper.in/post/why-upstox-could-be-your-perfect-investment-partner) covers it in more detail.

## How to stay safe with any trading app

- Check that the broker is registered with SEBI and the exchanges; their registration numbers are on their websites and on SEBI's website.
- Download apps only from the official app stores and never share your OTP or PIN.
- Your shares are held in your own demat account with CDSL, and you can check them through CDSL's e-statements.
- Be careful with F&O and MTF. SEBI studies have found that most individual F&O traders lose money.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Zerodha, Groww or Upstox a stock exchange?

No. The stock exchanges in India are the NSE and BSE. Zerodha, Groww and Upstox are SEBI-registered stockbrokers whose apps place your orders on those exchanges.

### Which trading app is best for beginners in India?

Groww is usually the easiest for beginners because of its simple app and zero demat AMC. Zerodha is a strong choice if you plan to buy and hold shares, since delivery trades are free.

### Does Zerodha offer margin trading (MTF)?

Yes. Zerodha offers MTF at 0.04% a day on the funded amount, plus brokerage and pledge charges, as listed on its charges page in November 2026.

### Which app has the lowest charges: Zerodha, Groww or Upstox?

For buying and holding shares, Zerodha is the cheapest because delivery brokerage is ₹0. Groww has no demat AMC, which helps small investors. For intraday and options, all three charge about ₹20 per order.
