Can Virat Kohli match Sachin Tendulkar's 100 international centuries? For years it looked possible. Now that Kohli plays only one format, the maths has changed, and the honest answer is less romantic than many fans hope.

**Quick answer:** Virat Kohli has **86 international centuries** as of 5 November 2026 (30 in Tests, 55 in ODIs and 1 in T20Is), so he needs **14 more** to reach 100. He has retired from T20Is (2024) and Tests (2025) and says the **2027 ODI World Cup will be his last World Cup for India**. To get there he would need a hundred every two or three ODIs, better than his best-ever run, so 100 is **very unlikely, though not mathematically impossible**.

Kohli's Test and T20I numbers are now final. His **30th Test hundred** came in Perth in November 2024, and his only **T20I hundred** was 122 not out against Afghanistan at the 2022 Asia Cup.

His ODI tally keeps growing. He broke Sachin's record of 49 ODI hundreds with his **50th** in the 2023 World Cup semi-final at the Wankhede, and since then:

- **51st:** against Pakistan at the 2025 Champions Trophy.
- **52nd and 53rd:** back-to-back hundreds against South Africa in late 2025.
- **54th:** against New Zealand in Indore in January 2026.
- **55th:** 139 not out against West Indies in Thiruvananthapuram on 27 September 2026, in the innings in which he passed **15,000 ODI runs**, only the second man after Sachin to do so.

On 4 November 2026 he made 74 in the first ODI of India's five-match series in New Zealand, so the count stays at 55 ODI hundreds for now.

Today is also Kohli's **38th birthday**; he was born on 5 November 1988.

## Why the target became so hard

The original version of this article, written in 2024, assumed Kohli would keep playing Test cricket, historically a steady source of hundreds. That did not happen.

- **T20Is:** he retired straight after India won the **2024 T20 World Cup** final, in which he was Player of the Match.
- **Tests:** he announced his Test retirement on **12 May 2025**, after 123 Tests, 9,230 runs and 30 hundreds.
- **ODIs:** he is the only format left. In September 2026 he said: "For me, it's the last World Cup I'm going to play for India." The 2027 World Cup is in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia in October and November 2027.

So every remaining hundred has to come in ODIs, and India play far fewer ODIs than they used to. A typical year has a handful of three-match series, plus tournaments.

## The maths of 14 more hundreds

Wisden ran the numbers in December 2025, when Kohli had 84 hundreds. It worked out that India had at most about **45 ODIs** left up to and including the 2027 World Cup, and only if the team went deep in every tournament. Since then, Kohli has added two hundreds but India have used up some of those matches.

Compare what he needs with what he has done:

- **His career rate:** roughly one ODI hundred every five and a half matches.
- **His best stretch (2016 to 2019):** 20 hundreds in 76 ODIs, about one every 3.8 matches, at an average near 81.
- **What 100 now needs:** 14 hundreds in perhaps 30 to 40 matches, or about **one every two to three matches**.

In other words, he would need to bat better than at the absolute peak of his career, at 38 and 39, in a format India play only now and then. At his normal rate, he would end somewhere in the low-to-mid 90s.

## What could still help him

It is not all against him. A few things work in his favour:

- **His form is good.** Four ODI hundreds between late 2025 and September 2026 show he is still scoring heavily.
- **He is the best chaser in ODI history.** Many of his hundreds have come in chases, and India still build their ODI batting around him.
- **Fitness.** Kohli remains one of the fittest players in the side, and playing one format means less wear and tear.
- **No fixed retirement date.** He has said the 2027 World Cup will be his last World Cup, not necessarily his last ODI. Whether he plays on after it is not known as of November 2026.

## So, can he do it?

Probably not, and that is fine. Sachin's 100 came from 664 international matches over 24 years across Tests and ODIs. Kohli chose to stop playing two formats, and that choice costs him the chance at the number.

What he has already done is enough. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does Virat Kohli have a chance to score 100 centuries?

Only a small one. He has 86 and needs 14 more, all in ODIs, before or soon after the 2027 World Cup. That needs a hundred every two or three matches, better than his best-ever form.

### How many centuries does Virat Kohli need to reach 100?

He needs 14 more. As of 5 November 2026 he has 86 international hundreds: 30 in Tests, 55 in ODIs and 1 in T20Is.

### Is Virat Kohli still playing international cricket?

Yes, but only ODIs. He retired from T20Is after the 2024 T20 World Cup and from Tests in May 2025, and has said the 2027 ODI World Cup will be his last World Cup for India.

### Who holds the record for most international centuries?

Sachin Tendulkar, with 100 (51 in Tests and 49 in ODIs). Virat Kohli is second with 86, and he holds the record for most ODI hundreds with 55.
