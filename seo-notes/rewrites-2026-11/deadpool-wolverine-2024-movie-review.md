Ryan Reynolds' foul-mouthed Deadpool and Hugh Jackman's Wolverine finally share the screen in *Deadpool & Wolverine* (2024), Deadpool's first film inside the Marvel Cinematic Universe. It is loud, crude, packed with in-jokes and, surprisingly often, sincere.

**Quick answer:** *Deadpool & Wolverine* is a very funny, very violent buddy comedy that works best if you know the old Fox *X-Men* films. Reynolds and Jackman are terrific together, the cameos are a treat for fans, and the "Bye Bye Bye" opening is one of the best action scenes in recent Marvel films. The story itself is thin and the villain is underused. It carries an **A (adults only)** certificate in India, and you can stream it on **JioHotstar**.

## The plot (spoiler-light)

Six years after *Deadpool 2*, Wade Wilson has hung up the suit. A failed attempt to join the Avengers has left him selling cars, and he is drifting apart from his friends.

Then the **Time Variance Authority (TVA)**, the timeline police from the *Loki* series, picks him up. A TVA official, **Mr. Paradox** (Matthew Macfadyen), tells Wade that his universe is going to wither away, because its "anchor being", the Logan of that world, died (as seen in the 2017 film *Logan*). Paradox offers Wade a place in the main Marvel timeline; Wade wants to save his own world and the people in it instead.

His plan is simple and stupid: find **another Wolverine** from another universe to take the dead one's place. The variant he ends up with (Jackman) is a bitter, hard-drinking Logan who is known as the "worst Wolverine" of them all because of how badly he failed his own X-Men. Before long, the two of them are dumped in **the Void**, a wasteland at the end of time ruled by **Cassandra Nova** (Emma Corrin), Charles Xavier's twin sister, who has powers like his and none of his kindness.

There is no time travel to meet a younger Wolverine, as some early descriptions suggested. The story is about jumping between universes, and Jackman plays a grown, broken Logan who has to decide if he is still worth anything.

## A startling opening

The film opens with Deadpool digging up something he really should not dig up, then using it to fight off a squad of TVA agents while NSYNC's "Bye Bye Bye" plays. It is gory, perfectly timed and very silly, and it tells you in three minutes exactly what kind of film you are watching. If you hate it, you will hate the rest; if you laugh, you are in for a good time.

## Reynolds and Jackman are the whole show

The best thing in the film is the pairing. Reynolds talks nonstop, breaks the fourth wall and jokes about Disney, Fox and Marvel's own troubles. Jackman, who came back to the role after saying goodbye in *Logan*, plays it straight: angry, tired and ashamed. Their fights are brutal (they cannot kill each other, so they keep trying), and their brawl inside a cramped minivan is one of the funniest scenes Marvel has made.

What lifts it above a joke machine is that Jackman's Logan gets a real arc. By the end, the film has something warm to say about second chances and about giving people (and characters) a better send-off than the one they got.

## Cameos galore

The Void is a dumping ground for forgotten characters, and the film uses it to bring back faces from 20-plus years of Fox Marvel films, plus a few surprises. We will not spoil them. Some are big crowd-pleasers; others only land if you remember films that most people have forgotten. Fans of the early-2000s superhero era will get the most out of this.

## Where it falls short

- **The story is thin.** Once the two leads reach the Void, the plot is mostly a series of fights and reunions.
- **Cassandra Nova deserved more.** Emma Corrin is chilling, but the villain gets little screen time to build a real threat.
- **The in-jokes pile up.** If you have not seen the *X-Men*, *Deadpool* and *Logan* films, many gags and cameos will pass you by.
- **The humour is relentless.** Some jokes are brilliant, some are just loud, and the film rarely slows down.

## Our verdict

*Deadpool & Wolverine* is a crowd-pleaser made for fans: two stars clearly having fun, a stream of R-rated jokes, and a surprisingly emotional story about a Wolverine who thinks he has nothing left. It is not deep and not tidy, but it is a lot of fun. Watch it with friends who know their *X-Men*, and keep the kids out of the room.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the plot of Deadpool & Wolverine?

The TVA tells a retired Deadpool that his universe will die because its Wolverine is dead. He recruits a disgraced Wolverine from another universe to take his place, and the two end up fighting Cassandra Nova in the Void.

### Is Deadpool & Wolverine kid-friendly?

No. The CBFC gave it an A (adults only) certificate in India, and it is rated R in the US, for strong, gory violence, constant swearing and crude sexual jokes.

### Do I need to watch other films before Deadpool & Wolverine?

It helps. *Deadpool* (2016), *Deadpool 2* (2018) and *Logan* (2017) set up the two leads, and the *Loki* series explains the TVA. The older *X-Men* films make the cameos land.
