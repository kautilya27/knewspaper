Askeladd is the cleverest man in the first arc of Makoto Yukimura's *Vinland Saga*. He leads a mercenary band, outwits armies and steers a Danish prince towards the throne. So why does he never try to take a crown for himself?

**Quick answer:** Askeladd does not want to be king because his real goal was never power. The son of a Danish chieftain and an enslaved Welsh noblewoman, he hates the Danes and wants one thing: to keep his mother's homeland, **Wales**, safe from invasion. He believes a true king, like the legendary Artorius his mother told him about, should rise to do that, and he decides **Prince Canute** can be that king. Askeladd sees himself as the kingmaker, not the king.

*Spoiler warning: this article discusses Askeladd's backstory and his fate at the end of the manga's War arc, which is also the end of Season 1 of the anime (episode 24).*

## Who is Askeladd? His real background

Some fan write-ups, including an older version of this article, say Askeladd's father was a former king. That is wrong. Here is what the story actually shows:

- **His father was Olaf, a Danish Viking chieftain**, not a king. Askeladd was his illegitimate son and was never even given a proper name by him.
- **His mother was Lydia, a Welsh noblewoman** whom Olaf captured and enslaved. She later lost her health and her mind and was left in the stables.
- **He was born into slavery.** As a child he worked to feed himself and his mother, and he got the nickname "Askeladd", roughly "the one covered in ashes", from the dirt he was always covered in.
- **Lydia claimed descent from Lucius Artorius Castus**, the Roman commander behind the King Arthur legend. She told her son that Artorius was healing in Avalon and would one day return to free the Welsh.

When Askeladd was about 11, he attacked Olaf to protect his mother. Olaf was impressed and freed him. Askeladd then spent about two years pretending to be loyal before killing Olaf and framing a half-brother, which let him inherit his father's men and property. He later took Lydia back to Wales, where she died.

That story explains almost everything about him.

## 1. His goal is Wales, not a throne

Askeladd's secret purpose is to **protect Wales from both the Norse and the English**. Everything else, including his mercenary work and his scheming, is a means to that end.

A crown would not help him. A half-Danish mercenary who seized a throne would have to rule the very Danes he despises and would spend his life fighting to keep it. What Wales needs, in his eyes, is a powerful ruler in England who leaves Wales alone. That is a job for a king he can influence, not for him.

## 2. He hates the Danes, and he is one of them

Askeladd despises Danes and Viking culture, even though he is half Dane and lives as a Viking captain. He identifies with his mother's Romano-Celtic side instead. Becoming a Danish king would mean becoming the thing he hates most: a man like Olaf, ruling by the sword over people he looks down on.

This is also why he is so good at manipulating his own men. He never fully belongs to them, so he can use them without guilt.

## 3. He was waiting for "Artorius", and found Canute

As a boy, Askeladd hoped the legendary Artorius would return and save the Welsh. As an adult, he concluded that anyone could fill that role if they had the strength and the vision.

At first, Canute is a timid, sheltered prince, and Askeladd has little respect for him. After Canute's protector Ragnar dies, Canute changes. He becomes cold, decisive and determined to build a better world with his own hands. Askeladd sees in him the king he had been waiting for, and pledges to serve him. Canute is one of only two people Askeladd truly respects; the other is Thorfinn's father, **Thors**, a warrior who had given up violence.

So the answer to "why doesn't Askeladd become king?" is partly: he thinks someone better has finally appeared.

## 4. He prefers to rule from the shadows

Askeladd's whole life has been about hidden power: pretending loyalty to Olaf, leading men who do not know his real aims, and steering events without taking credit. As a kingmaker he can act freely, take risks and change sides. A king is watched, bound by alliances and tied to one place. For someone as cynical and flexible as Askeladd, that is a cage.

## 5. The one moment he claims to be king

The great irony comes at the end of the War arc. **King Sweyn**, Canute's father, announces an invasion of Wales and privately forces Askeladd to choose: save Wales or save Canute. Askeladd refuses to give up either. He pretends to go mad in front of the court, declares himself **Lucius Artorius Castus**, and kills Sweyn. He is then killed by Canute, which clears Canute's path to power and keeps Wales safe.

The only time Askeladd takes on a royal identity, it is a performance and a sacrifice, not an ambition. He becomes "Artorius" for a few minutes so that Wales and Canute can both survive. His last words to Thorfinn tell him to let go of his hatred and become a true warrior, like Thors.

## What Askeladd is not

It is worth clearing up two more common mix-ups:

- **Vinland is not Askeladd's dream.** The idea of Vinland as a peaceful land across the sea comes from Leif Erikson and later becomes Thorfinn's goal. Askeladd's "promised land" is Wales and the Arthurian legend of Avalon.
- **He is not against kings.** He does not want to tear down kingship; he wants the right king, one who will serve his purpose.

## Why this makes him such a great character

Askeladd is a murderer, a liar and a manipulator, yet many fans rank him among the best characters in anime. The reason is that his ruthlessness serves something human: a slave boy's promise to his broken mother. He mocks everyone's ideals while secretly living for one of his own. ## Frequently asked questions

### Why doesn't Askeladd want to be king in Vinland Saga?

Because his real goal is to protect Wales, his mother's homeland. He hates the Danes, prefers to work from the shadows, and believes Canute is the true king Wales needs, so he chooses to be a kingmaker instead.

### Who were Askeladd's parents?

His father was Olaf, a Danish Viking chieftain, not a king. His mother was Lydia, a Welsh noblewoman enslaved by Olaf, who claimed descent from Lucius Artorius Castus, the figure behind the King Arthur legend.

### Was Askeladd a slave?

Yes. He was born into slavery as the son of an enslaved mother and worked from early childhood. Olaf freed him when he was about 11, and Askeladd later killed Olaf and took over his men.

### How does Askeladd die?

He kills King Sweyn at the royal court to stop the invasion of Wales while pretending to be mad and declaring himself Artorius. Canute then kills him. It happens in episode 24 of the anime's first season.
